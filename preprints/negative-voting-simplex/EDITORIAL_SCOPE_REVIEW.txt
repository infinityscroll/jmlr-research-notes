squared Frobenius norm w. Consequently tr(E)=0, which is required for
E_d = m P diag(diag E) P/(m-2) to leave a zero-diagonal remainder E_s.

2. The Procrustes sum explicitly runs over the m-1 eigenvalues of K restricted

of Gamma_p at fixed masses, defines the normalized edge offsets q_e, and
identifies the whitened covariance perturbation and offset differences.

The renamed analytic-interface environment is referenced consistently.

been replaced with explicit credit to Garcia-Soriano--Miyauchi. The algorithm
is stated as randomized, with slack and fixed-m dependence, and UGC is
attached only to hardness.

SCIENTIFIC SCOPE CONFIRMED AT THE INTERFACE LEVEL

The main theorem has one existential absolute alphabet threshold, all finite
N >= m-1, the entire negative voting interval including its negative endpoint,
and arbitrary measurable simplex-valued single assignments. It has no
prescribed-mass constraint. The manuscript does not claim the remaining small
alphabets, all negative correlations, positive noise, or a two-rule theorem.

The covariance framework retains the derivative factor 1/2. The Hodge
normal-strain bound remains separate from the stronger cycle-refined
covariance/offset inequality. The latter is still an explicit input to the
mass-envelope proof; the manuscript does not falsely describe the entire
proof as independent of the finite-fan cycle machinery.

The mass localization first obtains a positive mass box before invoking the
mass envelope. Its deficit is D = delta + r(w-W), and the exterior comparison
uses J = D/r, including when W>w. The exterior floor is strictly positive.
The two branch domains d<=1/m and d>1/m cover every competitive assignment.

The local comparison retains the positive r||M-B||^2 term and the resulting
exact cancellation with the affine first-chaos term. The moderate-amplitude
cap is not used by itself as an all-amplitude bound; the small-amplitude
argument is separately identified. Uniform analytic constants and their
proof obligations remain outside the formal Lean claim.

The exterior second-chaos and full signed-tail interfaces give exactly
 D[1 - C r L^3 - C r^2 m^(7/4) L^(7/2)].
At the voting endpoint the two error scales tend to zero. The manuscript
uses the exterior floor to pay its absolute truncation error, rather than
claiming that error is a global relative bound near the optimum.

The equality argument uses zero nonnegative routing loss and Gaussian-null
regular-score ties, so it applies to fractional rules. It does not infer
purity or regular boundaries for an arbitrary full-noise minimizer.

The discrete theorem uses low-degree influences with thresholds independent
of voter count. The UGC soundness calculation uses one averaged simplex-valued
rule in both noise slots. The fixed-alphabet MAX-m-CUT consequence is not
presented as a variable-alphabet polynomial-time statement or a deterministic
derandomization.
