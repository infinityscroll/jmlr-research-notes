FINITE WEIGHTED COAREA AND ORDERED L1 COMPARISON

The literal threshold cut {i | g_i <= t} is integrated over real t. Each
ordered crossing contributes max(g_j-g_i,0); this is proved from the actual
Lebesgue integral of its Ico interval indicator. Symmetry of the edge
weights converts the oriented integral to half the ordered absolute sum.
A cut lower bound kappa*min(|S|,|Sc|) implies
  (kappa/m)*sum_ij |g_i-g_j| <= sum_ij w_ij*|g_i-g_j|.
No median, coarea, integrability, or Poincare estimate is assumed. Kappa must
be nonnegative, V finite and nonempty; the L1 implication itself does not
need nonnegative edge weights. Nonnegativity enters the separately audited
Rayleigh/Cauchy argument. All19 compiled owned constants are audited,
including generated/private names; only the standard three axioms occur.
