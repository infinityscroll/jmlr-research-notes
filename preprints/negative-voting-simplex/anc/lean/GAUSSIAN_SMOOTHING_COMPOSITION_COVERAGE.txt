ACTUAL OU COMPOSITION

For every arbitrary raw f in L2(gamma_n) and 0<tau,sigma<1,

  T_tau(T_sigma f) = T_(tau*sigma) f, gamma_n-almost everywhere.

T is the actual finite-product Gaussian averaging integral, including its
sqrt(1-correlation^2) noise coefficient. The proof transfers the already checked
L2 Gaussian semigroup through its actual-integral coeFn identities and Gaussian
AE congruence; it does not assume a desired composition identity for raw inputs.

VARIABLE-EXPONENT NORMAL TRACE

For 0<tau<1, q=1+tau^(-2), and f in L2(gamma_n x gamma_1),

  ||T_tau f(.,a)||_q <= sqrt(C(tau,a)) ||f||_2,

  C(tau,a) = 1/[sqrt(1-tau^2)*sqrt(1+tau^2)]
             * exp(tau^2*a^2/(1+tau^2)).

The actual product-integral endpoint is
actual_product_normalTrace_lq_bound_sharp.

For A>=0, the literal supremum over EVERY real a in [-A,A] belongs to Lq and
has the same bound with a replaced by A. This is proved by
actual_product_normal_sup_lq_memLp_and_bound. The exponent is variable, so the
weighted-facet argument may choose q comparable to log(m), rather than being
restricted to q=32. The exact relation is q=1+tau^(-2).

HIGH-CHAOS COMPOSITION BOUNDARY

Suppose the actual product-Hermite coefficients of f below degree k vanish.
The existing checked GaussianChaosDecay theorem supplies actual L2 contraction
by sigma^k. This module combines it with composition and hypercontractivity:

  ||T_(tau*sigma) f||_(1+tau^(-2)) <= sigma^k ||f||_2.

It also supplies the q=32 specialization under tau^2<=1/31.

The stronger actual normal-supremum endpoint is
actual_gaussian_normal_sup_highChaos_lq_bound. For f in L2(gamma_(n+1)),
0<tau,sigma<1, A>=0, and vanishing coefficients below degree k, define

  S(x) = sup_{a in [-A,A]} |T_(tau*sigma) f(e^{-1}(x,a))|,
  e(v) = ((v(j+1))_{j=0,...,n-1}, v(0)).

The theorem proves S belongs to Lq(gamma_n), q=1+tau^(-2), and

  ||S||_q <= sqrt(C(tau,A)) sigma^k ||f||_2.

The measurable coordinate equivalence e is explicit, preserves the actual
Gaussian product measures, and preserves every appropriate Lp norm. It is not
an unstated identification between Fin(n+1) and a product domain.

WHY THE NORMAL-SLICE COMPOSITION IS VALID

A full-space AE identity alone would not justify equality on every fixed normal
slice. This module first transports the true full-space semigroup identity
through e. Fubini gives almost-every-normal equality on almost every tangent
fibre. Previously proved normal differentiability gives continuity on both
sides; the one-dimensional Gaussian has full support, so the equality extends
to ALL real normal parameters on one common tangential full-measure set. This
is normalTrace_smoothing_comp_ae_all_parameters. The subsequent supremum
comparison uses that simultaneous statement.

MATHEMATICAL SCOPE

No boundedness or Borel-only representative condition is imposed on the input.
The high-chaos hypothesis is vanishing of explicit Hermite coefficients, not an
assumed smoothing norm bound. The companion finite-projection module is the
place that derives this hypothesis for concrete low-mode subtraction.

The module does not establish the new negative-voting global comparison,
weighted Gaussian facet energies, arbitrary spatial orthogonal transport,
or a two-normal geometric estimate. It provides the actual analytic composition
and trace bridge those later estimates require. Classical Gaussian semigroup,
Hermite, and hypercontractive foundations remain attributed to their checked
upstream sources.
