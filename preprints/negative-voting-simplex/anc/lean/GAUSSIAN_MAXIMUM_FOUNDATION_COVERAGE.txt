ACTUAL INDEPENDENT GAUSSIAN MAXIMUM AND QUANTILE-TAIL FOUNDATION

Sources: GaussianMaximum.lean, GaussianTailRatio.lean.
Namespaces: NegativeVotingGaussianMaximum, NegativeVotingGaussianTail.

The maximum is the literal finite maximum under the product of standard
one-dimensional Gaussian measures. The source derives its actual CDF
Phi(t)^m and complementary survival probability, first/second moment
integrability, and finite union/Bonferroni tail bounds.

The sequence uses m=n+2 labels to keep its finite maximum nonempty at every
natural index. upperQuantile n is the actual Gaussian upper 1/(n+2)
quantile, with its CDF identity and divergence to infinity proved from the
existing actual inverse-CDF construction. scaledMaximum n is exactly
b_n*(M_(n+2)-b_n), and its mean is b_n*(mu_(n+2)-b_n).

Using the already-vendored, checked Mulgund Mills inequalities, the source
derives exact Gaussian upper/lower tail ratios, retaining the quadratic
exponent. Consequently the ACTUAL scaled maximum satisfies, for n>0:
  P(Z_n>t) <= exp(-t) for all t>=0;
  P(Z_n<=-t) <= exp(-exp((99/100)*t))
    for 0<=t<=b_n^2/50.
The actual quantile also satisfies b_n^2<=2log(n+2).

These are concrete distribution and moment-integrability statements.
They do not yet prove a mean/quantile gap, bounded scaled second moments,
a Gumbel limit, or the competitive mass-localization conclusion. The
remaining integral/finite-tail estimates are being proved separately.

Dependencies retain their existing attribution and pins. Every compiled
constant in the two owned modules, including private/generated helpers,
is checked by Lean.collectAxioms against the standard-three-only allowlist.
