p0=Gaussian(C)>0, and p1=Gaussian{x:(I+A)x+z belongs to C}. The module proves,
under the single deformation-size condition 4 exp(1)||A||HS<=1,

  L0=1+log(1/p0).

condition. The actual inverse control, positive masses, log derivatives,
conditional moment inequalities, Gronwall integration, and the two-sided
mean-value comparison are all proved in the source closure.

finite probability comparison, with no supplied derivative, ODE, moment,

the actual regular-facet cylindrical cap in the next geometry module.
