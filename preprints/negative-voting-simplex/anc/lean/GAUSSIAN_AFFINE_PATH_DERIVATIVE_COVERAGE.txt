GaussianAffinePathDerivative: actual log-mass rates at every invertible point

The affineMass definition is the literal Gaussian probability of the affine
pullback of a finite cap. For every invertible base map M and shift v, the
module derives differentiability and an explicit logarithmic derivative bound
along M+tA, v+tz. The bound uses exactly the relative velocities M^-1 A and
M^-1 z, with the actual base mass p=affineMass(M,v):
  |deriv_0 log p(t)| <= 4e(1+log(1/p)) ||M^-1 A||HS
      + ||M^-1 z|| (2 sqrt(log(1/p))+1/sqrt(2 pi)).

The normalized base cap, its unit normals and NoCoincident property, adjoint
injectivity, and exact relative-velocity pullback identity are derived. The
base assumptions are concrete: unit original normals, NoCoincident supports,
an actual continuous linear equivalence M, and positive actual base mass.
No differentiability or derivative bound is assumed.

The following norm and positivity modules remove the supplied inverse and
pathwise positivity when specializing to an actual near-identity line.
