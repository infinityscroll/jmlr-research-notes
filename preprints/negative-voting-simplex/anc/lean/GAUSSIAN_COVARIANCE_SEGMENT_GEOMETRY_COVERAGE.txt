GaussianCovarianceSegmentGeometry: genuine covariance factors on a segment

For every actual symmetric H with operator norm at most1/8 and t in[0,1],
the module derives a symmetric invertible factor M of I+tH, symmetry of
M inverse, inverse norm at most2, factor distance at most1/4, and positive
definiteness of the covariance. It then derives the actual mapped regular
simplex edge lower bound1 from the regular Gram matrix. No factor existence,
invertibility, or edge-length lower bound is supplied as a premise.

The result is a pointwise geometric adapter to the previously proved
spectral C1 covariance segment. All compiled source constants, including
helpers, passed the exhaustive standard-three transitive axiom audit.
