GAUSSIAN WEIGHTED FACET ENERGY: EXACT SCOPE
2 November 2026

GaussianWeightedFacetEnergy.lean, namespace NegativeVotingWeightedFacet.

The actual set-integral inequality is proved, for finite measures:
  integral_F v^2 <= measure(F)^(15/16) * ||v||_32^2.
The finite incidence sum is derived with the same exponent. The final
actual-Gaussian endpoint is affineWeightedFacetEnergy_eighth_power_bound.

Its energy convention is sum_(i != j) phi(a_ij)/ell_ij times the integral
of v_i^2 over the actual ij facet, in intrinsic tangent coordinates.
It does NOT sum v_i^2+v_j^2 over ordered pairs. For symmetric physical
facets it is the unordered sum of both endpoint squares, without a 2.
The chart uses a_ij=(h_j-h_i)/||B_i-B_j|| and the unit normal toward i.
The comparison-plane identity and closedness of the charted facet are
proved. The Gaussian density upper bound is discharged from its formula.

The final hypotheses left visible are:
* ell_ij >= c sqrt(L)/m, with c,L,m positive;
* actual weighted degree sum_j phi(a_ij) pi_ij/ell_ij <= degree;
* each source-label whole-plane trace has L32 norm at most K_i >= 0.
The K_i are uniform over that label's incident planes; their squared
sum is retained in the conclusion. No energy or cap conclusion is assumed.

The precise resulting bound is:
  E <= ((1/sqrt(2*pi))/c)^(1/16) * degree^(15/16)
       * m^(1/8) * L^(-1/32) * sum_i K_i^2.
The key finite Holder step uses
  A*pi^(15/16) = A^(1/16)*(A*pi)^(15/16),
where A=phi(a)/ell and A*pi is the actual offset-Laplacian edge weight.
This avoids every individual facet-probability upper or lower bound.

There is also an optional direct-probability theorem with its upper
probability hypothesis explicit. No claim is made that the sharp
constant-factor bound pi_ij <= C sqrt(L)/m^2 holds for all nearby fans;
it is unnecessary for the degree-based endpoint.

This is sufficient for the all-amplitude cap's main term once the actual
forcing trace bounds are supplied. With sum K_i^2 bounded, its additional
relative shape loss is O(m^(-7/8)*L^(271/32)), which tends to zero.
If the audited forcing normalization gives sum K_i^2=O(L^-3), that is
O(m^(-7/8)*L^(175/32)). These last asymptotic substitutions are mathematical
bookkeeping here, not an assertion that the global theorem is formalized.

The actual degree, fan separation, and forcing trace hypotheses still need
their separate assembly. The exact affine normal disintegration has already
been proved in GaussianFacetDisintegration; no proof source was altered.
