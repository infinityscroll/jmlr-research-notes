Actual moving Gaussian fan cells
Source: GaussianFanMovingCells.lean
Namespace: NegativeVotingFanMovingCells

The new fixed-base continuous linear map sends varying rows/offsets (B',h')
to comparison normals (B'_j-B'_i)/||B_j-B_i|| and supports
(h'_i-h'_j)/||B_j-B_i||. All denominator lengths are fixed at the base point.
The represented polyhedron equals the literal winningCell(B',h',i) for EVERY
varying parameter, assuming only distinct base rows. The proof does not
normalize the varying rows and silently omit the derivative of a norm.

Actual derivative
hasFDerivAt_testedCell composes the proved GaussianMovingCap derivative with
this concrete linear map. Unit normals and NoCoincident are derived from a
positive centered singular lower bound on B; no boundary derivative identity
is an input. F is continuous with the explicit global linear-growth bound
|F(x)| <= C(1+||x||), C>=0. The affine-test corollary derives that growth bound
for F(x)=<E,x>+e using C=||E||+|e|.

Evaluated endpoints
testedCellDerivative_apply gives the actual intrinsic Gaussian tangent-face
integral. testedCellDerivative_apply_ambient transports it to the ambient
conditional Gaussian law on the same literal affineFacet, with the exact
coefficient gaussianPDFReal(0,1,pairOffset(B,h,j,i))/||B_j-B_i|| and integrand
  F(x) [<delta B_i-delta B_j,x> + delta h_i-delta h_j].
The chart pushforward law and chart preimage of the actual facet are proved
separately. There is one Gaussian density and one edge-length denominator;
no extra conditional face-probability factor is inserted.

Meaningful remaining boundary
This source proves a first Frechet derivative of the tested moving cells.
It does not yet establish continuity of that derivative as base rows/offsets
vary, a joint C1 prescribed-mass offset choice, the paired expected-maximum
Hessian, the optimized covariance Hessian, or the main negative-voting theorem.
The paired-Hessian and joint-regularity modules can now use these actual
identities rather than assume boundary variation formulas.
