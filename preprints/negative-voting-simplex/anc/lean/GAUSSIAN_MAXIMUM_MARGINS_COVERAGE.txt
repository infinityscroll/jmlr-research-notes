FINITE POSITIVE GAUSSIAN MARGIN AND NEGATIVE ENVELOPE INTEGRAL

GaussianMaximumTailMargins.lean derives, for the actual independent Gaussian
maximum scaled by its actual upper 1/m quantile b, whenever b^2>=10^6:
  P(Z>t) >= (999/1000)exp(-t) - exp(-2t)/2,  0<=t<=8.
This is a finite inequality. Its proof combines both checked Mills bounds,
the actual finite maximum CDF and the proved Bonferroni inequality; it has
no assumed limiting law or moment-convergence premise. The far lower tail
is also proved directly:
  P(M_m<=-s) <= 2^(-m)exp(-m s^2/2),  s>=0.

GaussianDoubleExponentialIntegral.lean proves the actual integral bound
  integral_[0,infinity) exp(-exp((99/100)t)) dt < 23/99.
It derives the half-line substitution u=exp(t), then bounds the actual
exponential integral integral_[1,infinity) exp(-u)/u du by reciprocal
secants on [1,3/2], [3/2,2], [2,3] and the constant upper bound 1/3 on
[3,infinity). Certified mathlib exponential bounds yield the rational
upper bound 23/100 before rescaling. The exact substitution and all
integrability premises are proved; no special-function constant is assumed.

These are the two strict-margin ingredients for an eventual positive
mean/quantile gap. The remaining middle/far-tail integrals and signed
expectation assembly are separate tasks. No completed mass-localization
or Gumbel-convergence theorem is claimed here.

Reproduce:

  lake env lean GAUSSIAN_MAXIMUM_MARGINS_AUDIT.lean
