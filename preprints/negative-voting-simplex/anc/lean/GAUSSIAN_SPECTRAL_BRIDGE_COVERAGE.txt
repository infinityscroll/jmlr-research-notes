MAIN RESULT

Let kappa be any finite coordinate type and iota any finite label type.
Let gamma be the finite product of actual standard Gaussian measures.
For measurable functions f_i with f_i(x)>=0 and sum_i f_i(x)=1, and
every real rho with |rho|<=1, the file proves

  integral_(x,y) sum_i f_i(x) f_i(rho*x + sqrt(1-rho^2)*y) d(gamma*gamma)
    = sum_(k>=0) rho^k A_k(f).

A_k(f) is DEFINED as the sum of squares of the actual normalized tensor
Hermite coefficients of the components f_i over multiindices of total
degree k. It is not a free parameter or an assumed spectral expansion.

The proof derives:
  * square-integrability of each simplex coordinate;
  * the actual Gaussian L2 noise coefficient identity;
  * the full series identity from the constructed complete Hermite basis;
  * nonnegative A_k, summability, and sum_k A_k <= 1;
  * absolute convergence of the signed noise series;
  * A_0 = sum_i (integral f_i)^2;
  * A_1 = sum_i sum_j (integral x_j f_i(x))^2.

The degree-one identification proves that degree-one multiindices are
exactly the coordinate unit multiindices. Empty coordinate spaces are
allowed; their degree-one fiber is empty.

NEGATIVE CORRELATION

The imported coefficient theorem requires only rho^2+s^2=1 for real rho,s.
It has no nonnegative-rho restriction. Instantiating s=sqrt(1-rho^2)
therefore proves the representation throughout [-1,1], with the factor
rho^k unchanged. For negative rho, every odd degree retains its negative
sign. Neither absolute rho nor an unsigned spectral sum replaces it.
