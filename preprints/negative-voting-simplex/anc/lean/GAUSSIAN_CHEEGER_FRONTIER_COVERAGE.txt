ACTUAL GAUSSIAN CHEEGER AND AFFINE-FAN CUT CONDUCTANCE

Main endpoints in GaussianCheeger.lean and GaussianFanCheeger.lean:
  min(gamma(A),1-gamma(A))/pi <= openBoundaryMeasure(gamma,A)
  min(sum_{i in I} mass_i,1-sum_{i in I} mass_i)
    <= 2*pi*sum_{i in I,j notin I} ||B_i-B_j||*facetWeight_ij.
Here mass and facetWeight are the literal winning-cell probabilities and
intrinsic facet formulas. Sites must be injective. The ambient finite
Euclidean dimension is arbitrary. Identification of those facet formulas
with a differentiable mass Jacobian uses the separate full-rank theorem;
no differentiability claim for every injective degenerate fan is made here.

The proof derives the Gaussian functional inequality from the frozen
GaussianL1Variance rotation/FTC/Rademacher result, uses an attributed
Lipschitz-ramp conversion, covers each cut shell only by crossing-face tubes,
and proves the true normal/tangent Gaussian tube estimate. It also derives
cut-mass additivity using Gaussian tie-nullness. No Gaussian Cheeger,
perimeter-to-facet, cut-mass, or boundary normalization premise remains.

Constants are deliberately nonsharp: Cheeger 1/pi and a two-sided tube
factor 2. No sharper manuscript constants are being reused.
