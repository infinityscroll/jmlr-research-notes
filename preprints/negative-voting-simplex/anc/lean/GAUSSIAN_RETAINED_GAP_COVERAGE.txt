ACTUAL RETAINED GAUSSIAN GRAPH GAP AND MIXED-CURVATURE CONNECTION

GaussianFanRetainedCuts.lean derives the original cut constant
  kappa = p0/(2*pi*ellMax)
from actual winning masses >= p0 and actual edge lengths <= ellMax. It proves
that deleting weights below tau loses at most tau*|S|*|S-complement| per cut.
Thus tau*m <= kappa/2 retains cut constant kappa/2. Retained weights are literal
thresholded intrinsic Gaussian facet weights, remain nonnegative and symmetric,
and preserve every upper bound D on the original weighted row degrees.

GaussianFanRetainedGap.lean combines the proved finite coarea and signed-square
Rayleigh arguments with these actual Gaussian cuts. For centered real v:
  ((p0/(2*pi*ellMax))^2/(32*D))*sum_i v_i^2
    <= (1/2)*sum_{i,j retained} facetWeight_ij*(v_i-v_j)^2.
The required scalar hypotheses are p0>=0, ellMax>0, tau>=0, D>0, the stated
actual mass/edge/degree bounds, and tau*m<=kappa/2. No cut-expansion, coarea,
Poincare, or spectral-gap estimate is assumed.

For generic injective sites this is a statement about the literal facet
formula. The full-rank Jacobian interpretation is used in the final affine-
basis application, where that interpretation has already been proved.

The affine-basis corollary uses exactly retainedOffsetEnergy from
GaussianCovarianceMixedEstimate. The final
canonicalOffset_control_from_actual_graph theorem inserts the proved
coefficient into actual covariance-Hessian control, removing the earlier
supplied Rayleigh premise. Its retained facet coefficient ceiling K remains
explicit, along with the concrete mass/edge/degree/threshold bounds. These
geometric inequalities must still be specialized to the main mass window.

Constants are the proved nonsharp constants, not sharper manuscript values.
For p0=c/m, ellMax=C*sqrt(L)/m and D<=D0, the coefficient has order 1/L;
this scaling statement is arithmetic guidance, not a new Lean export here.
