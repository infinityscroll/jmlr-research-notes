1. Actual derivative, not an assumed Hessian
   For fixed sites B and offsets h, NegativeVotingFan.mass B h i is the
   integral of the actual maximum-score assignment. Under a positive
   CenteredSingularLowerBound B sigma, mass_eq_capMass identifies it with
   the real Gaussian measure of the winning polyhedron. Unit normals and
   noncoincidence are derived by frozen GaussianFacetDegree lemmas.

   hasFDerivAt_mass follows by composing the checked FSC cap-mass Frechet
   derivative with the explicit continuous linear threshold map
       h -> ((h_i-h_j)/||B_j-B_i||)_(j != i).
   hasFDerivAt_mass_vector assembles the full vector derivative.
   contDiff_mass_vector proves the actual mass map is C1 in all offsets
   for these fixed sites, using the actual cap C1 theorem.

2. Exact coefficient and orientation
   facetWeight B h i j = phi(pairOffset_ij)/||B_i-B_j|| times the intrinsic
   Gaussian probability of the actual ij facet. This is exactly the
   coefficient in GaussianWeightedFacetEnergy/GaussianFacetDegree, with
   one density factor and one edge denominator.

   massDerivative_apply evaluates the actual derivative as
       D mass_i(h)[v] = sum_j a_ij (v_i-v_j).
   massDerivative_eq_offsetJacobian identifies its literal finite matrix
       J = diag(sum_j a_ij) - (a_ij).
   Diagonal a_ii is zero. There is no duplicate pair factor in this formula.

3. Symmetry, energy, and positivity
   facetWeight_eq_ambient transports the intrinsic probability to the
   actual ambient affine-slice Gaussian measure. Reversing both the unit
   normal and its scalar offset leaves this pushforward measure unchanged.
   The actual affine facet is symmetric under i,j. These facts prove a_ij=a_ji.

   offsetJacobian_isHermitian and offsetJacobian_posSemidef are unconditional
   matrix facts for these literal coefficients, not PSD hypotheses.
   The exact ordered-pair identity is
       v^T J v = (1/2) sum_i sum_j a_ij (v_i-v_j)^2.
   offsetJacobian_constant proves J annihilates constant offsets.

4. Strict positivity is derived from actual geometry
   affineIndependent_of_centered_lower derives affine independence from
   the positive centered singular lower bound.
   exists_inner_values extends the linear functional on an independent
   family and applies finite-dimensional Riesz representation.
   exists_strict_pair_point uses this to produce, for any distinct i,j and
   any h, an actual point where i,j tie and strictly beat all other labels.

   facetWeight_pos_of_strict_point constructs a nonempty open preimage
   under the affine Gaussian plane projection. Full-support Gaussian
   positivity implies the actual face measure is strictly positive.
   Thus facetWeight_pos_of_affineIndependent has no assumed face-measure
   or graph-connectivity input.

   offsetJacobian_energy_zero_iff and offsetJacobian_kernel_iff show the
   nullspace is exactly the constants under affine independence.
   offsetJacobian_pos_centered proves v^T J v > 0 for every nonzero v
   satisfying sum_i v_i=0. The final _of_lower corollary supplies affine
   independence from the manuscript's positive centered singular bound.

5. Remaining boundaries
   This is the complete offset-mass block for fixed sites. It does not
   identify the full joint site/offset Hessian or prove moving-normal
   derivative formulas. Strict positivity is qualitative at each B,h;
   no uniform spectral lower bound in label count or along rank limits
   is claimed. No full negative-voting theorem is asserted.
