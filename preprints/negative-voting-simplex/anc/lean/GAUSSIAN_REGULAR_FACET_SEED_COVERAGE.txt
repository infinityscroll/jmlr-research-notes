GaussianRegularFacetSeed: exact actual Gaussian regular-facet normalization

For any actual regular score rows with Gram delta_ij-1/m, m>=3, the literal
Gaussian facet weights are exactly alpha/m for i!=j, with alpha=mu/(m-1).
For the actual self-centroid rows alpha B_i, the weights are exactly 1/m.
These equalities are derived by pairing the actual Gaussian centroid-boundary
identity with a different regular row, isolating one facet. No face symmetry
or Gaussian surface weight formula is assumed.

The module also proves the regular edge length sqrt(2), and the exact
intrinsic Gaussian facet probability
  P_facet = alpha sqrt(2)/(m gaussianPDFReal(0,1,0)).
This is the genuine measure on the tangent Gaussian space, with its normal
PDF normalization derived from the literal facetWeight definition.

These are seed identities for the later quantitative covariance/facet
perturbation argument. They are not a neighborhood stability estimate.
