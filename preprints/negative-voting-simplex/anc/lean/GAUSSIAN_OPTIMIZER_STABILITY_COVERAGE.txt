Compactness of the unit sphere derives a coercivity constant; Hessian continuity makes it locally uniform; a real mean-value argument proves strong gradient decrease. Actual constrained variational inequalities and local C1-gradient Lipschitz estimates prove optimizer stability, including boundary maxima. No differentiability of the optimizer is assumed.
