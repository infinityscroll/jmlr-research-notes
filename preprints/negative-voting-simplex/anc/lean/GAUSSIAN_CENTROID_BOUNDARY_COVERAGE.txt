ACTUAL GAUSSIAN CENTROID-BOUNDARY AND INTERIOR IDENTITIES

Source: GaussianFanCentroidBoundary.lean.
Namespace: NegativeVotingFanCentroid.

For actual Gaussian winning cells under a positive centered singular bound,
centroid_eq_boundary_laplacian proves
  centroid_i = sum_j facetWeight_ij * (B_i-B_j).
Both centroid and facetWeight are the existing concrete Gaussian integrals.
The theorem derives this identity from GaussianCapFirstMoment's vector
boundary formula, the actual cap=winning-cell identity, and the checked
normalization/plane transport to the intrinsic facet weight. It does not
assume a boundary identity.

centroid_pairing proves, for every vector family V_i,
  sum_i <centroid_i,V_i>
   = (1/2) sum_ij facetWeight_ij <B_i-B_j,V_i-V_j>.

centroid_linear_pairing gives the corresponding formula with V_i=Q(B_i)
for any actual linear operator Q. In particular, for a genuine symmetric
linear operator H, centroid_symmetric_square_pairing proves
  sum_i <centroid_i,H(H(B_i))>
   = (1/2) sum_ij facetWeight_ij ||H(B_i-B_j)||^2.

centroid_symmetric_mixed_pairing retains a second arbitrary linear operator K:
  sum_i <centroid_i,H(K(B_i))>
   = (1/2) sum_ij facetWeight_ij <H(B_i-B_j),K(B_i-B_j)>.
Only H is required to be symmetric. This is the polarized interior term
needed when differentiating a covariance factorization.

The finite summation-by-parts identity is separately proved for arbitrary
symmetric real weights and arbitrary vector families. For the concrete
application, weight symmetry is the previously proved actual Gaussian
facet-weight symmetry.

This module does not supply the covariance square-root path, optimized
offset derivatives, or full negative-voting theorem. Its exported inputs
are concrete Gaussian geometry and actual linear-operator symmetry.
