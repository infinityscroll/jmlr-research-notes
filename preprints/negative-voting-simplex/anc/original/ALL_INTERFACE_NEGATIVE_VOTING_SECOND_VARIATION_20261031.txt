4. Finite moment space and exact decomposition
Let Pfin be generated by facet speeds
 (u_i-u_j), and (z_i-z_j) dot x,
where u in H, z_i in H, and sum_i z_i=0. These are exactly the N_0
representers of the zeroth and first label-boundary moments. Their possible
trace direction gives zero speed, since(e_i-e_j) dot x=0 on its facet.

This space is the direct sum of
 Pstd: (u_i-u_j)+(w_i-w_j)(z_e-M), u,w in H;
 Psh:  (T_i-T_j) dot x/sqrt2, T=T^T,T1=0,T_ii=0;
 Prot: normal speeds of rigid rotations within H.                       (13)
Here z_e=x_i=x_j in H. For completeness, split the centered row matrix(z_i)
into skew and symmetric parts. The skew part gives rotations. From the
symmetric part subtract a scalar multiple of P=I-11^T/m, which gives zero
speed. Its remaining trace is zero. Subtract P diag(u)P to kill its
diagonal, using u=m/(m-2) times that diagonal. The remainder is T as in
Psh. Centering the tied coordinate by M is absorbed by the first standard
copy. This also proves completeness and absence of a missing low mode.

N_0 makes these components orthogonal except for the intended two copies
inside Pstd. Q_0 has no cross term between Pstd and Psh. An elementary
invariant-tensor check suffices: the regular partition and kernel are
permutation invariant. Every coefficient tensor with three or four label
indices is a linear combination of index-equality patterns. For centered
matrices, after zero row/column sums the surviving contractions are
entrywise, transposed-entrywise, diagonal-entrywise, and traces. A symmetric
zero-diagonal T pairs to zero with P diag(u)P in every such contraction;
the vector/matrix contractions are diagonal or row sums and also vanish.
This proves standard/shape orthogonality for any invariant bilinear form,
including Q_0 and N_0, without postulating a spectral classification.

Rotation speeds annihilate the FULL Q_0 bilinear form. Every rotated
reference partition is stationary for every test deformation. Differentiate
that first-variation identity in rotation time. The acceleration term
vanishes because the reference first variation is zero. Thus
Q_0(p_rot,f)=0 for regular compact tests and then for the weighted closure
by(2). Quadratic vanishing alone would not justify this cross identity.

Indeed the conditional mean is

Cov_e(x,z_e)=[mv/(m-2)](e_i+e_j-2/m).
The incidence sum with(u_i-u_j) is m P diag(u)P, whose squared Frobenius
norm is(m-2)|u|^2/m. These facts verify every factor in(14).

The tie potential has second derivative at least2, so Var(T)<=1/2 and
 m v/(m-2)=[m Var(T)-1]/(m-2)<=1/2.                                   (15)
Using |a+b|^2<=3|a|^2+(3/2)|b|^2 gives
 A_0(p)+r[N_0(p)-A_1(p)]>=c r N_0(p).                                (16)
In detail the u coefficient is at least

Changing the diagonal N_0 to N_r costs at most Cr^2L^2N_0 by(11).

7. Arbitrary weighted speeds: projection and coupling
Let N_r(f)<infinity without assuming N_0(f)<infinity. Its zeroth and first
moments exist by the inverse-diagonal damping bound and Gaussian moments.
Define p in Pfin by the finite-dimensional Riesz equations
 sum_e int p_e w_e d lambda_e=sum_e int f_e w_e d lambda_e
                                        for every w in Pfin,
and put g=f-p. The N_0 Gram form is positive definite after zero-speed
directions are discarded. Equation(11) ensures boundedness of these
moment functionals in N_r, so this projection remains valid for tail speeds
outside unweighted L2. By construction
 A_0(g)=A_1(g)=0, and sum_e int p_e g_e d lambda_e=0.                   (21)
The trace of the first boundary-moment matrix is identically zero, so
there is no omitted trace constraint.

Keep all even chaos terms and use(2) only for odd degrees>=3:
 Q_0(g)>=rN_r(g)-Cr^3L^3N_r(g)>=rN_r(g)/2.                            (22)
The mixed degrees0 and1 vanish. Its diagonal cross subtracts B0 exactly
by(21). Applying(10),(3), and(11) therefore gives
 |Q_0(p,g)|
 <=r|sum_e int (beta_e-B0)p_e g_e d lambda_e|
                +Cr^2L^2 sqrt(N_r(p)N_r(g))
 <=Cr^2L^2 sqrt(N_0(p)N_r(g)).                                       (23)
The diagonal term alone is O(r^2L^(3/2)); the stated bound includes the
entire degree>=2 boundary cross. No arbitrary-speed unweighted estimate
has replaced N_r.

Remove the rotation component of p, which changes neither Q_0(f) nor
the mixed form. Combining(20),(22),(23), then absorbing the cross term,
 Q_0(f)>=[c rL/m-Cr^3L^4]N_0(p)+(r/4)N_r(g).
Because r^2 mL^3<=C L^3/m tends0 uniformly, this proves
 Q_0(f)>=c' r(L/m)N_0(p)+(r/4)N_r(g)>=0.                              (24)
Equality requires p=g=0 after rotation removal. Thus the intrinsic
nullspace is exactly Prot. By(11), the right side also controls a constant
times r(L/m) times squared N_r-distance to the rotation space.

8. The indispensable extra-coordinate degree1 block
The intrinsic result alone does not establish all ambient dimensions.
The external Hermite degree1 block is
                       Q_1(f)=r[N_r(f)-A_-r(f)].                     (25)
For constant translation speeds t_e(v)=n_e dot v, v in H, the exact Jacobi
identity in[I] is A_-r t=beta t. Hence Q_1(t,f)=0 for all f.

Now take f N_r-orthogonal to these translations. Equivalently
sum_e b_e int beta_e f_e d lambda_e=0. Its zeroth moment vector is therefore
sum_e b_e a_e with a_e=int(1-beta_e/B0)f_e d lambda_e.
The complete-graph incidence identity BB^T=mP and Cauchy imply
 A_0(f)<=m sum_e a_e^2
       <=m(s/B0)chi_r N_r(f)=chi_r N_r(f).                            (26)
Indeed the scalar Cauchy factor on each edge is
int(1-beta/B0)^2/beta d lambda=(s/B0)chi_r, and s/B0=1/m.
Using(2),(12) and
A_-r<=A_0+sum_(l>=2)r^l A_l, we obtain
 Q_1(f)>=r[1-Cr^2L^2]N_r(f)>=rN_r(f)/2                               (27)
on that orthogonal complement. Thus Q_1 is nonnegative on the full space
with nullspace exactly the constant translation speeds. Multiplying one
of these by an extra-coordinate linear Hermite gives an ambient rotation
mixing H with that coordinate, not a mass-changing translation.

9. All ambient degrees and the precise conclusion
Expand a general speed in Hermites of the unused coordinates. Since beta
depends only on H, this expansion is orthogonal for N_r. The exact block is
 Q_k(f)=(-r)^k A_-r(f)+rN_r(f).
For l>=0,
 Q_(2l)=r^(2l)Q_0+(1-r^(2l))rN_r,
 Q_(2l+1)=r^(2l)Q_1+(1-r^(2l))rN_r.                                  (28)
Every block is nonnegative; every nonzero speed in degree k>=2 is strictly
positive because 0<r<1. The degree0 and1 kernels are exactly the ambient
rotation speeds already identified. This proves the stated all-N theorem.

For compact smooth regular-patch speeds these are ordinary second
variations of actual Gaussian partitions. Truncating radius and deleting
junction neighborhoods gives density in the natural weighted space,
and(2),(1) give the continuous form extension. There is no negative-case
mass restriction. No nonlinear remainder or global comparison is inferred
from this Hessian statement. The pure-lens/core classification problem
