It states that there is an absolute integer m0 such that, for each integer

2. Original named target and history
Isaksson--Mossel, arXiv0903.3362v3, Conjecture1.4, formulates Standard
Simplex for N>=m-1: positive correlation maximization requires equal
masses, while negative correlation minimization has no mass restriction.
It attributes the Gaussian conjecture to Mossel around2004. The first
arXiv version appeared19March2009; v3 is dated3August2009.

Their Conjecture1.9 formulates Plurality is Stablest for the symmetric
m-state channel rho in[-1/(m-1),1]. Its negative branch also imposes no
output-balance condition. Theorems1.10--1.11 provide the Gaussian/discrete
connection on that channel range. Consequently the proposed Gaussian
statement addresses a recognized branch of a longstanding named problem,
rather than an invented fixed-mass or communications-simplex variant.
The exact discrete quantifiers and hardness transfer are audited separately.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/0903.3362v3
Locations: Conjectures1.4,1.9; Theorems1.10--1.11.

as "an asymptotic large-m result": that wording obscures the exactness
and interval quantifiers that constitute the proposed advance.
