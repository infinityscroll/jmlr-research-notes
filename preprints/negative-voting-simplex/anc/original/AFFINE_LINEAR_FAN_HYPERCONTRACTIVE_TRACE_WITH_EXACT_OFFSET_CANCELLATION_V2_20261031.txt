AFFINE LINEAR FANS: HYPERCONTRACTIVE TRACE WITH EXACT OFFSET CANCELLATION
31 October 2026. Branch derivation. Independent hostile audit pending.

THEOREM
Fix positive constants c0,C0,Cp. For sufficiently large m let
alpha=E max_(i<=m)Z_i/(m-1). Let B be a centered m-by-N Gaussian score
matrix of rank exactly m-1, N>=m-1, whose m-1 nonzero singular values
lie in[c0 alpha,C0 alpha].
Let its affine winning fan, with arbitrary optimal offsets, have masses
0<p_i<=Cp/m. For each actual facet e=ij write
ell_e=|B_i-B_j| and lambda_e=Gaussian surface measure on that facet.
Set q=2+log(m/alpha) and Lambda=2q-1=O(log m).
There is C depending only on c0,C0,Cp such that, whenever s^2 Lambda<=1,

 sum_e (1/ell_e) int |T_s(u_i-u_j)|^2 d lambda_e
       <=C(s^2 Lambda)^k sum_i||u_i||_2^2                       (1)

for every vector u supported in total Hermite degrees at least k>=0.
The constant is independent of N, every facet probability, and the
offset magnitudes. Consequently, for boundary speeds f with
N_lin(f)=sum_e ell_e int f_e^2 d lambda_e<infinity, and their label
boundary measures h_i=sum_e(B_inc)_ie f_e lambda_e,

 sum_(l>=k) s^(2l) A_l(f)<=C(s^2 Lambda)^k N_lin(f).             (2)

Here A_l is the squared degree-l Hermite coefficient norm of the vector
boundary distribution. Statement(2) is a Hilbert-space trace estimate,
not a finite nonlinear perturbation theorem.

1. Gaussian facet area has a bounded row sum at the score scale
Let mu_e=lambda_e(Sigma_e), w_e=mu_e/ell_e, and L the offset Laplacian.
Gaussian integration by parts gives M=L B, where M_i is the unnormalized
centroid of winning cell i. Since p_i<=Cp/m,
 ||M_i||<=p_i sqrt(2log(1/p_i))<=C sqrt(log m)/m<=C alpha.
The pseudoinverse bound ||B^+||op<=1/(c0 alpha) implies ||L_i||<=C,
and hence every degree sum_j w_ij=L_ii<=C. Also
 c alpha<=ell_e<=C alpha.
It follows that
                          sum_j mu_ij<=C alpha.                 (3)
No lower cell mass, minimum facet probability or offset bound enters(3).
All sites are distinct by the singular-value hypothesis. Empty facets,
if any, contribute zero and can simply be omitted.

2. Exact normal restriction and the offset cancellation
Consider any affine hyperplane at signed distance a from the origin.
In orthogonal coordinates x=(a,z), standard Gaussian surface measure is
phi(a) d gamma_(N-1)(z). Let E be any measurable part of this plane,
pi=gamma_(N-1)(E), and mu=phi(a)pi.

Let t^2=1/(2q-1). The normal Gaussian Mehler row at a has squared L2 norm
 K_t(a)^2=(1-t^4)^(-1/2) exp[t^2 a^2/(1+t^2)].                   (4)
This follows by a one-dimensional Gaussian integral, or the semigroup
identity int M_t(a,y)^2 dgamma(y)=M_(t^2)(a,a). Write T_t as normal
restriction followed by tangential OU smoothing. Cauchy in the normal
variable and tangential L2-to-L^(2q) hypercontractivity give
 ||T_t v(a,.)||_(2q)<=K_t(a)||v||_2.
Holder on E therefore gives
 int_E |T_t v|^2 d lambda
 <=mu^(1-1/q) phi(a)^(1/q) K_t(a)^2 ||v||_2^2.                  (5)

The offset exponent cancels EXACTLY at the chosen hypercontractive time:
 t^2/(1+t^2)=1/(2q),
 phi(a)^(1/q) K_t(a)^2
       =(2pi)^(-1/(2q))(1-t^4)^(-1/2)<=C.                       (6)
Thus(5) has no factor involving a. It holds for arbitrary measurable
facet subsets, without cone homogeneity or a conditional radial MGF.

3. Sum the traces without a minimum-facet-probability assumption
Apply(5)-(6) to each actual facet with v_i-v_j. The resulting effective
edge weights are omega_e=mu_e^(1-1/q)/ell_e. By(3) and Holder over the at
most m-1 incident labels,
 sum_j omega_ij
 <=(C/alpha) m^(1/q) (sum_j mu_ij)^(1-1/q)
 <=C m^(1/q) alpha^(-1/q)
 <=C exp(log(m/alpha)/q)<=Ce.                                  (7)
The complete edge sum of ||v_i-v_j||_2^2 with these weights is at most
twice their maximum degree times sum_i||v_i||_2^2. Consequently
 sum_e ell_e^-1 int |T_t(v_i-v_j)|^2 d lambda_e
                                      <=C sum_i||v_i||_2^2.     (8)

For a vector u in degrees at least k and s<=t, write
 T_su=T_t v, v=T_(s/t)u.
Orthogonality of Hermite degrees gives
 sum_i||v_i||^2<= (s/t)^(2k) sum_i||u_i||^2
               =(s^2 Lambda)^k sum_i||u_i||^2.
Combining with(8) proves(1), including k=0. Infinite-dimensional L2 tests
follow by density and the bounded restriction operators just constructed.

4. Dual boundary-distribution statement
For a finite Hermite test u in degrees at least k, Cauchy on the facets
and(1) give
 |sum_i <h_i,T_su_i>|^2
 <=N_lin(f) sum_e ell_e^-1 int |T_s(u_i-u_j)|^2 d lambda_e
 <=C(s^2 Lambda)^k N_lin(f) sum_i||u_i||_2^2.
The Riesz representation theorem therefore defines the projected
smoothed boundary vector as an L2 function with that norm bound. Taking
the supremum over finite Hermite tests gives(2); monotone convergence of
the nonnegative Hermite norm sum removes the finite truncation.

5. Intended use and remaining nonlinear issue
The lemma controls high Hermite perturbations uniformly around every
conditioned affine score fan, including the mass-calibrated perturbed
fans used by the fixed-mass first-chaos dual. The exact cancellation(6)
removes the ambient evaluation factor and arbitrary-offset penalty.

It does not assert that a finite nonlinear high-degree perturbation
retains affine interfaces or the degree bound(3). Nor does it compare
the full reference potential to a linear cost. Those nonlinear steps
remain required before this trace can close the full-noise argument.
