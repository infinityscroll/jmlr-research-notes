CRITICAL DEPENDENCY REVIEW: CYCLE REFINEMENT AND THE MASS ENVELOPE
2 November 2026

OUTCOME AND SCOPE

The specific chain examined was
  finite-fan Gaussian boundary identities
  -> exact cycle cancellation with a retained Ehrhard term
  -> a uniform mixed covariance/offset derivative bound at regularized optima
  -> V(p) <= w + C(c,Cbar)(1+log m)^2 ||p-U||^2
on the fixed positive relative-mass box c/m <= p_i <= Cbar/m.

I reconstructed this chain directly and found no blocking gap in it. The
quantitative constant shorthand required a correction: the elementary
normalized-offset estimate gives q_e^2 <= 2 log(m/c), not directly
2(1+log m) for an arbitrary fixed c. The new preprint now writes the
correct estimate and absorbs its fixed-c dependence explicitly. The
regularization monotonicity step is also expanded into its direct coupling
argument. Historical sources are preserved unchanged.

This is a focused analytic reconstruction by an AI agent, not external
expert review, proof-assistant certification of these analytic steps, or
an independent certification of the entire negative-voting theorem. The
separate local high-chaos/cap and exterior estimates are outside this
review's reconstructed conclusion.

1. GAUSSIAN BOUNDARY IDENTITIES: SIGNS AND GEOMETRY

At positive covariance, whitening gives affinely independent sites y_i in
R^(m-1). Every offset vector is c-y_i.a, so its diagram is a translated
simplex normal fan. Its strata are convex polyhedra with Gaussian-decaying
tails. The following integration by parts can be justified on truncated
strata; the sphere terms vanish by Gaussian polynomial tail bounds.

Let mu_S and m_S be the intrinsic Gaussian surface mass and conditional
mean on a stratum, and P_S its tangent projector. With inward boundary
normals n_(S,k), the first-moment identity is
  mu_S P_S m_S = sum_(k outside S) mu_(S+k) n_(S,k).
For an edge facet e, differentiating the centered first moment gives
  mu_e(P_e-Cov_e X)
    = sum_(k outside e) mu_(e+k) n_(e,k)(m_e-m_(e+k))^T.
The sign can also be seen directly from the outward normal -n_(e,k).
The displacement between the two conditional means is tangential, so the
right side acts in the facet tangent space. Gaussian convex-restriction
Brascamp--Lieb gives Cov_e X <= P_e; no invertibility of this deficit is
needed.

For a target triangle g, put D_g=2 Area(g)/mu_g. Let v_k be minus the
gradient of target barycentric coordinate k. Then sum_k v_k=0 and
v_k.(y_i-y_j)=delta_(k,j)-delta_(k,i). For facet e=(i,j), the projected
vectors P_e(y_i-y_k), k outside e, are a basis and have dual rows v_k^T.
Consequently the matrix N_e with columns (mu_(e+k)/|y_i-y_j|) n_(e,k)
has inverse rows D_(e+k) v_k^T. These inverses are on the tangent space.

Writing w_e=mu_e/|y_i-y_j| and E_e=w_e(P_e-Cov_e X), the boundary identities
therefore give
  E_e=N_e T_e^T,  T_e[:,g]=m_e-m_g,
  B_e=N_e^-1 E_e N_e^-T=N_e^-1 T_e,
  N_e 1=w_e P_e m_e.
No inverse of E_e appears.

2. THE TRIANGLE/TETRAHEDRON FACTORIZATION

Use oriented simplex boundary maps partial_2 and partial_3 and write W for
diag(w_e). Assemble A=partial_2^T W^-1 partial_2 and B from the local B_e
with the same edge/triangle incidence signs. Then
  A-B=-D partial_3 diag(mu_h/(6 Vol(h))) partial_3^T D.

To verify an off-diagonal entry, triangles g and g' sharing an edge e
have one outside vertex k. The vector v_k is tangent to the g' stratum
and pairs to zero with all its boundary normals except that for adding k.
The surviving pairing is the reciprocal height, giving
  v_k.m_g' = mu_(g union g') Area(g')/[3 Vol(g union g') mu_g'].
Combining this with the inverse-row formula gives the off-diagonal entry
with coefficient D_g D_g' mu_h/(6 Vol(h)); its sign is the negative product
of the two tetrahedron incidence signs because partial_2 partial_3=0.
For a diagonal entry, sum the three outside-edge barycentric vectors on g
and use sum_k v_k=0. The result is -D_g^2 sum_(h containing g) mu_h/(6Vol(h)),
which is exactly the diagonal of the same factorization. Nonincident
triangles give zero. This argument is dimension-independent: target
triangle areas and tetrahedron volumes are intrinsic Euclidean volumes.

Fix an actual edge cycle j. All lambda with partial_2 lambda=j differ by
im(partial_3). Minimizing lambda^T D lambda on this affine space gives
partial_3^T D lambda=0 without changing j. Therefore the factorization gives
  sum_e eta_e^T B_e eta_e=j^T W^-1 j,
where eta_(e,g)=(partial_2)_(e,g)lambda_g. It is the triangle representative
that changes, not the physical offsets or target masses.

3. EXACT RESIDUAL AND THE MIXED OFFSET TERM

Give cells vectors z_i; put z_e=z_i-z_j and c_e=z_e.m_e. Let b minimize
sum_e w_e(c_e+b_i-b_j)^2. Its Euler equation makes
j_e=w_e(c_e+b_i-b_j) a cycle, and its energy is CYC=j^T W^-1 j=j.c.
Triangle telescoping gives sum_e z_e.T_e eta_e=j.c. With the preceding
weighted gauge and r_e=z_e-N_e^-T eta_e, direct square expansion proves
  sum_e r_e^T E_e r_e=sum_e z_e^T E_e z_e-CYC.
This is an equality after subtracting the full cycle energy. Also
  r_e.P_e m_e=-(b_i-b_j)-z_e.(I-P_e)m_e,
since N_e^-1 P_e m_e=1/w_e and the entries of eta_e sum to j_e.

For a convex tangential section K of probability pi, intrinsic Ehrhard
applied to translations of K gives
  I-Cov(X|K) >= a(pi) E[X|K]E[X|K]^T,
  a(pi)=1+Phi^-1(pi) pi/phi(Phi^-1(pi)) > 0.
Indeed differentiation of Phi^-1(gamma(K+x)) yields this matrix inequality.
For negative q=-t, a(pi)=integral_0^infinity u exp(-tu-u^2/2)du; restricting
u to [0,1/(1+t)] gives the quantitative reciprocal bound
  a(pi)^-1 <= 8 exp(3/2)[1+log(1/pi)].

Apply Ehrhard to r_e, not to z_e. For covariance perturbation H at the
whitened base, set z_i=H y_i. The covariance square-root derivatives are
H/2 and -H^2/4. The exact covariance Hessian thus has factor four, and
its minimizing offset variable is b=2h'. If ell_e=|y_i-y_j|,
x_e=d_e^T H d_e/ell_e, and q_e=(h_i-h_j)/ell_e, then
  -4 F_CC[A,A] >= sum_e w_e [x_e^2+a(pi_e)(2h'_e-q_e x_e)^2].
The normal component of the facet mean is -q_e d_e/ell_e, fixing the
minus sign in this bracket. No raw covariance-deficit bound is substituted
for the corrected residual.

4. REGULARIZATION, PAIR SEPARATION, AND THE PHYSICAL GRAPH

Square completion identifies V(p)=max_(C>=0){2F_p(C)-tr C}, including
fractional assignments. A support maximizer may be chosen pure for this
linear fixed-mass problem, with measurable splitting of ties; this does
not prove purity of any full-noise minimizer.

The bound F_p(C)<=sqrt(tr C) makes optimizing covariances bounded. If
(X,Y) is any admissible source/target coupling, and G is an independent
N(0,eps I_H) vector, then (X+G,Y) has the new Gaussian source covariance
and the same target law, with E[(X+G).Y]=E[X.Y]. Taking suprema proves
F_p(C+eps I_H)>=F_p(C), even at singular C. Testing C*+eps I_H at a true
optimizer therefore gives
  0<=V(p)-V_eps(p)<=eps(m-1).

At a true optimum, square completion forces score rows B to equal actual
centroids. Re-split two cells' fixed union along a direction perpendicular
to their centroid sum. Its unnormalized projected density is bounded by
phi(0). If the upper and lower masses are p_i,p_j, integration of the
smallest possible tail first moments gives a projected centroid difference
at least p_i p_j/phi(0). Keeping the vector sum fixed and using optimality
of the squared centroid norm gives |B_i-B_j|>=sqrt(2pi)p_i p_j.
Gaussian exponential Jensen gives the corresponding upper edge bound.

At fixed m, the covariance bound, joint continuity in (C,p), compact mass
box, and the uniform value error imply that every accumulation point of
regularized optimizing pairs (p,C_eps) lies in the closed true optimizer
graph. This transfers the uniform edge lower and upper bounds with fixed
slack. It does not assume convergence to the optimizer fiber at the same
varying mass vector or rank persistence of true optima.

At each regularized optimum, the derivative F_C=Laplacian/2 and KKT give
  0<=Laplacian<=I_H,
  (I_H-Laplacian)(C_eps-eps I_H)=0.
Physical weighted degrees are consequently at most one. Isoperimetry of
cell unions, positive masses, and the edge upper bound give conductance
h0 >= c/[4 Cbar sqrt(pi L_c)], where L_c=1+log(m/c). Removing weights below
m^-4 loses at most |S|m^-3 on a cut. For large m the retained conductance
is at least h0/2, and counting-measure Cheeger gives
  lambda_* >= c^2/[128 Cbar^2 pi L_c].

For a retained edge, its weight and pair separation give
pi_e=mu_e/phi(q_e)>=pi c^2/m^6. The two winning halfspaces give
q_e^2<=2 log(m/c)<=2L_c. Hence a(pi_e)^-1<=56 exp(3/2)L_c. Weighted Cauchy
in the two terms x_e and sqrt(a)(2h'_e-q_e x_e) yields
  4(h'_e)^2 <= (a^-1+q_e^2)[x_e^2+a(2h'_e-q_e x_e)^2].
Summing on retained edges and using lambda_* yields
  ||D_C h[A]||^2 <= A_m[-F_CC[A,A]],
  A_m=128 Cbar^2 pi[56 exp(3/2)+2] L_c^2/c^2.
All dependence on fixed c,Cbar is explicit. Since L_c is bounded by a
fixed-c multiple of 1+log m, this is the stated O_(c,Cbar)(log m)^2 bound.
The corrected draft uses this dependence instead of silently replacing
log(m/c) by log m inside a literal coefficient.

5. ACTIVE SETS AND THE SINGULAR LIMIT

For each fixed eps>0 and m, the covariance feasible set can be restricted
to a compact positive-definite region. Strict negative covariance Hessians
and continuity give a positive strong-concavity constant on that region;
mixed derivatives are bounded. The two optimizer inequalities imply that
C_eps(p) is Lipschitz along mass segments. These constants may diverge as
eps tends to zero and are not used as uniform constants.

Write g=2F_p-tr C. Along p(t)=p0+t u, the constrained envelope satisfies
V_eps'=g_p.u and has a Lipschitz first derivative for each fixed eps. At
almost every differentiability point, the two optimizer inequalities give
  E=-g_CC[C',C'] <= B=g_Cp[C',u].
This is an inequality, not an interior stationary equation. Since g_Cp=-2h_C,
the uniform mixed estimate gives B^2<=2A_m ||u||^2 E. Thus
0<=E<=B<=2A_m ||u||^2. Finally g_pp=-2Laplacian^-1<=0, so
  V_eps''=g_pp[u,u]+B<=2A_m||u||^2 almost everywhere.
The Lipschitz first derivative ensures that integration loses no singular
positive part. At uniform masses, uniqueness and permutation symmetry of
the regularized optimum give zero centered mass derivative. Integrating
from U to p gives V_eps(p)<=V_eps(U)+A_m||p-U||^2. Uniform convergence from
the explicit eps(m-1) value error passes this inequality to V, including
singular optimizing covariance matrices.

The cutoff eps0 may depend on m. This does not invalidate the uniform
large-m coefficient: for each fixed m the estimate holds for all sufficiently
small eps, and the same explicit A_m survives the eps->0 passage. The mass
box constants are fixed before choosing the eventual m threshold. Ambient
dimension is eliminated by the finite centroid/score representation.

7. WHAT THIS REVIEW DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE

The stronger mixed covariance/offset estimate and its constrained-envelope
transfer withstand this focused hostile reconstruction, after the harmless
fixed-c logarithm precision correction. No additional rank assumption,
optimizer-fiber continuity, uniform-in-eps strong-concavity constant, or
