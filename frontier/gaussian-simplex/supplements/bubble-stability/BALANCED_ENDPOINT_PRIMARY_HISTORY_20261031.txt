Conclusion within the inspected citation chain

An explicit FOUR-CELL balanced first-moment conjecture is already present
on 30 November 2012, in Heilman1211.7138v1. Its 2014 revision makes the
quantitative-stability consequence explicit. An explicit ALL-m>3 problem
with prescribed masses is stated in Heilman1901.03934v1, January2019;
its equal-mass subcase is exactly the balanced endpoint, up to a positive
factor and an additive constant. The earlier Khot--Naor antecedents in
that citation chain optimize over unrestricted masses. The all-m balanced
Standard Simplex noise conjecture dates at least to Isaksson--Mossel's
March2009 arXiv version, but is a full noise question, not a separately
stated first-moment endpoint in the inspected passage.

These are earliest VERIFIED explicit formulations in this bounded chain,
not a claim of exhaustive historical priority or absence of earlier work.

1. Explicit four-cell endpoint: November2012, not merely May2014

Steven Heilman, Euclidean Partitions that Optimize an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck
Quadratic Form, arXiv:1211.7138v1, submitted30November2012.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.7138v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.7138v1

Printedp7, equation(11), identifies
  (d/d rho)|_(rho=0) sum_i integral 1_Ai T_rho 1_Ai d gamma
     =sum_i |integral_Ai x d gamma|^2.
Definition2.2 on that page defines Delta_k^0 by simplex-valued assignments
with each integral exactly1/k. Section6, printedp32, Conjecture3, sets
k=4,n=3 and asks whether every indicator maximizer of psi_0 over this
balanced class is simplicial conical. The conclusion there does NOT
say regular simplicial; our regular classification is stronger.

Section6 contrasts this with the unrestricted class Delta_k, for which
the conical structural result is already known, and identifies the volume
constraint as the difficulty. This is an explicit endpoint question, not
an inference solely from the general Standard Simplex conjecture.

2. Explicit all-m prescribed-mass endpoint: January2019

Steven Heilman, Stable Gaussian Minimal Bubbles, arXiv:1901.03934v1.
Repository submission date13January2019.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.03934v1
https://arxiv.org/html/1901.03934v1#S1.SS4

Section1.4, Problem1.15, fixes m>3, positive a_i summing to1 and a vector
w from the translated regular partition in equation(1). It sets
  w^(i)=w/a_i
and asks to maximize, over partitions with gamma(Omega_i)=a_i,
  sqrt(pi/2) sum_i |integral_(Omega_i)(x-w^(i)) d gamma|^2.
Conjecture1.16 predicts translated regular simplicial cones when m-1 is
at most the ambient dimension. This is an all-m formulation and includes
arbitrary prescribed unequal masses, which our balanced theorem does not
claim to settle.

Exact algebraic comparison: put b_i=integral_(Omega_i)x d gamma.
Since integral_(Omega_i)w^(i)d gamma=w and sum_i b_i=0, its objective is
  sqrt(pi/2)[sum_i |b_i|^2+m|w|^2].
Thus the shift changes only a constant for the prescribed mass problem.
For a_i=1/m one can choose w=0; the objective is precisely sqrt(pi/2)
times our balanced first-moment objective. This cancellation is an
independent calculation, not an additional claim attributed to the paper.

The opening of section1.4 expressly connects this problem to rho tending
to0. The paper also uses it as a condition for many-cell Gaussian-bubble
stability and associated weak small-noise bounds. Those are not, merely
from this citation, full noise optimality or the entire unequal-mass
Conjecture1.16. The other agent is checking the exact published stability
theorem separately.

3. Khot--Naor antecedents: unrestricted masses

Khot--Naor, Approximate kernel clustering, arXiv:0807.4626,
submitted29July2008, revised9December2008; Mathematika55(2009),129--165.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4626
https://cs.nyu.edu/~khot/papers/kernel.pdf
The introduction's propeller-problem paragraph and Theorem1.2, printedp5
of the author PDF, maximize sum_i |z_i|^2 over all measurable partitions
of R^(k-1). The maximizing conical reduction can have only m<=k active
cells. The proposed optimizer uses three120-degree sectors and permits
empty extra cells. It is not an equal-mass k-cell conjecture. Its stated
consequences concern kernel-clustering approximation thresholds under UGC.

Khot--Naor, Sharp kernel clustering algorithms and their associated
Grothendieck inequalities, arXiv:0906.4816v1,25June2009;
Random Structures & Algorithms42(2013),269--300.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.4816v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.4816v1
The abstract and opening definition of C(B) maximize
  sum_ij b_ij <z_i,z_j>
over all measurable k-partitions, without prescribed cell probabilities.
This is the other cited variant in Heilman's section1.4. It does not
justify attributing the balanced all-m endpoint formulation to2008/2009.

4. Earlier all-m noise conjecture and its consequence distinction

Isaksson--Mossel, New Maximally Stable Gaussian Partitions with Discrete
Applications, arXiv:0903.3362v1,19March2009.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.3362v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.3362v1
Conjecture1.4, printedp3, gives the regular-simplex balanced maximum for
all positive correlations and the unrestricted-mass minimum for negative
correlations, for3<=q<=n+1. Section1.2 and Theorems1.7--1.8 connect this
to Plurality is Stablest. Theorem1.10 discusses the full conjecture's
MAX-q-CUT approximation consequence under UGC, involving an infimum over
the full admissible correlation interval. A near-zero theorem alone
does not supply that full-range hardness conclusion.

Differentiating the positive-correlation inequality at independence would
imply the balanced first-moment bound. That is a mathematical implication;
the inspected source passage states the noise conjecture, rather than a
standalone first-moment conjecture. The current PDF's internal date line
appears inconsistent with its arXiv stamp, so the date above uses version
metadata. No attempt was made to reconstruct unpublished earlier drafts.
