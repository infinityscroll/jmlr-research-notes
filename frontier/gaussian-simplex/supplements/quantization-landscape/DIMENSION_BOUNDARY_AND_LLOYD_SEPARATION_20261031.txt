A. A NONGLOBAL STRICT LOCAL MINIMUM BELOW THE DIMENSION THRESHOLD
Take standard Gaussian data in R, three labeled centers, and prescribed
masses p=(1/2,1/4,1/4). Thus N=1<m-1=2. Use the same capacity-constrained
population distortion as in the landscape theorem. Put
  q=Phi^(-1)(3/4), a=phi(0), b=phi(q).
Consider the two center configurations, in label order,
  C=(0,-4b,4b),
  E=(-2a,4(a-b),4b).                                  (A1)
Both have weighted mean0. The first has mass ordering(1/4,1/2,1/4)
from left to right; the second has ordering(1/2,1/4,1/4).

For strictly ordered one-dimensional centers, the optimal prescribed-
mass assignment consists of successive Gaussian quantile intervals in
that same order. This follows by the elementary exchange argument:
if x<y are assigned in reversed center order, swapping equal amounts
increases the linear score by (a_j-a_i)(y-x)>0. Equivalently it is the
one-dimensional monotone transport coupling. Once a center-order chamber
is fixed, these intervals and their masses are fixed, independently of
the distances between the centers. The distortion in that open chamber
is therefore a strictly convex quadratic with Hessian2D_p. Its unique
stationary point is the vector of the intervals' conditional means.
Those means are strictly increasing, so it lies inside the chamber and
is a strict local minimum of the full center objective.

For the central-large-cell order, the intervals are
  (-infinity,-q), (-q,q), (q,infinity),
with conditional means -4b,0,4b. For the edge-large-cell order they are
  (-infinity,0), (0,q), (q,infinity),
with conditional means -2a,4(a-b),4b. Thus both configurations in(A1)
are strict local minima, with center-coordinate Hessian
  2D_p=diag(1,1/2,1/2).
The within-interval formula gives distortion=1 minus explained variance.
Their explained variances are
  V_C=8b^2,
  V_E=6a^2-8ab+8b^2.
Consequently
  distortion(E)-distortion(C)=2a(4b-3a).              (A2)
The following entirely rational bounds certify this is positive.

First a>3/8. For example
  pi=4 integral_0^1 (1+x^2)^(-1)dx
       <4(1-1/3+1/5)=52/15<32/9,
using1/(1+x^2)<1-x^2+x^4 for0<x<=1. Hence
  a=(2pi)^(-1/2)>3/8.
Since exp(-t^2/2)>=1-t^2/2,
  Phi(3/4)>=1/2+a[3/4-(3/4)^3/6]
           >1/2+(3/8)(87/128)>3/4.
Therefore0<q<3/4 and b/a=exp(-q^2/2)>exp(-9/32).
For x=9/32, the bound n!>=2*3^(n-2) for n>=2 gives
  exp(x)<=1+x+(x^2/2)/(1-x/3)
         =2459/1856<4/3.
Thus b/a>1856/2459>3/4. In fact(A2) obeys the explicit gap
  distortion(E)-distortion(C)
       >2*(9/64)*(47/2459)=423/78688>0.                (A3)
The exact constants are intentionally crude; no numerical quantile
estimate is used. This also checks that4(a-b)<4b, although strict
ordering already follows from the interval conditional means.

Hence E is a genuine nonglobal strict local minimum for prescribed-mass
population Gaussian quantization. The condition N>=m-1 in the general
landscape theorem cannot simply be dropped. This one example does not
assert that the threshold is sharp for every m,N or every mass vector.
It concerns unequal prescribed masses, not a balanced equal-mass
counterexample to the Gaussian simplex endpoint.

B. ONE LLOYD UPDATE HAS UNIFORM SEPARATION AND A UNIFORM NORM BOUND
Let a_1,...,a_m be pairwise distinct Gaussian sites in R^N. Let their
optimal Laguerre cells E_i have positive prescribed masses p_i. Write
  c_i=(1/p_i)integral_(E_i)x dgamma_N(x)
for the updated centers. For every pair i!=j,
  |c_i-c_j| >=sqrt(2pi)(p_i+p_j)/2.                    (B1)
This estimate is dimension independent and does not depend on the old
center separation or the offsets.

Proof. Let u=(a_i-a_j)/|a_i-a_j|. The Laguerre inequalities provide a
threshold t with E_i contained in {u.x>=t} and E_j contained in {u.x<=t}.
The one-dimensional Gaussian density is at most M=1/sqrt(2pi). For s>=0,
  gamma(E_i intersect {u.x-t>s})>=max(p_i-Ms,0).
Integrating this lower bound in s gives
  integral_(E_i)(u.x-t)d gamma>=p_i^2/(2M).
Similarly integral_(E_j)(t-u.x)d gamma>=p_j^2/(2M).
After division by the respective cell masses,
  u.(c_i-c_j)>= (p_i+p_j)/(2M),
which proves(B1). The argument uses only pairwise separating hyperplanes
and the marginal Gaussian density; no boundary regularity is needed.

Every update also satisfies
  |c_i|<=phi(Phi^(-1)(p_i))/p_i.                       (B2)
Indeed, in the direction c_i/|c_i|, among all sets of Gaussian mass p_i
the largest first moment is attained by an upper halfspace, whose first
moment is phi(Phi^(-1)(p_i)). The zero-vector case is immediate.
Finally sum_i p_i c_i=E X=0.

Thus all updates starting from distinct sites lie in a fixed compact
set of centered, pairwise-separated configurations. The distinct-site
hypothesis matters: an arbitrary tie assignment at coincident old sites
need not be separated by any hyperplane, so(B1) is not asserted for such
an initialization. These one-step bounds do not establish convergence
of Lloyd iterates, avoidance of saddle sets, or convergence to a global
optimum from generic or all initial conditions.
