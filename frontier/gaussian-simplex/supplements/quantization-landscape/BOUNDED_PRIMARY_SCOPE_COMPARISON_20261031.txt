PRESCRIBED-MASS GAUSSIAN QUANTIZATION: BOUNDED PRIMARY COMPARISON
31 October 2026. Root. This is a scope check, not exhaustive novelty clearance.

The new note studies W2^2(N(0,I_N), sum_i p_i delta_(a_i)) as a function
of the centers, with fixed positive p and N>=m-1. Its assignment may depend
on the realized Gaussian vector. It is derived from the separately audited
covariance-concavity theorem. No prior theorem is claimed false here.

1. Luo and Mixon, BalLOT: Balanced k-means clustering with optimal transport
arXiv:2512.05926v1, 5 December 2025.
https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05926v1
Primary passages checked: Theorem 6, Section 2 discussion, Section 3.2.

Theorem 6 proves absence of nonglobal local minima for E f(F,mu), with
F a fixed finite coupling matrix, under a balanced mixture model with
distinct planted means. Section 3.2 takes expectation with this F fixed;
the result is transport between planted means plus constant noise variance.
That objective differs from our population transport, where the assignment
is chosen as a function of the observation. The theorem therefore does
not directly establish the present result. Conversely our result does not
establish their finite-sample or mixture-model guarantees.

A direct illustration of the distinction: for the symmetric one-dimensional
mixture of N(-a,1) and N(a,1), a>0, the two equally weighted optimal
population centers are +/-E|a+Z|, where
  E|a+Z|=2phi(a)+a(2Phi(a)-1)>a.
The fixed-coupling expected-cost reduction instead has centers +/-a.
The strict inequality follows from phi(a)>a[1-Phi(a)], by integrating
x phi(x)>a phi(x) over x>a. This illustrates different objectives; it is
not a counterexample to the literal Theorem 6.

2. Mulgund, Stochastic Domination of Gaussian Maxima by the Regular Simplex
arXiv:2609.28452v2, revised 27 September 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28452
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28452v2
Primary passages checked: abstract and Theorem 1.1.

The stated comparison fixes every coordinate variance to one and compares
the distribution of the unconstrained maximum with the regular-simplex
maximum. It does not state prescribed-label-mass covariance concavity,
nor a Gaussian quantization landscape theorem. Our covariance domain and
optimized assignment offsets are different. This limited comparison is
not an independent audit of that paper or a proof that no deeper
implication between the results exists.

3. Older terminology
The bounded search also used 'principal points', 'self-consistent points',
'optimal Gaussian quantization', and 'prescribed probabilities'. Ordinary
principal-point objectives optimize nearest-center assignments and their
resulting masses; that is not our fixed-mass objective. Search snippets
and absence of matching hits are not evidence of priority. A complete
historical check of the prescribed-mass landscape remains outstanding.

The claim supported by this file is the literal scope distinction above.
No claim of a newly resolved named clustering conjecture, exhaustive
literature exclusion, or external expert validation is made.
