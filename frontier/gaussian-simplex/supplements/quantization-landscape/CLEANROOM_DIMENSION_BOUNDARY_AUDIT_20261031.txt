quantile intervals, by the equal-mass exchange argument. Within
each open center-order chamber the distortion is a quadratic with

conditional means and lies strictly inside that chamber.

For every distinct pair of old sites, the corresponding Laguerre
cells lie on opposite sides of their pairwise score hyperplane,
whether or not they share a facet. The marginal density bound
M=1/sqrt(2pi) makes the cell's residual tail at least(p_i-Ms)_+.
Layer-cake integration gives a one-sided first moment pi^2/(2M).
Dividing by cell masses and adding the two sides proves exactly
|c_i-c_j|>=sqrt(2pi)(p_i+p_j)/2.

The updated-center norm bound is the one-dimensional halfspace
first-moment bound in the direction of c_i; weighted centering
follows from summing the cell integrals. For each fixed N and p,
these inequalities indeed place all updates from distinct sites
in a fixed compact centered set with positive pair separation.
No tie rule at coincident old sites is covered, and no convergence
or saddle-avoidance claim follows from these one-step estimates.
