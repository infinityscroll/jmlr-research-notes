1. A constructive replacement for qualitative localization
Use all notation of the base proof. For a competitive pair at r<=1, put
F=(f+h)/2, p=E F, u=half||p-U||_1. The base Hermite estimates imply
  u<=sqrt(3m)/2 sqrt(r), W(F)>=<b(f),b(h)> >= w-2r.

Globally balance F as follows. For each donor i with p_i>1/m, remove the
fraction (p_i-1/m)/p_i of F_i(x). Distribute the total removed function to
recipient labels proportionally to their deficits 1/m-p_j. This is a
simplex-valued balanced assignment F0 and
  sum_i||F0_i-F_i||_1=2u.
If u=0 set F0=F. The signs of the changes are fixed by the donor/recipient
label sets. Since each change has absolute value at most1, Bessel's
inequality for the Gaussian coordinate functions gives
  e^2:=||b(F0)-b(F)||_F^2
     <=sum_i||F0_i-F_i||_2^2<=2u,
  e<=(3m)^(1/4)r^(1/4).
This bound has no unnecessary factor of dimension.

Both centroid matrices have norm at most1 by Parseval. Consequently
  Delta0=w-W(F0)<=2r+2e.
Choose c nearest in the regular centroid orbit to b(F), and put
q=||b(F)-c||_F^2. H_m and orbit-distance Lipschitz continuity give
  sqrt(q)<=sqrt(K Delta0)+e,
  q<=4Kr+4Ke+2e^2.

Let J=w-<c,b(F)> be the regular linear assignment cost. Its pointwise
nonnegativity and the exact identity 2J=w-W(F)+q do not need balance.
Writing a=|c_i-c_j| and beta=binom(m,2)sqrt(2/pi)/a, the usual Gaussian
score-gap union bound and layer cake give
  d(F,g_c)^2<=2beta J
     <=beta[(4K+2)r+4Ke+2e^2]
     <=C_loc r^(1/4),
  C_loc=beta[(4K+2)+4K(3m)^(1/4)+2sqrt(3m)].
Thus the explicit sufficient cutoff for the anchor condition is
  r<=[c_A^2/(576 C_loc)]^4.                              (L)
This holds in the ORIGINAL nearest frame and covers all competitive
free-common-mass pairs in fixed d=m-1. It replaces section1's qualitative
cutoff in the base proof; every other argument is unchanged.
