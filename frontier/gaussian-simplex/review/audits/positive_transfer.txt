Verdict. No substantive gap found. The conditional theorem follows with the
stated constants and quantifiers. H_m is the remaining endpoint premise;
this audit establishes neither H_m for five or more cells nor an unconditional

The original source and frozen four-cell packages were not changed.

1. Quantifiers and same-frame L1 estimate
H_m applies to all balanced relaxed assignments in dimension m-1. Since K>0,
its nonnegative left side implies Delta>=0. Orthogonal compactness supplies
a nearest centroid matrix c. Its winning-score cells are well-defined up to
null hyperplanes. Direct expansion gives 2J=Delta+q. The two-largest score
gap is bounded by a union over all unordered Gaussian score differences;
each has standard deviation a. Thus P(Gamma<=t)<=beta*t, with the stated
beta. For fractional mismatch p in [0,1], Tonelli gives
 E[p Gamma]=integral E[p 1_{Gamma>t}]dt >= integral(d0-beta*t)_+dt.
There is no separate L1 alignment and no assumption that f is an indicator.
Equality Delta=0 forces J=q=0 and then f=g almost everywhere.

2. Saturation and dimension-dependent weighted constants
The independent normal score representation remains valid in H by centering
m independent normals; centering does not change their maximizer. The gap
between labels i,j is the integral of positive H_delta above the maximum of
the other m-2 scores. Lowering those means can only increase the gap. The
event all other centered normal variables are <=0 has probability 2^{-(m-2)}.
Integration over u in [0,1] then gives the exact stated c_m. The argument
for delta>=1 uses monotonicity of the gap when the j mean decreases.

For one unit-normal plane write x=u e+z. On |u|<=1, the triangular positive
part integrates to phi(0)*exp(1/4)*E exp(|z|^2/4)/(lambda*rho), and that
Gaussian expectation is 2^{(d-1)/2}. On the complement, the one-plane
saturated weight is >=rho and the square inequality gives 2^{d/2}/(4lambda
rho). The maximum over planes is at most their sum. This proves B_d,
including the factor P, without a hidden bounded-support restriction.

D >= (c_m/2) sum_i integral |h_i|w_rho; therefore summing the optimized
one-plane estimates yields M2=8 B_d/c_m. The scalar mismatch and tail mass
need factor sqrt(m)/2; the vector centroid tail needs no such factor. The
choice A2=max(1,m/4) M2 covers both exactly. The function
(1+r)exp(-r^2/8) decreases for r>=2, giving the claimed uniform tail bound.

3. General Mehler estimate
Let r=|rho|<=1/16 and L=|x|^2+|y|^2. The exact log derivatives obey
 |ell'| <= d*r/(1-r^2)+L/[2(1-r)^2],
 |ell''| <= d*(1+r^2)/(1-r^2)^2+L/(1-r)^3.
The four coefficients at r=1/16 are <1, <1, <2, <2 respectively, so the
source's d+L and 2d+2L bounds hold uniformly. The kernel exponential is
bounded by exp(L/34), since the quadratic exponent is at most
r*L/[2(1+r)]. Its determinant prefactor is certainly <=2^d.
The gap between 1/8 and 1/34 is t0=13/136. Bounding L*exp(-t0 L) by
1/(2t0) and L^2*exp(-t0 L) by 1/t0^2 proves the displayed C_d after the
Taylor factor 1/2. Integrability follows from Gaussian exponential moments.

R_quad>=0 is used only for positive rho, as required by the theorem;
the absolute kernel estimate alone also holds for negative rho. The exact
comparison keeps -2D separate from the quadratic perturbation remainder.
No invalid bound on the total difference of higher-chaos energies is used.

4. Facet geometry and fractional balance repair for every m>=3
The anchor v_i+v_j has squared norm 2-4/m. The i and j scores tie, and each
exceeds any other score by exactly 1. Two distinct unordered anchors are
distance sqrt(2) when sharing one label and distance 2 otherwise. The patch
displacement has norm <=sqrt(2)/8. This makes all distinct patches disjoint,
puts them in B2, and reduces any third-label score gap by at most 1/4.
The two orientations at the same anchor are disjoint half-slabs.

The Gaussian lower density on B2 and the tangential ball volume give c_A
exactly as stated. A donor patch loses at most d0 mass compared with its
reference indicator. With tau=(d0+2u)/c_A its remaining donor mass is >=2u,
and d0<=c_A/24 ensures tau<=1/8. Each required transfer is <=u. Multiplying
the donor density by the needed mass divided by its available integral is
an explicit admissible fractional transfer. Disjoint supports avoid reuse.

The repair cost is at most a*tau*u <=3d0*u/c_A because a<=1. This implies
C_J=3A2/c_A. For centroid error the tail contributes epsilon_R*A*z and
the transfers contribute <=2sqrt(2)u (or the coarser 3sqrt(2)u used in the
four-cell proof), so e<=6epsilon_R*A*z is safe. These estimates do not
require the repaired centroids to choose the same nearest rotation.

5. Original-frame coercivity and final strictness
Distance to the orthogonal orbit is one-Lipschitz. Both that distance and
the distance to the original c are >=(sqrt(q)-e)_+. Applying H_m to the
balanced f' proves the displayed lower bound for 2J'. The stated Young
parameter gives coefficient 2-1/(K+1), not merely 2. This is the strict
margin needed for the argument.

The Gaussian convolution Hessian estimate is dimension-free: for a unit u,
u^T Hess H_i(y)u=E[((u.Z)^2-1)g_i(y+Z)], whose absolute value is <=sqrt(2)
by Cauchy--Schwarz. Conicality and the t-rho estimate therefore give the
gradient remainder. Reflection in the i,j bisector fixes all other vertices
and swaps those two; the pair remainder is exactly zero on that plane.
The projected point and intervening segment remain in B_R. This proves the
relative compact-potential bound with B_R=2(1+3R)/a.

The order of choices is valid: m,K determine all fixed constants, then a
finite R makes E_R<=sigma/4, then a positive rho_m satisfies all four upper
bounds. Neither choice depends on a competitor or ambient dimension.
Competitiveness gives Delta <=(1-1/m)rho by Hermite positivity and Parseval,
including relaxed f. The L1 estimate then supplies the exact repair capacity.

The worst final coefficient is
 -2+(2-sigma)/(1-sigma/16)+sigma/2.
Adding sigma/4 and putting over its positive denominator leaves numerator
 -sigma/8-3sigma^2/64 <0.
Thus the required -sigma*D/4 follows with strict slack for all K>=1. If
f differs from g on positive measure, the saturated score inequality gives
D>0, excluding competitiveness. No sequence-dependent tail condition occurs.

6. Ambient dimensions, relaxed maxima and discrete transfer
In any fixed finite dimension, the feasible set is bounded, closed and
convex in the finite product of L2 spaces, hence weakly compact. T_rho is
compact and positive definite for 0<rho<1, so its quadratic form is weakly
continuous and a maximum exists. On a positive-measure fractional overlap
of two labels, choose two equal-measure pieces and a sufficiently small
opposite-pair perturbation. Both signs preserve mass and feasibility. Their
average stability exceeds the original by strict positivity of T_rho.
Consequently every maximizing relaxed assignment is an indicator partition.

Primary dimension reduction checked again at its exact version:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.05583v2
Heilman--Tarter, Theorem 1.9, printed page 7. It applies to every maximizing
positive-mass partition in ambient N>=m-1, and reduces it after rotation and
null changes to m-1 coordinates. Thus value and equality transfer both hold.
For N<m-1, embed directly into R^{m-1}; equality would force the full-rank
regular centroid matrix, impossible for an N-dimensional assignment.

Primary discrete transfer checked at:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.3362v3
Isaksson--Mossel, Theorem 7.1 and Lemma 7.3, printed pages 23--24. The former
preserves the expectation vector and approximates stability at the same
fixed rho; the latter identifies the plurality limit. This yields the
usual additive-epsilon, low-influence corollary, with thresholds depending
on m,rho,epsilon. No uniform threshold as rho tends to zero is supplied.
