1. Duality, geometry and regularity

E max_i(y_i.X+h_i) >= max_i h_i. For positive p, max h-p.h is at least
p_min times the offset range. After removing common constants, this is
coercive. Each distinct pair has a nonzero score difference, so its Gaussian
tie set is null when C is positive definite. The mass derivative therefore
exists, and optimal offsets supply exactly the prescribed probabilities.
The corresponding deterministic winning map attains the dual objective.

For d+1 affinely independent sites, h_i=c-y_i.a is an isomorphism modulo
constants. The translated fan has every simplex face as a winner stratum.
All the relative interiors have positive intrinsic Gaussian mass, including
the apex with its zero-dimensional counting measure. Its normal cones are
simplicial in their own affine spans; no generic-position assumption on a
or the masses is required.

Writing the objective using the FIXED support function and a translated
Gaussian gives locally smooth dependence on a and C. Gaussian derivatives
have polynomial factors and the support function has linear growth. The
offset Hessian is a positive complete-graph Laplacian, hence invertible
modulo constants. Thus the optimized Hessian exists smoothly. This supplies
regularity without assuming smooth motion of multiple junctions.

Both gluing inequalities are valid for arbitrary couplings, not only for
transport maps. A coupling of a singular Gaussian with Y can be lifted to

Adding epsilon I then extends simplex concavity to the closed PSD cone.

The lift (y_i,epsilon e_i) is affinely independent for any epsilon>0.
With covariance diag(C,0), the additional coordinates contribute exactly

couplings of the projected Gaussian can be lifted by its conditional law.
The resulting covariance is a LINEAR restriction of diag(C,0), not a
nonlinear square-root parametrization. Thus concavity pulls back in C.
