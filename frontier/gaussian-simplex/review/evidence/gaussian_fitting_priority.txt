1. Wang--Wei, Relative Wasserstein Angle and the problem of W2-Nearest
Gaussian Distribution, arXiv2601.22355v2 (latest version checked).
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.22355v2
Exact locators: section3.2, section3.4 equation(3) and the reconstruction
paragraph immediately before Remarks; sections2.2 and3.1 for cone geometry.
The paper fixes the best mean to the target mean, normalizes shape to trace1,
and reconstructs covariance as r^2 cos^2(theta*) Sigma*. Since for a
trace-one shape F_nu(Q)=r cos(theta_Q), that is exactly C*=F_nu(Q)^2Q.
Those reductions must not be claimed new. The inspected convex-cone language
concerns Wasserstein metric geometry; the inspected higher-dimensional
algorithm and its eigencoordinates do not state ordinary-C convexity.
No full-paper theorem-exclusion claim is inferred merely from text search.
