\section{Frozen sources and proof provenance}\label{app:sources}

The authoritative internal theorem statement is \cite{unconditional}, with the direct competitive proof \cite{direct}. The endpoint and remainder equality facts are proved explicitly from covariance concavity in Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint}, following \cite{endpoint}; the older endpoint references in Section~10 of \cite{covariance} are not used. The new proofs in this manuscript correspond to \cite{hazards,radial,assembly}; the radial premise left conditional in the last of these is proved here in Theorem~\ref{thm:radial}.
