The compactness statements in Section2 have the following exact meaning.
If eps_n->0, p_n belongs to the fixed compact mass box, and C_n is a
regularized optimizer, every convergent subsequence (p_n,C_n)->(p,C)
has C in Argmax g(.,p). Equivalently the distance of (p_n,C_n) to the
CLOSED GRAPH {(p,C):C optimizes g(.,p)} tends to0 uniformly on the compact
mass box and bounded covariance set.

This does NOT assert that dist(C_n,Argmax g(.,p_n))->0 for the optimizer
fiber at the varying p_n. That stronger lower-hemicontinuity assertion
can fail for parameterized optimization with nonunique limiting maxima,
and is neither proved nor needed here.

The use of compactness is only to transfer UNIFORM edge bounds valid for
EVERY true optimizing pair (p,C) in the whole mass box. If a fixed-slack
edge bound failed along regularized optimizers, a compact subsequence
would converge to a true optimizer at its limiting mass p, contradicting
that uniform bound there. Edge lengths are continuous in C. Thus the
pair separation and upper-length transfer in Section3 of12dc is valid
with the graph interpretation, as are all subsequent pruned-graph and
semiconcavity conclusions. No fiber continuity or rank persistence is
required anywhere in the argument.
