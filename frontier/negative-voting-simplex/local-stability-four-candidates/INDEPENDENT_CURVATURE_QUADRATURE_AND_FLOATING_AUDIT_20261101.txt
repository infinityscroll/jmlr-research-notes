1. ACTUAL NORMAL DERIVATIVE AND LOG-CURVATURE

Let rho=-1/3, r=1/3, sigma^2=8/9. Let n be the unit normal from C_i to C_j,
and x belong to their tie plane n.x=0. Reflection in this plane swaps the
two tetrahedral cells. Differentiating the conditional Gaussian and then
reflecting the C_j integral gives exactly

 d(x) = partial_n T_rho(1_Ci-1_Cj)(x)
      = (2r/sigma^2) int_Ci (-n.y) phi_sigma(y-rho*x) dy.        (1)

Here C_i lies in n.y<=0. The integrand is jointly log-concave: the cone is
convex, the logarithm of its nonnegative linear weight is concave, and the
Gaussian has a negative squared affine norm. The Prékopa marginal theorem
therefore proves concavity of log d on the tie plane. Boundary zeros cause
no difficulty for nonnegative log-concave functions. The integral is strictly
positive and smooth at every finite x.

For an independent check of the lower curvature bound, normalize the positive
measure in (1) to a probability nu_x. Differentiation under its Gaussian
integral yields, in tie-plane directions,

 Hess log d = -rho^2/sigma^2 I + rho^2/sigma^4 Cov_nu_x(Y)
            = -I/8 + (9/64) Cov_nu_x(Y).                     (2)

The covariance is positive semidefinite. Thus

                    -I/8 <= Hess log d <= 0.               (3)

Equation (1), and hence equality with the actual normal derivative, is only
asserted on the tie plane. Extending its positive integral off the plane does
not identify that extension with the actual normal derivative there.

These formulas independently verify the mechanism in
LOG_DIAGONAL_CURVATURE_AND_EXECUTABLE_QUADRATURE_20261101.txt, lines17-42.
An alternative upper-bound proof uses the Brascamp-Lieb covariance inequality
for the sigma^-2-strongly log-concave density in (1); it is unnecessary here.

2. THE CENTER-TANGENT QUADRATURE LEMMA

Use physical coordinates x=(q,w,-w) on one facet. Its half-sector is
q>=w>=0. Reflection w->-w supplies the other half. Intrinsic area is
sqrt(2) dq dw. Accordingly the full-facet prefactor is exactly

       2sqrt(2)/(2pi)^(3/2) = pi^(-3/2).                    (4)

The retained moments are m_p,n=int q^p z^n dmu, where z=sqrt(2)w,
n is even, p+n<=60, and dmu=exp(-|x|^2/8)d lambda/d(x).
There are 961 such moments. Their half-sector coordinate density is

 pi^(-3/2) q^p (sqrt(2)w)^n exp[-5(q^2+2w^2)/8]/d(q,w).

Let a cell have side h=1/8 and bounding-box center c. A diagonal triangular
cell uses the same center, which lies on its boundary but still on the full
tie plane, where (3) holds. Write xi=q-qc, eta=w-wc and g=log d. Then

 g(c)+g_q(c)xi+g_w(c)eta-(xi^2+2eta^2)/16
       <= g(x) <= g(c)+g_q(c)xi+g_w(c)eta.                  (5)

Since |xi|,|eta|<=h/2, replacing g by its tangent makes the reciprocal-weight
model a pointwise lower bound, and actual/model lies in [1,exp(E)], where

                       E=3h^2/64=3/4096.                 (6)

Its exponential factor separates into exp(f_q)exp(f_w), with

 f_q=-(5qc/4+g_q)xi-5xi^2/8,
 f_w=-(5wc/2+g_w)eta-5eta^2/4.                             (7)

For U=int d d lambda, replace (7) by
 f_q=-(qc-g_q)xi-xi^2/2, f_w=-(2wc-g_w)eta-eta^2.
Here actual/model lies in [exp(-E),1].
For V=int d^-1 d lambda, use plus g instead of minus g in those coefficients;
actual/model lies in [1,exp(E)]. These one-sided directions matter.

A direct derivative bound verifies a strict uniform |f|<3. The positive
boundary-Laplace representation gives
 grad log d=-x/8-(3/8) E(Y). In its two-dimensional cone measure, radial
integration by parts gives
 (9/8) E|Y|^2=2-(3/8)x.E(Y).
Therefore E|Y|<=(s+sqrt(s^2+64))/6, and
 |grad log d|<=(3s+sqrt(s^2+64))/16<=s/4+1/2.
On 0<=w<=q<=14, s<=14sqrt(3), so |g_q|<7 and |g_w|<10.
The largest coefficient in (7) has magnitude <45. Thus |f_w| is at most
45/16+5/1024<2.818; all other factors have smaller bounds. This also leaves
a substantial margin for conversion and arithmetic error.

Let P_18(t)=sum_{j=0}^18 t^j/j!. On |t|<=3,

 |P_18(t)/exp(t)-1| <= eta_18=exp(6)*3^19/19!
                              <3.854576e-6.               (8)

In particular P_18 is positive. The product of the two polynomial factors
lies between (1-eta_18)^2 and (1+eta_18)^2 times the exponential model.
The two errors must both be included; a single-factor allowance is wrong.

For ANY nonnegative monomial in the retained list, suppose the polynomial
quadrature Q is evaluated with relative error at most f=10^-6, and an
absolute allowance a=2^-600. Then the actual moment lies between

 (Q-a)/[(1+f)(1+eta_18)^2],
 exp(E)(Q+a)/[(1-f)(1-eta_18)^2].                          (9)

For U the lower expression receives exp(-E), while its upper expression
omits exp(E). For V the factors in (9) apply. Formula (9) is valid because
all monomials, quadrature weights, and polynomial factors are nonnegative;
there is no moment-wise cancellation.

3. EXACT RECTANGLE AND TRIANGLE DEGREES

The domain 0<=w<=q<=14 consists of 6216 full h-squares and 112 diagonal
triangles. A degree18 exponential polynomial of a quadratic has degree36.
On a rectangle each coordinate degree is at most60+36=96, so Gauss49 in
each coordinate is exact (degree97). Separation reduces the work to one-
dimensional moment sums and their outer products.

For the diagonal triangle use
        q=a+h*u, w=a+h*u*v, 0<=u,v<=1,
with Jacobian h^2*u. The q-polynomial factor has u-degree36, while the
w-polynomial factor has degrees36 in both u and v. Including a monomial
of total degree<=60 and the Jacobian gives
                    degree_u<=133, degree_v<=96.
Gauss67 in u and Gauss49 in v are therefore exact. No comparison of two
quadrature orders is being substituted for an error theorem.

4. ACTUAL ARB FORMULA AND CODE CHECK

arb_facet_diagonal.py lines19-37 uses independent unit-variance utility
variables with means
 (-q/(4sqrt2),-q/(4sqrt2),(q/2-w)/(2sqrt2),(q/2+w)/(2sqrt2)).
These equal rho*y_i.x/sigma for the tetrahedral sites y_i=(+,+,+)/2 etc.
For a tied pair of utilities the common conditional value has variance1/2.
Its two comparisons with the other utilities have variance3/2 and
correlation1/3. Hence the pair weight is exactly

 W_ij=exp[-(mu_i-mu_j)^2/4]/(2sqrt(pi))
       *Phi_2(sqrt(2/3)(avg-mu_k),sqrt(2/3)(avg-mu_l);1/3).

On the selected tie plane, differentiation along the physical unit normal
gives d=(2W_12+W_13+W_14)/2. This agrees with line35, including the factor
1/2. The two derivatives in lines30-36 are ordinary chain-rule derivatives
of this expression. The bivariate CDF uses the integral of its correlation
derivative from0 to1/3 plus the product of univariate CDFs (lines10-15).
Arb encloses this integral. The code subsequently tests positivity and
enclosure widths, so a non-finite or overly broad integral does not silently
become a certificate.

The code maps Gauss nodes to [0,1] in Arb before converting to binary64.
This avoids the relative-error loss from subtracting nearly equal doubles
at a node close to zero. Each node and weight is compared with its Arb
enclosure, relative discrepancy <2^-48. Center constants are tested to
relative discrepancy <10^-12 and log-gradients to absolute discrepancy
<10^-12. The inspected successor also forms powers of2 from exact integer
shifts, and checks coefficient conversions and the binary64 format.

5. EXPLICIT FLOATING ERROR AUDIT

This is the part that cannot be justified merely by observing convergence.
The following conservative estimates cover the actual operations.

(a) Node and input errors. Physical q,w use nonnegative additions and
multiplications of the unit nodes and exact dyadic a,h. Their relative
perturbations are <2^-45. Thus every retained monomial changes relatively
by <2e-12. The centered coordinates can cancel, but their ABSOLUTE errors
are <2^-47. Using |g_q|<7, |g_w|<10, q,w<=14 and the tested 10^-12 gradient
uncertainty, every computed polynomial argument differs from its exact
argument by <10^-11. This bound includes arithmetic in its short quadratic
formula. Centers and h themselves are exact dyadic numbers.

(b) Horner conditioning. For |t|<=3, both
 sum |t|^j/j! / P_18(t) and |P_18'(t)|/P_18(t) are <404.
Indeed the numerator is <=exp(3), the denominator >=(1-eta_18)exp(-3),
and exp(6)/(1-eta_18)<404. Thus negative-argument cancellation is explicitly
covered. Two argument perturbations contribute less than 810*10^-11
relatively, allowing for products and a negligible second-order term.

(c) Positive arithmetic after the polynomial values. Integer powers are
computed by repeated multiplication. Rectangle moment sums are positive
dot products followed by a positive outer product. Triangle inner sums,
matrix products, and cell accumulation are also positive. Each dot product
has length<=67, integer powers have length<=60, and there are6328 cell
contributions. Counting product branches additively and sum branches by
their longest path gives error depth<20000. The looser allowance of
2,000,000 is therefore ample, including the short polynomial evaluation
paths. With unit roundoff u=2^-53 and gamma_n=nu/(1-nu), the deliberately
overcounted total bound

             404 gamma_2000000 + 1000*10^-11 + 10^-10
                        <9.981e-8 <10^-6                 (10)

covers operation rounding, both argument perturbations, center constants,
node/weight perturbations, monomial perturbations, and coefficient rounding.
The small data terms not multiplied by404 fit well inside 10^-10. The use
of404 on the entire operation allowance overcounts the actual Horner cost.
Fused multiply-add or another order of positive summation cannot worsen
this gross bound. No floating exp, log, trigonometric function, or fractional
power is used in the retained numerical integration stage.

(d) Underflow. The first diagonal triangle can contain w so small that
w^60 underflows. A relative-only proof would therefore be incomplete.
The inspected successor explicitly inserts an absolute 2^-600 allowance
in every retained moment. Here is a conservative independent proof that
this suffices. There are fewer than2^34 elementary operations in the entire
run. Even with flush-to-zero, a local underflow error is <2^-1022. Its later
amplification is <2^300: q^60,w^60<2^240, the orthonormal scaling is <=2^30,
the cell constant can be bounded by2^20 using the compact-sector bound,
and the two polynomial factors by2^10. (Only one remaining high power is
needed along a retained-moment path.) Consequently all propagated errors
sum to less than2^(-1022+300+34)=2^-688<2^-600.

For a separate positivity check, integrate on1<=q<=9/8,1/2<=w<=5/8.
Here d<1/2, pi^-3/2>1/8, exp(-5|x|^2/8)>1/9 and the area is1/64.
Thus every retained orthonormal-z moment is >2^-42. A raw-w moment before
the factor2^(n/2) is only guaranteed >2^-72; confusing those statements
would be an error, although it has no effect on the absolute allowance.

6. CONTRACTIONS AND TAILS

The moments in orthonormal(q,z) coordinates have odd-z entries zero. For
the full tensor-moment expansion, with b=3/4,

 H=sigma^-6 sum_k (-b)^k A_k/k!,
 A_k=||sum_e c_e tensor c_e tensor int x^tensor k dmu_e||^2.

There are six self-facet terms with incidence-square4 and24 ordered
adjacent-facet terms with incidence-square1. Opposite incidences are
orthogonal. The common coefficient is therefore24 times the sum of the
self and adjacent contractions. For adjacent facets the frame Gram matrix
is [[0,1/sqrt2],[1/sqrt2,1/2]]. The resulting coefficient is

 k!/[l!m!n! 2^((l+m)/2+n)],  l+m+n=k,

and its moments are m_(l,m+n) m_(m,l+n). A term survives precisely when
l,m,n have the same parity; then the power of2 is integral and positive.
The actual loops implement this condition and coefficient. The same-facet
sum is sum_j binom(k,j)m_(k-j,j)^2. No hidden subtraction occurs until the
outer alternating series. The upper bound uses upper even terms and lower
odd terms; the lower bound reverses them.

The independent global moment-tail proof is in
TENSOR_MOMENT_EXPANSION_AND_EXACT_ABSOLUTE_TAIL_20261101.txt. I rechecked
the implementation's recurrence J_(n+2)=(8/9)(n+1)J_n with upper initial
values J_0=32/27,J_1=8/9, prefactor3456, and two-step ratio
q_k=(4/9)(k+4)^2/((k+1)(k+2)). The tail after60 is <.008217978.
The same bound applies to truncated positive moments. The q<=14 cutoff
is invariant under the facet symmetries used in the contractions.

Outside that cutoff one has |x|>14. The existing compact-sector estimate
