FINITE-DIMENSIONAL ROTATION GAUGE AND ITS CUTOFF DEFECT
2026-11-01. Successor note; no edits to the existing Hessian theorem.

RESULT
Every paired partition sufficiently close in Gaussian L1 to(C,C) has a
well-defined local reference orientation obtained by minimizing a smooth
three-dimensional cross-agreement functional. It obeys an exact rotation
orthogonality identity before truncation. The compact matched-mass
replacement constructed in
DUAL_BOUND_AND_COMPACT_REDUCTION_OF_NEAR_MINIMIZERS_20261101.txt
obeys the same identity with defect O((R+1)t_R)=o(epsilon^2).
This finite-dimensional statement is proved here. Turning it into the
weighted boundary orthogonality needed for nonlinear coercivity still
requires the moving-junction remainder estimate being developed separately.

1. EXACT CHOICE OF ORIENTATION
Let rho=-1/3, G_i=T_rho1_Ci and let A,H be measurable four-way partitions.
They need not minimize any objective for this lemma. Set
 L_(A,H)(Q)=sum_i int (1_Ai+1_Hi)T_rho1_(Q C_i) dgamma,
 Q in SO(3) near identity.                                      (1)
At A=H=C the identity is a strict local minimum. If Omega is skew, put
 r_Omega,e(x)=n_e.Omega x,
 D(r,s)=sum_e int_(Sigma_e)d_e r_e s_e d lambda_e.
The one-sided second variation at C gives
              Hess L_(C,C)(I)[Omega,Omega]=2D(r_Omega,r_Omega). (2)
This is positive definite on the three-dimensional skew matrices: if all
the rotation speeds vanish on the facets, Omega annihilates the spanning
facet normals and hence Omega=0. The boundary integrals are finite since
d_e is bounded and Gaussian surface measure integrates |x|^2.

The functional is uniformly C2-close to L_(C,C) when A,H approach C in
Gaussian L1. Indeed T_rho commutes with rotations, so
 T_rho1_(Q C_i)(x)=G_i(Q^(-1)x).
The first two spatial derivatives of G_i are bounded, by differentiating
the Gaussian convolution. Its first two rotation derivatives are bounded
by const*(1+|x|^2), uniformly for Q in a fixed compact coordinate chart.
They therefore belong to L2(gamma), with a uniform bound. Gaussian
Cauchy--Schwarz applied to 1_Ai-1_Ci and 1_Hi-1_Ci gives
 ||L_(A,H)-L_(C,C)||_C2<=const*sqrt(epsilon),              (3)
where epsilon is the maximum summed label symmetric difference.

Choose a sufficiently small fixed coordinate ball about I on which the
reference Hessian is uniformly positive definite. By(3), the perturbed
functional remains strictly convex in those coordinates. Its minimum is
interior for sufficiently small epsilon, is unique in that coordinate
ball, and satisfies
                 |Q-I|<=const*sqrt(epsilon).             (4)
One can prove(4) directly from the gradient at I and uniform strong
convexity. This argument does not assume differentiability of the
minimizer with respect to the measurable partitions.

Rotate A and H together by Q^(-1), so the selected reference orientation
becomes I. Simultaneous rotation preserves the noise objective and every
label mass. The post-alignment Gaussian L1 distance epsilon' tends to0:
the rotation of the polyhedral cones changes their Gaussian measures by
O(|Q-I|), giving epsilon'<=epsilon+O(sqrt(epsilon)). The growing-core
construction should be restarted using epsilon', not the previous
epsilon. No comparison in the other direction is required. If epsilon'
is zero, the pair is already a common rotation of(C,C).

2. THE EXACT ROTATION IDENTITY AND ITS LINEARIZATION
After alignment, let
 g_Omega,i(x)=d/dt|_(t=0) T_rho1_(exp(tOmega)C_i)(x)
            =-(Omega x).grad G_i(x).
Optimality of Q gives the exact identities
      sum_i int (1_Ai+1_Hi)g_Omega,i dgamma=0             (5)
for every Omega. The same identity holds for(C,C), so either the raw or
reference-subtracted integrand can be used.

At the reference facet,
 g_Omega,i-g_Omega,j=-d_e r_Omega,e.                      (6)
For smooth first-order boundary speeds u,v, differentiation of the left
side of(5) therefore gives
                    -D(u+v,r_Omega).                   (7)
Thus this gauge has precisely the common-rotation normal direction
required by the certified Hessian. It does not impose any condition on
the contrast speed and does not restrict common label masses.

Equation(7) alone does not justify replacing the exact nonlinear identity
by D-orthogonality for a finite deformation: the error must be controlled
relative to its actual boundary energy, including the junction caps.

3. EFFECT OF COMPACT CUTOFF AND MASS REPAIR
Apply the compact reduction note after alignment and rename epsilon'
as epsilon. Its pair(A*,H**) differs from(A,H) only outside B_(R-3),
apart from the fixed compact mass-repair patches of total changed measure
O(t_R). Here
 R^2=(21/5)log(1/epsilon),
 t_R=gamma_3(|x|>R-3)=epsilon^(21/10-o(1)).

For ||Omega||<=1, the convolution derivative bound gives
 |g_Omega,i(x)|<=const*|x|.
Consequently the defect of(5) for(A*,H**) is at most
 const*int_(|x|>R-3)|x| dgamma+O(t_R)
             <=const*(R+1)t_R=o(epsilon^2),              (8)
uniformly over a fixed orthonormal basis of the three rotation directions.
The tail moment bound follows explicitly from
 int_s^infinity r^3 exp(-r^2/2)dr=(s^2+2)exp(-s^2/2)
and the elementary lower bound on the Gaussian radial tail. The compact
mass-repair fields contribute only O(t_R), since g is bounded on their
fixed supports.

An exact rotation gauge on the truncated boundary heights is therefore
unnecessary. A later cap/slab expansion may use(8) as an additive error.
For example, if it proves for the normalized common height p+q that
 |D(p+q,r_Omega)|
 <=C polynomial(R)*eta*sqrt(E)+C(R+1)t_R,
then finite-dimensional projection gives
 ||P_rot(p+q)||_D^2
 <=C polynomial(R)*eta^2 E+C(R+1)^2 t_R^2.                (9)
The last term is o(epsilon^2), even without a lower bound on E. If an
energy-to-measure estimate E>=c epsilon^2 is available, it is also o(E).

4. EXACT SCOPE
Equations(1)-(8) are independent of any unproved nonlinear junction
estimate. Equation(9) is a conditional consequence of the stated
cap/slab approximation; that approximation has not been proved here.
This note closes the finite-dimensional orientation and cutoff-gauge
bookkeeping, not the full nonlinear local-minimum argument.
