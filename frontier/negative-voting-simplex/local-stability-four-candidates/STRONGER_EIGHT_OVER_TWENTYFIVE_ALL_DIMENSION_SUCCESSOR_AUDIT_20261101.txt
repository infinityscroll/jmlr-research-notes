3. ALL FINITE N: NO DIMENSION REDUCTION ASSUMPTION

Put d=N-3. At the cylindrical reference, the weighted surface measure
factors as d_e d lambda_e,3 tensor gamma_d. The normal derivative d_e
depends only on the first three coordinates. Consequently

 H_N=H_3 tensor L^2(gamma_d),
 A_N=A_3 tensor T_{-1/3}^{(d)}.                            (I)

The formula uses the relative Gaussian kernel and the actual weighted
Hilbert space; no extra Gaussian density or square root is missing.
Mass representers are q_c tensor1, so only extra-coordinate Hermite
degree0 is constrained by M_Nw=0. On degree k, A_N=(-1/3)^k A_3.

Degree0: (F) and (H) apply.

Degree1: A_N=-A_3/3. Its only eigenvalue-1 modes are T tensor the linear
Hermite functions, because the only eigenvalue3 modes of A_3 are T.

gap is at least1-h/3>19/24>3/8. The contrast channel is I+A_3/3 and has
gap at least2/3 because A_3>=-I.

Degree k>=2: ||A_N||<=3(1/3)^k<=1/3. Both channel gaps are at least2/3.
These estimates are uniform over every extra-coordinate Hermite component
and sum by orthogonality. They do not require A_N to have the same

5. ADMISSIBLE SPEEDS AND ACTUAL PATHS

Draft lines221-236 correctly separate the continuous quadratic form from
classical moving interfaces. Smooth compactly supported speeds away from

and the weight is positive and smooth in each open facet. Such finitely
supported facet speeds extend to ambient smooth fields in disjoint facet
neighborhoods. The mass map is continuous because its representers have
finite weighted norms, including the extra Gaussian coordinates.

Three fixed nonnegative facet bumps on a spanning tree have independent
mass images. They therefore correct a vanishing residual mass derivative
without disrupting density. For a smooth pair with first-order matching
masses, the same rank-three map and the implicit-function theorem impose
exact matching along the path. The correction has zero first derivative
and starts at order s^2. Since the unconstrained reference first variation
is zero, this correction does not change(C). All statements about an
actual second derivative retain the draft's condition that the requisite
moving-boundary differentiation is justified.

No further conclusion follows merely from density. In particular a
uniform nonlinear neighborhood result requires a deformation chart and
uniform control of junctions, tails, and the higher-order remainder.
The limitations in draft lines238-248 are accurate and should survive
any presentation of the stronger constant.
