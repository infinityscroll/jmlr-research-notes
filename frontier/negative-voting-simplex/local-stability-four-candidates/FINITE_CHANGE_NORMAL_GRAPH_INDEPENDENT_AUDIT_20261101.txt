5. COMPACT ROTATION ANGLE

The lower curvature bound and origin constants give

Every unit rotation has outside energy at least beta_R. A speed supported
inside radius R consequently has squared rotation projection at most

Compact-source lines198-231 are correct in the stated three dimensions.

integral_0^infinity r exp(-r^2/2)dr=1. A valid tail fraction is

7. HONEST IMPLICATION GAP

These results prove nonlinear inequalities on specified graph classes.
They do not show that an arbitrary near-reference pair has one crossing
per normal fiber, that its junctions remain fixed, that it admits the exact
rotation gauge within this class, or that every competitive pair is close
to a tetrahedral orbit. The common-mass box alone supplies none of those
geometric conclusions. The compact source's final limitations are accurate;
the new Gaussian-envelope estimate resolves its unbounded-radius kernel
issue only under the additional exact gauge and graph hypotheses.
