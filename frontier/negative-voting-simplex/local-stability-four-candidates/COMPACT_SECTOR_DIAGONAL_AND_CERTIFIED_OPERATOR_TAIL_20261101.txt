1. Normalization

Take four centered regular sites Y_i in R^3 with Y_i.Y_j=delta_ij-1/4.
Let C_i be their winning cones. For e=(i,j), i<j, let Sigma_e be their common
two-dimensional facet, n_e=(Y_j-Y_i)/sqrt(2) its outward normal from C_i,
and c_e=e_i-e_j its label incidence vector. Each facet is a planar sector
of angle L=acos(-1/3). Write
  d lambda_e(x)=(2pi)^(-3/2) exp(-|x|^2/2) dH^2(x).
The correlation is rho=-r=-1/3, sigma^2=1-r^2=8/9. Relative to the two
ambient Gaussian densities the Mehler kernel is
  K(x,y)=sigma^(-3) exp[-(|x|^2+|y|^2)/16-(3/8)x.y].        (1)
Put I_f(x)=int_Sigma_f K(x,y)d lambda_f(y).

The actual positive diagonal in the second variation is
  d_e(x)=partial_n_e T_-r(1_Ci-1_Cj)(x)
        =r[2 I_e(x)+(1/2)sum_{f adjacent to e} I_f(x)].       (2)
There are four adjacent facets. To check (2), distributionally differentiate
1_Ci-1_Cj in direction n_e. The common facet has coefficient -2; each of
the four adjacent facets has coefficient -1/2. The semigroup derivative
multiplies this signed boundary measure by rho=-r. All terms in (2) are
therefore positive. In particular d_e>=2r I_e.

Let H be the direct sum of L2(Sigma_e,d_e d lambda_e). Its self-adjoint
boundary operator A is defined by
  <u,A v>_H=sum_{e,f}(c_e.c_f) int int u_e(x)K(x,y)v_f(y)
                                      d lambda_e(x)d lambda_f(y).
Consequently
  ||A||_HS^2=sum_{e,f}(c_e.c_f)^2 int int K(x,y)^2/[d_e(x)d_f(y)]
                                      d lambda_e(x)d lambda_f(y).     (3)
These are surface Gaussian measures, not normalized planar probabilities.
