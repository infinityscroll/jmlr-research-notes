6. The paired Borell step is legitimate. Applying the two-set positive-rho
   upper bound to A_i and B_i^c yields
   P_{1/3}(A_i,B_i) >= p_i-J_{1/3}(p_i,1-p_i)

   F(p)=J(p)+3J((1-p)/3). Strict convexity makes F decrease to 1/4 and
   increase thereafter. No equality-of-all-four-masses assumption enters.
