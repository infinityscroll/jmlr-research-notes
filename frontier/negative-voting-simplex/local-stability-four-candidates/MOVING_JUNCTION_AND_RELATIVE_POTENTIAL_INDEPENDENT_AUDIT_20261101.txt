1. WHOLE-PLANE GRAPHS AND THE EXACT DEFECT WITNESS

For Phi=id+w with ||Dw||<1/2, F_e(x)=x+P_e w(x) on the entire tie plane
is a global bijection by contraction. Its inverse is Lipschitz, so

Along a normal line n_e.Phi^-1 has strictly positive derivative, because
||DPhi^-1-I||<=eta/(1-eta)<1. Thus the image halfspaces really are the
graph halfspaces. This argument uses only C1 control.

The restriction h_e|Sigma_e is supported inside B_R. If a point outside
B_R had an interior preimage under F_e, it would have two preimages, since
F_e is the identity outside B_R. This verifies the support assertion used
later in the fixed-R angle argument.

Let i be the unique reference score winner. Projection to the i,j tie
plane averages r_i,r_j and leaves all other scores unchanged. Therefore
eligibility is exactly(r_i+r_j)/2>=max_{k outside{i,j}}r_k. Every eligible
pair includes i. A formal signed slab is present exactly when the i,j
comparison flips, and its vector is e_j-e_i. Hence the source's expression
for the formal sum is exact, including both slab signs.

If the formal sum differs from the true winner, either that winner's
projection is ineligible or at least two eligible comparisons flip. In
the first case, the witness triple i,k,l has every score difference
bounded by r_i-r_l, controlled by the crossing i,l height. In the second,
the two crossing heights control the two score gaps from the reference
winner. All these heights belong to the INCIDENT triple. No ambient
displacement norm or unrelated fourth-label height is substituted here.

The two comparison normals have Gram eigenvalues1/2 and3/2. If b bounds
the two normalized score gaps, distance to their full triple line is at
most2b. Projection to an incident plane is a contraction and fixes that
line, so b<=H(s)+2eta*t. Absorption gives t<=4H(s).

If the longitudinal parameter s is negative, the reference winner must
overcome the fourth-label gap. The stated |s|<=2t is valid (a sharper
constant is unnecessary). Thus |z|<=6b and b<=h0+2eta|z|; for eta<=1/24,
|z|<=12h0. This independently checks the endpoint case: it is controlled
by the vertex normal heights, not by tangential displacement.

Outside the A h0 vertex ball, with A=4096, the actual narrower tube

universal eta. The fourth-label normalized gap is then>4s/5. Every
