SPATIAL REFLECTION IN THE FRACTIONAL COROLLARY
1 November 2026. Notation clarification; the frozen audit is unchanged.

In Section 10 of
TRANSFER_COMPACTNESS_AND_FRACTIONAL_ALL_DIMENSION_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_20261101.txt,
the phrase "near the pair (theta,-theta)" uses the set-reflection shorthand
from the partition discussion. For an assignment vector it should read

                 near (theta,Rtheta),
                 (Rtheta)(x)=theta(-x).

It does not mean the componentwise negative vector -theta(x), which is
not a simplex-valued assignment. Indeed T_rho R=T_(-rho), so replacing
the second rule h by Rh changes correlation -1/3 to +1/3 and sends the
reference pair (theta,theta) to (theta,Rtheta). The proved negative-
correlation statement, mass constraints, orbit distance and coefficient
are unchanged. The public main theorem is stated for partitions; the
audit's fractional extension uses this spatial-reflection convention.
