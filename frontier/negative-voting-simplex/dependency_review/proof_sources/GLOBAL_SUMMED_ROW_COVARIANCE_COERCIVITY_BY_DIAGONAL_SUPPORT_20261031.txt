GLOBAL SUMMED ROW COVARIANCE COERCIVITY BY DIAGONAL SUPPORT
31 October 2026. strong_signal_prior_art. Candidate for full audit.

This proves a GLOBAL summed row-variance payment from the balanced
Gaussian covariance deficit. The target covariance may be singular and
far from regular. Only the auxiliary supporting covariance is a small
diagonal perturbation. The proof does not sum single-row estimates,
differentiate a singular target, or discard an adverse signed term.
It does not itself assert a new noise-stability theorem.

NOTATION AND INPUTS
Let m>=3, n=m-1, P=I-11^T/m, L=1+log m,
mu=E max_(i<=m) Z_i, alpha=mu/n, and w=alpha^2 n.
For centered scores B in R^(m by N), N>=m-1, define
F_U(B)=sup_(simplex assignments of masses 1/m) E sum_i f_i B_i.X,
R_U(B)=(w+||B||_F^2)/2-F_U(B).
For centered PSD C let Gamma(C)=F_U(C^(1/2)), using its realization on
1-perp. Covariance concavity and continuity are the accepted theorem[C].
Gamma(P)=mu, alpha^2 is comparable to L/m^2, and R_U is nonnegative.

The new derivative input[H] supplies the following uniform statement.
For sum xi_i=0, ||xi||infinity<=A1, and
D_t=P diag(1+t xi_i)P, 0<=t<=c_H/L^2, take the balanced independent
scores sqrt(1+t xi_i)Z_i+h_i(t). Put F_i=Phi((x-h_i)/sqrt(1+t xi_i)),
a_i=F_i'/F_i, W0=prod_i F_i, and
ell_i(t)=integral W0 a_i sum_j a_j dx.
Then, with constants depending only on A1,

 ell_i(0)=alpha,
 ell_i'(0)=-alpha eta_m xi_i,        1/4<=eta_m<=1/2,
 |ell_i''(t)|<=C_H alpha L^2(xi_i^2+m^-1sum_j xi_j^2).       (H)

The precise source is be93031a; its full moment, mass-offset and

THEOREM 1: GLOBAL SUMMED RADIAL COERCIVITY
Fix A>=1. For all sufficiently large m depending only on A, every
centered score matrix B with

 ||B||_F^2=w,                  |B_i|^2<=A alpha^2

satisfies

 R_U(B)>=c_A alpha^2 L^(-2)
                       sum_i (|B_i|^2/alpha^2-n/m)^2,       (1)

or, equivalently,

 sum_i (|B_i|^2-w/m)^2
             <=C_A alpha^2 L^2 R_U(B)
             <=C_A L^3 R_U(B)/m^2.                         (2)

No lower row bound, pair separation, balance of an original assignment,
rank, small total covariance perturbation, or extra Gaussian coordinate
is required. Balanced masses occur only in the value defining R_U.

1. Exact hazard decomposition at a supporting covariance
For positive v_i, set D=P diag(v_i)P. The independent scores
sqrt(v_i) Z_i, after subtracting their common sample mean, realize D;
subtracting this common value changes neither the winning allocation
nor the expected score value. With balanced offsets, the offset weights
are omega_ij=integral W0 a_i a_j dx for i!=j.
Let mathcal L_D be their Laplacian. Including the analytic diagonal
omega_ii only to define ell_i=sum_j omega_ij gives EXACTLY

 mathcal L_D=P diag(ell_i)P-H_D,
 H_D=integral W0(Pa)(Pa)^T dx >=0.                          (3)

Indeed diag(ell)-integral W0 aa^T is the ordinary Laplacian, already
annihilates 1, and can therefore be projected by P on both sides.

At the positive definite supporting D, the Gaussian boundary identity
M=mathcal L_D B implies gradient_C Gamma(D)=mathcal L_D/2.
Euler homogeneity gives

                 Gamma(D)=tr(mathcal L_D D).               (4)

By concavity, for ANY centered PSD target C,

 Gamma(C)<=Gamma(D)+(1/2)tr[mathcal L_D(C-D)]
          =(1/2)tr[mathcal L_D(C+D)]
          <=(1/2)sum_i ell_i(C_ii+D_ii).                   (5)

The last step DROPS the nonpositive term
-tr[H_D(C+D)]/2. Both C and D are PSD, so this sign is exact.
No operator-norm estimate on C and no estimate of H_D is needed.
Concavity at the interior point D gives (5) directly at singular C
by value continuity; there is no endpoint derivative assumption.

2. Choose a diagonal supporting point using the actual row errors
Set C=BB^T/alpha^2. Thus C>=0, C1=0, tr C=n, C_ii<=A.
Write

 z_i=C_ii-n/m,       sum z_i=0,
 kappa=1-2/m in[1/3,1],
 xi_i=z_i/kappa,     max |xi_i|<=A1:=3(A+1).

Take D_t=P+tP diag(xi)P. Because sum xi=0,

 tr D_t=n,
 (D_t)_ii=n/m+kappa t xi_i=n/m+t z_i.                       (6)

Choose a fixed b_A>0, depending only on A and the constants in(H), with

 b_A<=min{c_H, 1, 1/(2A1), 1/[24 C_H(A+2)]},
 t=b_A/L^2.                                                (7)

Then D_t is positive definite on 1-perp, all 1+t xi_i lie in[1/2,3/2],
and (D_t)_ii<=2. The full interval[0,t] lies within(H).
Taylor's theorem with integral remainder yields

 ell_i(t)=alpha-alpha eta_m t xi_i+e_i,
 |e_i|<=C_H alpha L^2 t^2(xi_i^2+mean xi^2)/2.               (8)

Inserting(8) into(5), the constant term is alpha n=mu.
The linear term is computed exactly, not bounded absolutely:

 sum_i xi_i[C_ii+(D_t)_ii]
       =kappa(1+t)||xi||_2^2.                              (9)

The remainder obeys, since 0<=C_ii+(D_t)_ii<=A+2,

 (1/2)sum_i |e_i|[C_ii+(D_t)_ii]
       <=[C_H(A+2)/2] alpha L^2 t^2 ||xi||_2^2.             (10)

Therefore

 mu-Gamma(C)
 >=alpha{eta_m kappa t(1+t)/2
                   -C_H(A+2)L^2t^2/2}||xi||_2^2
 >=alpha kappa t ||xi||_2^2/16
 =alpha t ||z||_2^2/(16kappa)
 >=alpha b_A ||z||_2^2/(16L^2).                            (11)

The penultimate lower bound follows from eta_m>=1/4, kappa>=1/3 and
(7). In particular the error remains quadratic in the coordinate row
errors even when their Frobenius norm grows with m.
Finally R_U(B)=w-alpha Gamma(C)=alpha[mu-Gamma(C)]. Equation(11)
proves(1). Since |B_i|^2-w/m=alpha^2 z_i, (2) follows.

3. What creates the sum, and what is not used
The linear response in(9) pairs a single vector of coefficient changes
with ALL target diagonal errors at once. The structured remainder in
(8) sums to a constant times ||xi||^2. This is why the argument proves
a sum over labels without the factor m lost by averaging single-label
symmetrizations. The exact PSD hazard square in(3) supplies the correct
sign for every off-diagonal target entry simultaneously.

We have not used smallness of ||C-P||_F, full rank of C, an operator
bound on C, a Jensen second-chaos variance added to a separate lower
bound, or an unequal-variance maximum-CDF comparison.

COROLLARY 2: UNNORMALIZED SCORES AND REFERENCE-RADIUS ERROR
Fix A,s0,s1 with A>=1 and 0<s0<=s1<infinity. Let B be centered and put
s=||B||_F/sqrt(w). Assume

 s in[s0,s1],        |(B/s)_i|^2<=A alpha^2.                 (12)

Then

 sum_i (|B_i|^2-||B||_F^2/m)^2<=C L^3 R_U(B)/m^2,
 sum_i (|B_i|^2-w/m)^2<=C L^3 R_U(B)/m^2,                  (13)
 E_rad:=sum_i(|B_i|-sqrt(w/m))^2<=C L^2 R_U(B).              (14)

Constants depend only on A,s0,s1. The lower bound on s is explicitly
retained when (12) is obtained from an ABSOLUTE unnormalized row cap;
an absolute cap alone would not make the normalized cap uniform as
s tends to zero.

Proof. Set Bhat=B/s and Rhat=R_U(Bhat). Homogeneity gives the EXACT
radial decomposition

 R_U(B)=w(s-1)^2/2+s Rhat.                                 (15)

Let v_B,i=|B_i|^2-||B||_F^2/m. Then v_B=s^2 v_Bhat, so Theorem1 gives

 ||v_B||^2<=C s^4 alpha^2 L^2 Rhat
           <=C s^3 alpha^2 L^2 R_U(B).

Also, by orthogonal centering,

 sum_i(|B_i|^2-w/m)^2=||v_B||^2+(||B||_F^2-w)^2/m,
 (||B||_F^2-w)^2/m
       =w^2(s+1)^2(s-1)^2/m
       <=2w(s+1)^2 R_U(B)/m.                              (16)

Since w=O(L/m), the latter is at most C L R_U(B)/m^2 and proves(13).
Finally (|B_i|-beta)^2<=(|B_i|^2-beta^2)^2/beta^2, beta^2=w/m.
Summing and using w comparable to L/m proves(14).

COROLLARY 3: ACTUAL-MINIMIZER GEOMETRIC PAYMENT ONLY
For the actual unrestricted negative-noise minimizers in the accepted
mass window, with 0<r<=1/(m-1), source[R] proves s=1+o(1), bounded
normalized rows, R_U(B)<=C r/m, and uniformly |B_i|/sqrt(w/m)=1+o(1).
Thus (13)-(14) apply uniformly and improve the summed radial budget to

 sum_i(|B_i|^2-w/m)^2<=C r L^3/m^3,
                         E_rad<=C r L^2/m.                 (17)

Source[R, Section6] supplies an EXACT centered equal-row proxy Btilde
in the same centroid span, with |Btilde_i|=sqrt(w/m) and
||Btilde-B||^2<=C E_rad. Its angular-frame assumption there follows
from the actual bound B^T B<=p_max I and the uniform row lower bound.
Consequently

                 ||Btilde-B||^2<=C L^2 R_U(B).              (18)

Neither the proxy construction nor (18) says that the original rows
are equal, that the assignment is an affine fan, that the proxy preserves
pair separation, or that replacing the assignment by a proxy optimizer
improves noise. A direct proxy-tensor/full-noise argument is separate.
