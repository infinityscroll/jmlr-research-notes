This closes a uniform short-row exclusion, not exact row symmetry or the
full negative Standard Simplex conjecture. The first theorem is a global
finite balanced-score inequality. Its actual-minimizer corollary DOES NOT
assume balanced masses, a conical/affine partition, full centroid rank,
or unused coordinates. It uses the accepted mass/deficit localization.

THEOREM 1: EXACT GLOBAL ONE-ROW PAYMENT
For ANY centered score matrix with ||B||_F^2=w, put
                    a_i=|B_i|^2/beta^2.
Then0<=a_i<=m-1, and for each label i separately,

 R_U(B) >= [alpha I(1/m)/(2 sqrt(2))]
                         [sqrt(1+a_i)-sqrt(2)]^2.               (1)

No row cap, separation, rank, or individual facet lower bound is assumed.
In particular, on a fixed bounded interval0<=a_i<=A,

 R_U(B)>=c_A [L/m^2](a_i-1)^2                                (2)

for all sufficiently large m. This is a maximum-over-rows statement;
no sum of(1) over labels without an extra factor is asserted.

1. Symmetrize only the other labels
Set K=BB^T/alpha^2, so K>=0, K1=0, tr K=n. Fix i, put
u=Pe_i/sqrt(n/m), and average K over the finite permutation group
fixing i. Denote the average Kbar. Covariance concavity and permutation
invariance give
mathcal F(Kbar)>=mathcal F(K).
The diagonal entry K_ii is unchanged. The invariant decomposition is

 Kbar=K_a=a uu^T+b(P-uu^T),
 a=m K_ii/n=a_i, b=(n-a)/(n-1).                              (3)

Indeed the fixed-label standard subspace is span(u), the remaining
standard representation has dimension n-1, and trace is n. Positivity
gives0<=a<=n. Consequently

 R_U(B)>=w-alpha mathcal F(K_a).                             (4)

This averages COVARIANCES, not allocations or centroid rows. It makes
no assertion that a symmetrized allocation is an actual minimizer.

2. The exact strain and the Gaussian perimeter payment
For0<a<n the covariance K_a is positive definite on H. Its balanced
affine allocation has positive-mass convex polyhedral cells C_j(a).
Let lambda_ij be the Gaussian surface measure of its ij facet and
ell_ij=|K_a^(1/2)(e_i-e_j)|. Its physical offset weight is
w_ij=lambda_ij/ell_ij. These are the exact weights in[C]'s covariance
normal-strain inequality, as checked by coarea in[F, Section4].

Let E=dK_a/da=uu^T-(P-uu^T)/(n-1). For every j!=i,
(e_i-e_j).u=sqrt(m/n), hence EXACTLY

 (e_i-e_j)^T E(e_i-e_j)=1,
 (e_i-e_j)^T K_a(e_i-e_j)=a+1.                              (5)

All facets incident to i therefore have the SAME physical edge length
sqrt(a+1). The normal-strain inequality reads

 -4 mathcal F''(K_a)[E,E]
 >=sum_(e=jk) w_jk [(e_j-e_k)^T E(e_j-e_k)]^2
                                  /[(e_j-e_k)^T K_a(e_j-e_k)].

Discarding the nonnegative edges not incident to i and using(5),

 -4 mathcal F''(K_a)[E,E]
 >= [sum_(j!=i)lambda_ij]/(a+1)^(3/2)
 =P_gamma(C_i(a))/(a+1)^(3/2)
 >=I(1/m)/(a+1)^(3/2).                                    (6)

The facet sum is the once-counted boundary of the i-th cell. Junctions
have Gaussian surface measure zero. There is no factor2 from counting
all cells, and no double counting of oriented edges. No lower bound on
any particular incident facet is needed.

3. Integrate the curvature with its tangent term
Let phi(a)=alpha mathcal F(K_a). At a=1, K_a=P,
phi(1)=alpha mu_m=w. The regular covariance gradient is a scalar
multiple of P; equivalently[C] gives
phi'(1)=(alpha^2/2)tr E=0.
Thus(6) can be integrated on either side of1. The function

 G(a)=sqrt(2)(a+3)-4sqrt(a+1)
     =sqrt(2)[sqrt(a+1)-sqrt(2)]^2

satisfies G(1)=G'(1)=0 and G''(a)=(a+1)^(-3/2). Therefore
w-phi(a)>=alpha I(1/m) G(a)/4.
Together with(4) this is(1). At a=0 or n, pass to the one-sided limit
using covariance-value continuity; no endpoint differentiability or
convergence of individual duplicate-label cells is required.
The classical Gaussian maximum and Mills bounds give
alpha I(1/m) asymptotic to2 log(m)/m^2. On a bounded interval, rationalize
the square in(1) to obtain(2). Only the order comparison is used below.

THEOREM 2: ACTUAL MINIMIZERS HAVE NO SHORT ROWS
For all sufficiently large m, let f be an UNRESTRICTED global minimizer
of the single-rule negative noise objective at0<r<=1/(m-1), in ANY finite
N>=m-1. Let p be its masses and B its ACTUAL centroid matrix. Then

 max_i abs(|B_i|^2/(w/m)-1) <= C sqrt(m r/L).                  (7)

In particular all rows have length beta[1+O(L^(-1/2))], uniformly over
the entire voting interval. The constants and m threshold are absolute.
This does not imply equality of row norms, full rank, radial exposure
between every pair, or conicality. It is stronger than an averaged
row-variance estimate: no exceptional short label remains.

4. Actual own-score deficit is O(r/m), without exact balance
Write delta=||p-U||^2, W=||B||^2, and D=delta-r(W-w).
The accepted mass-window/positive-second-chaos localization[M,A] gives

 p_i in[1/(2m),3/(2m)], delta<=C r^2/m,
 D<=C r^2/m, and W>=w-C r/m.                                (8)

The global mass-value semiconcavity[V] gives
W<=V(p)<=w+C L^2 delta.
Hence W/w stays between fixed positive constants, and more precisely

 abs(W/w-1)<=C(r/L+r^2 L)<=C r/L                             (9)

for sufficiently large m uniformly on voting r.

Let h_U(B) be its balanced optimal offsets in the mean-zero gauge.
Normalize Bhat=B/s with s=sqrt(W/w). Positive score scaling gives

 R_U(B)=w(1-s)^2/2+s R_U(Bhat), h_U(B)=s h_U(Bhat).

The rank-free offset certificate[O], using the actual entropy row cap
|B_i|<=p_i sqrt(2log(1/p_i))<=C alpha, gives
||h_U(B)||^2<=C L^2 R_U(B), since s is bounded.
The ACTUAL assignment is admissible in F_p(B), so F_p(B)>=W.
The balanced optimal offsets are an admissible dual competitor at p,
thus F_p(B)<=F_U(B)-h_U(B).(p-U). Therefore

 R_U(B)<= (w-W)/2-h_U(B).(p-U)
        <= (w-W)/2+C L sqrt(R_U(B)) sqrt(delta).

Young and(8) yield

 R_U(B)<=w-W+C L^2 delta<=C r/m.                            (10)

This uses exact duality, not a false inference that f itself is affine.
Nonnegativity of R_U follows from the balanced endpoint theorem even
though f is not balanced. The normalized deficit obeys
R_U(Bhat)<=R_U(B)/s<=C r/m.

5. Apply the finite one-row certificate
The actual entropy row cap and bounded s ensure

6. Quantitative exact equal-row proxy: geometry only
The following optional consequence records what the improved row bound
adds to the earlier averaged budget; it is NOT an energy transfer.
Let beta=sqrt(w/m), E_rad=sum_i(|B_i|-beta)^2. Source[S] gives
E_rad<=C/(mL). Also Cauchy with the actual assignments gives the exact
operator estimate

 B^T B<=p_max I<=C I/m.                                    (11)

Indeed (B_i.v)^2<=[int f_i][int f_i(v.X)^2], and summing proves it.
By(7), all |B_i|/beta tend1 uniformly. Thus, writing u_i=B_i/|B_i|,

 ||m^(-1)sum_i u_i u_i^T||op<=C/L.

Let Phi(c)=m^(-1)sum_i |B_i-c| on the centroid span. It is convex.
For |c|<=beta/4, all distances are comparable to beta, and
m^(-1)sum_i [(B_i-c)/|B_i-c|][(B_i-c)/|B_i-c|]^T
 <=C/L+C |c|^2/beta^2 in operator norm (absolute constants).
Choose instead a fixed sufficiently small ball |c|<=c0 beta. The
Hessian of Phi there is at least c/beta times the identity, for large m.
Its gradient at0 has magnitude |g0|, where

 g0=m^(-1)sum_i u_i
   =m^(-1)B^T (1/|B_i|-1/beta)_i,
 |g0|<=C ||B||op sqrt(E_rad)/(m beta^2).                     (12)

This tends0. Strong convexity on that ball and outward positivity of
the radial derivative give an interior minimizer c, with
|c|<=C beta |g0| and sum_i(B_i-c)/|B_i-c|=0. Convexity makes it a global
minimizer; no point B_i lies in the small ball, so the derivative is
ordinary. Define

                   Btilde_i=beta (B_i-c)/|B_i-c|.

Then Btilde is EXACTLY centered with EXACTLY equal row norms beta,
and

 m|c|^2<=C E_rad/L,
 ||Btilde-B||_F^2<=C E_rad,
 ||Btilde||op^2<=C/m.                                     (13)

For the middle bound, compare first to beta u_i, then use the Lipschitz
bound of radial projection away from0 and the first bound in(13).
For the last, write Btilde=diag(beta/|B_i-c|)(B-1 c^T) and use(11).
The construction preserves the centroid span: c lies in that span;
if v annihilates all Btilde_i, then v.B_i=v.c for every i, and sum B_i=0
forces v.c=0 and v.B_i=0. It does not add independent coordinates.

This geometric proxy is not asserted to consist of actual centroids of
a competitive assignment, nor to decrease noise or the score remainder.
Its distance scale is still much larger than A/m in general. A signed,
deficit-relative transfer remains necessary before using it to invoke
the equal-actual-row rigidity theorem.
