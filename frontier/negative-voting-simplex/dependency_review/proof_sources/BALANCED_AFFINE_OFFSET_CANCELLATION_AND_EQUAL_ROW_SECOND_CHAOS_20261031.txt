THEOREM
Fix kappa>0. Let B be centered, with m>=3 unit-norm rows, and let
C=BB^T>=kappa P on1-perp. Let h be the mean-zero offsets for which
f_i=1_{B_i.X+h_i=max_j(B_j.X+h_j)} has masses1/m. With the regular
reference g and beta=mu_m/sqrt(m(m-1)),

        A2(f)-A2(g)>=-K_kappa(1+log m) R_U(beta B).             (1)

R_U is the exact fixed-mass covariance remainder; the offsets for
beta B are beta h. Constants are independent of the ambient dimension.
This extends the accepted equal-row result[Q] to NONZERO balancing
offsets. No stochastic order for the affine maximum is assumed.
Unequal row norms and rank loss remain outside this theorem. The
calibrated sparse pair source[S] has slightly unequal centered rows,
so it is not silently included despite being consistent with(1).

1. Two distinct mean deficits
Write L=1+log m, C_*=mP/(m-1), D=C-C_*, and let M_* be the regular
unit-row maximum. Define
 M_s=max_i(B_i.X+s h_i), F_s=E M_s, G_s=E M_s^2, 0<=s<=1.
Let F_U(C)=F_1 be the balanced fixed-mass value. Thus

 epsilon_bal=E M_*-F_1,
 epsilon_free=E M_*-F_0,
 delta=F_0-F_1,
 epsilon_bal=epsilon_free+delta.                              (2)

Both summands are nonnegative. Delta is the convex offset minimization
gain, since F'_1=sum h_i/m=0. The free conical mean comparison follows
from the primary Mulgund theorem used in[Q], regardless of whether the
zero-offset conical cells have equal masses.

The polynomial balanced covariance gap from[Q, Sections1-2] remains
valid here:

 epsilon_bal>=c_kappa exp(-B_kappa L)||D||_F^2.                (3)

Indeed along C_u=C_*+uD one always chooses the BALANCED offsets.
The proof of every intermediate facet lower bound uses these masses,
not zero offsets. The covariance normal term gives(3), and at the
endpoint the value is F_U(C)=F_1. No equality with the free value F_0
is required. The derivative at C_* is still zero because tr D=0.

2. The free maximum's second moment is paid by the balanced deficit
Apply the transformed-CDF argument of[Q] to the zero-offset maximum
M_0. The transformed covariance is R=(m-1)C/m+J/m. Its CDF gap H
against the independent maximum is nonnegative by Mulgund's theorem,
and the density-Hessian calculation gives

 |H(t)|<=C_kappa sqrt(m)||D||_F^2 exp(-t^2/4).

Its integral is sqrt((m-1)/m) epsilon_free. Split the first absolute
threshold moment at T=K_kappa sqrt(L). The central part is at most
T sqrt((m-1)/m) epsilon_free<=T epsilon_bal. The tail is paid by(3),
with the same covariance-deficit amplitude. Consequently

               (E M_*^2-G_0)_+<=C_kappa sqrt(L)epsilon_bal.    (4)

This does not claim the sharper version with epsilon_free on the
right. The balanced value deficit is precisely what is needed below.
It also avoids asserting any CDF sign for M_1.

3. The exact offset derivatives
Let p_i(s) be the masses along the straight offset path, and let an
ij facet have surface Gaussian mass mu_e(s), edge length ell_e,
weight w_e(s)=mu_e(s)/ell_e, and Gaussian conditional mean m_e(s).
Write h_e=h_i-h_j. The full-rank sites are affinely independent;
the offsets give translated polyhedral fans. The ordinary Gaussian
facet differentiation formulas are therefore valid. Gaussian tails
justify their integration; lower-dimensional junctions have no surface
contribution. Direct differentiation gives

 F'_s=sum_i p_i(s)h_i,
 F''_s=sum_e w_e(s)h_e^2,
 G'_s=2sum_i h_i E[M_s f_i(s)],
 G''_s=2sum_i p_i(s)h_i^2
                  +2sum_e w_e(s)h_e^2 E[M_s|Gamma_e(s)].      (5)

Also, if A_s=sum_i p_i(s)h_i^2, then

                 A'_s=sum_e w_e(s)h_e^2(h_i+h_j).             (6)

These are exact identities. In particular no terminal G'_1 is set
to zero merely because the masses at1 are balanced. Balance only
gives F'_1=0 and hence

                    delta=int_0^1 s F''_s ds.                (7)

4. Cancellation inside the quadratic certificate
Put Q_i=E[(XX^T-I)f_i(1)] and
T_i=B_i B_i^T-(B^TB)/m. Since sum Q_i=0 and the rows have norm1,
the numerator in the quadratic certificate is

 T:=sum_i<Q_i,T_i>
   =E[(B_winner.X)^2]-1
   =G_1-G'_1+A_1-1.                                         (8)

Now integrate G'' and A' exactly:
 G_1-G'_1=G_0-int_0^1 sG''_s ds,
 A_1-2int_0^1 sA_s ds=int_0^1 s^2 A'_s ds.
On an ij facet,
 2M_s-s(h_i+h_j)=(B_i+B_j).X.
Combining(5),(6),(8) gives the cancellation identity

 T=G_0-1-int_0^1 s sum_e w_e(s)h_e^2
                                  (B_i+B_j).m_e(s) ds.       (9)

The problematic terminal derivative cancels with the mixed affine
terms of the exact quadratic certificate. Neither term was bounded
separately. Equation(9) is valid before any estimate or sign claim.

5. Paying the entire offset correction
The endpoint mass condition implies
 |h_i-h_j|/ell_e<=Phi^-1(1-1/m)<=sqrt(2log m),
and therefore |h_i|<=2sqrt(2L) in the mean-zero gauge. Along s h these
bounds only improve. The explicit facet shift construction of[Q,
Section1] applies with the SAME fixed score matrix B at every s,
whether or not the intermediate masses are equal: the endpoint offset
bound is all that construction needs. Thus each tangent facet section
has probability at least c exp(-B L), and its normal coordinate is
bounded by sqrt(2L).

A Gaussian set of probability p has conditional mean of norm at most
sqrt(2log(1/p)), by the Gaussian entropy/centroid bound. Apply this in
the facet tangent space, then restore its fixed normal coordinate.
It follows that ||m_e(s)||<=C_kappa sqrt(L), uniformly over e,s.
Since ||B_i+B_j||<=2, equations(7),(9) yield

                   |T-(G_0-1)|<=C_kappa sqrt(L)delta.          (10)

Combining(2),(4),(10),
          T>=E M_*^2-1-C_kappa sqrt(L)epsilon_bal.             (11)

6. The denominator and conversion to R_U
Exactly as in[Q],
 D_B:=sum_i||T_i||_F^2=m-tr(C^2)/m<=m(m-2)/(m-1)=D_*.
Hence A2(f)>=T_+^2/(2D_*). The regular reference attains equality,
with numerator E M_*^2-1. The elementary squared-positive-part

 A2(f)-A2(g)
 >=-[E M_*^2-1] C_kappa sqrt(L)epsilon_bal/D_*.

Finally ||beta B||^2=w and F_U(beta B)=beta F_1, so

E M_*^2-1=O(L), and D_* is of order m, this is exactly(1).
If D=0 the balanced offsets are0 and all deficits vanish; no division
