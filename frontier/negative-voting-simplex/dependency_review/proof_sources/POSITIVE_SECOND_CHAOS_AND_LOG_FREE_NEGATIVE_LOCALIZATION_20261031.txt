POSITIVE SECOND CHAOS AND LOG-FREE NEGATIVE-RANGE LOCALIZATION
31 October 2026. Root proposal; independent branch derivation.
Frozen mathematical candidate for independent audit.

This is a further localization theorem for competitive SINGLE-rule
negative-noise assignments, throughout 0<r<=1/(m-1), for all sufficiently
large m. It retains positive degree two rather than bounding it away.
It does not prove exact uniform masses or the Standard Simplex Conjecture.
No finite numerical threshold for m, publication, or priority is asserted.

Pinned inputs are listed in the companion JSON. In particular the accepted
mass-window semiconcavity includes singular optimal covariance matrices
and uses the optimizer-GRAPH compactness clarification. No varying-fiber
continuity or full-rank hypothesis is added here.

1. Notation and an assignment-independent second-chaos inequality
Let X be standard Gaussian in R^N, and let f=(f_1,...,f_m) be measurable,
0<=f_i<=1, sum_i f_i=1. Set
  p_i=E f_i, b_i=E[X f_i], P=sum_i p_i^2, W=sum_i |b_i|^2,
  A2(f)=1/2 sum_i ||E[f_i(XX^T-I)]||_F^2.
Thus P,W,A2 are exactly the nonnegative Hermite weights in degrees0,1,2.
For every such assignment, in every dimension,

  A2(f) >= (W-P)_+^2/(2P).                              (1)

Proof. Terms with p_i=0 have f_i=0 a.e. and b_i=0. For p_i>0 and b_i!=0,
test the matrix in the direction e_i=b_i/|b_i|. Conditional Jensen gives
  e_i^T E[f_i(XX^T-I)] e_i >= |b_i|^2/p_i-p_i.
The Frobenius norm therefore dominates the positive part of the right
side. The same conclusion is immediate when b_i=0. Consequently
  2A2 >= sum_i (|b_i|^2-p_i^2)_+^2/p_i^2
       >= [sum_i (|b_i|^2-p_i^2)_+]^2/P
       >= (W-P)_+^2/P,
where zero-mass summands are omitted. This proves (1).

The function F(W,P)=(W-P)_+^2/(2P), P>0, is jointly convex: it is the
perspective P*((W/P-1)_+^2/2). The positive part is essential for a global
tangent inequality; we do not claim A2 >= (W-P)^2/(2P) when W<P.

2. Tangent inequality at the balanced regular simplex
Let g be the m-cell regular simplex partition in R^(m-1), embedded when
N>=m-1 and used as an external benchmark when N<m-1. Put
  M_m=max of m independent standard Gaussians, mu=E M_m,
  w=mu^2/(m-1), L=mw-1,
  A2g=m(E M_m^2-1)^2/[2(m-1)(m-2)],
  F0=F(w,1/m)=L^2/(2m), Delta2=A2g-F0.
For sufficiently large m, L>0 and L=O(log m). By (1) applied to g,
Delta2>=0. For any assignment define
  delta=P-1/m=|p-(1/m,...,1/m)|^2,
  D=delta-r(W-w).
The tangent inequality for F at (w,1/m) is

  A2(f) >= F0+L(W-w)-((mw)^2-1)delta/2
         = F0-(L/r)D+c_{m,r} delta,                    (2)
  c_{m,r}=L[1/r-(L+2)/2].

For all sufficiently large m and every 0<r<=1/(m-1), c_{m,r}>=0 and
1-rL>=1/2. These follow from L=O(log m), uniformly in r. There is no
assumption that W>P for the competitor in (2); convexity of the positive-
part perspective supplies the tangent globally.

3. The reference defect Delta2 is O(1/m), not O(log^2 m/m)
Write u=mu^2-1 and v=Var(M_m). An exact algebraic identity gives

 Delta2 = m*u^2/[2(m-1)^2(m-2)]
        + m*(2uv+v^2)/[2(m-1)(m-2)]
        - u/(m-1)^2 - 1/[2m(m-1)^2].                 (3)

The previously pinned Gumbel-tail proof gives, with
b=Phi^-1(1-1/m) and Z_m=b(M_m-b), bounded second moments of Z_m and
mu/b ->1. In particular v=Var(Z_m)/b^2=O(b^-2), u=O(b^2), and b^2=O(log m).
Hence uv=O(1), v^2=O(1), and u^2=O(log^2 m). Dropping the negative terms
in (3) for large m yields

  0<=Delta2=O(1/m+log^2 m/m^2)=O(1/m).                 (4)

This uses the actual centered variance bound, rather than merely the two
separate estimates E M_m^2=O(log m) and mu^2=O(log m). The exact formula
for A2g and the second-moment uniform integrability are pinned inputs.

4. Retaining positive degree two in the actual noise comparison
Assume S_{-r}(f)<=S_{-r}(g). Hermite parity and
sum_k a_k(f)=sum_i E f_i^2<=1 give the exact one-sided bounds
  S_{-r}(f)>=P-rW+r^2 A2(f)-r^3,
  S_{-r}(g)<=1/m-rw+r^2 A2g+r^4.
Thus
  D+r^2[A2(f)-A2g]<=r^3+r^4.
Inserting (2) proves the sharper joint inequality

 (1-rL)D+r^2 c_{m,r} delta <= r^2 Delta2+r^3+r^4.      (5)

The previously accepted actual-noise localization first places p in the
fixed box c/m<=p_i<=C0/m, with c=1/100,C0=2, for all sufficiently large m.
The accepted mass-window bound V_m(p)<=w+A_m delta, A_m=O(log^2 m), gives

 D >= (1-rA_m)delta+r[V_m(p)-W]
   >= delta/2+r[V_m(p)-W] >=0.                        (6)

Here V_m(p) is the true prescribed-mass first-moment optimum, including
singular optimum Grams. The fixed c,C0 make all eventual constants
absolute. Neither (5) nor (6) identifies the full-noise functional with
its truncated part.

From (4)-(6), uniformly over the entire interval 0<r<=1/(m-1),

 D <= [r^2 Delta2+r^3+r^4]/(1-rL)=O(r^2/m),           (7)
 delta=O(r^2/m),  V_m(p)-W=O(r/m),  |W-w|=O(r/m).     (8)

For the last estimate, (6) gives 0<=delta<=2D, so
|delta-D|<=D and |W-w|=|delta-D|/r<=D/r. The simplification of (7) uses
r<=1/(m-1), hence r^3+r^4=O(r^2/m). At the largest admissible scale,

 |p-U|^2=O(m^-3),
 sum_i(mp_i-1)^2=O(m^-1),
 max_i|mp_i-1|=O(m^-1/2),
 V_m(p)-W=O(m^-2),  |W-w|=O(m^-2).                   (9)

These estimates improve the earlier log^2 losses by paying most of the
reference degree-two weight with the unavoidable competitor degree-two
weight. They concern every competitive assignment, not only a stationary
or locally smooth partition.

5. Remaining limitation
Delta2 and the uncontrolled odd-tail allowance remain positive. The bound
(5) therefore does not force D=0 at positive r. It is not a relative
full-chaos comparison and does not rule out a non-simplex competitor.
No inequality comparing each higher chaos separately to D is asserted;
far-interface deformations still obstruct such a globally unweighted route.
The full weighted/resummed noise comparison remains a separate gate.
