READING ORDER AND EXACT LOGICAL CHAINS

1. Covariance and endpoint.
Read 225 §§1–9 for the covariance functional, Price derivative, cycle cancellation, normal-strain bound, concavity and singular continuity. Then read 907 for the balanced endpoint and strict zero remainder. This is an EXPLICIT dependency replacement: 225 §10 historically cites 3cfd and 8a, but 907 supplies exactly the needed endpoint/equality proof directly. No quantitative statement from 3cfd/8a is imported. The quantitative covariance remainder instead comes from 47.

Full independent read of 907e0360: PASS. The regular centroid normalization yields grad Gamma(C0)=P/2. Complete edge quadratic forms determine centered symmetric directions, so the normal-strain term gives a strict initial tangent gap in every nonzero direction. Concavity and continuity preserve it to singular endpoints. The final pointwise routing equality applies to arbitrary simplex-valued assignments whose actual centroid Gram is regular.

2. Prescribed-mass window and competitive localization.
11 + 6d -> 12dc -> d261 -> 225, with 907 supplying endpoint/equality.
The graph-limit clarification 6d is essential: convergence is to the GRAPH of true optimizers at limiting masses, not necessarily to the varying same-p argmax fiber. The refined d261 covariance Hessian retains the cycle subtraction and uses the external Ehrhard theorem.
45 is the independent full-voting competitive mass localization and exact reference A2 calculation. It is missing from the manuscript's 17 internal copies but is mathematically required by 251. Then 251 + 11 + 6d give the log-free competitive localization used by c33 and the manuscript. These statements apply to competitive fractional assignments and do not require attainment or Euler--Lagrange stationarity.

3. Global offsets and one-row control.
41 uses the covariance dual, elementary Gaussian centroid estimates, Gaussian isoperimetry/coarea, and the 11+6d value envelope. Its arbitrary-rank value/offset extension must not be confused with an affine partition uniqueness claim at duplicate rows.
e33 supplies the global one-row covariance estimate. The old actual-only subset budget ab9 is not required: c33 supplies the competitive scope and replaces the radial budget by E_rad<=C r L^2/m. Its geometric-median construction uses that revised budget and conditional Cauchy, not an assumed actual-minimizer theorem.

4. Facet bootstrap and global covariance remainder.

In particular, the entire negative-noise Hessian theorem in ec1 and the higher-chaos sign theorem in 1f5 are NOT inputs to 47. The conditional tie law and its moment normalization are written in the included sources; Gaussian maximum estimates are also available in 45.
5ed §1 extends the initial covariance segment to arbitrary PSD endpoints. The unnormalized version is explicitly the paragraph in 05176 V2 §7 after (25) and before (26). It follows from the same tangent-remainder integration and does not use the equal-row, pair-gap, weak-exposure or cap hypotheses of the surrounding 05176 theorem.
5ed §§3–4 supply the regular margin and rare-label signed-tail continuity. They do not require 5ed §6's inside-affine comparison or §7's rank-free A2 combination. The final exterior tensor estimate is independently assembled in 84 using Mulgund, so 49 and 003 are not transitive inputs of this chosen proof.
