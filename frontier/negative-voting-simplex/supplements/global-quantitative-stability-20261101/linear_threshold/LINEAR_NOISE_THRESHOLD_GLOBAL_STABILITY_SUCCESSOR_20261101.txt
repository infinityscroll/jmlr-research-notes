THEOREM
There are absolute c>0 and m2 such that for every m>=m2, finite N>=m-1,
0<r<=1/(m-1), and measurable f:R^N->Delta_m,

 E(f) >= c min{ r/m^2,
               delta+r*d1(f)^4/[m^(5/2)(1+log m)^2] }.       (T')

If E(f)<=r/m^2, the stronger intermediate statement remains

 E(f)>=[delta+r R_U(B)]/6.                                 (S')

In particular, near the optimum,
 delta<=6E(f),
 d1(f)<=C[m^(5/2)(1+log m)^2 E(f)/r]^(1/4).

The threshold is now linear in r. After division by r the geometric
lower bound has a nonzero small-r limit. No positive lower bound on r,
balance, purity, stationarity, or interface hypothesis is introduced.
The exponent and alphabet threshold are not claimed optimal or numerical.

1. LOCALIZATION WITH THE LARGER SMALL-r ALLOWANCE

Assume only E(f)<=r/m^2. The additive term in the coarse mass estimate is
  m^2 E(f)/(1-rB_m)<=r/(1-rB_m)=O(1/m).
The fixed box1/(100m)<=p_i<=2/m therefore still follows. The retained
positive-degree-two tangent and the prescribed-mass value bound give

 0<=D, delta<=2D,
 D<=2[E(f)+C r^2/m]<=C r/m^2,                            (1)

because rm<=m/(m-1)<=2. Hence

 |W-w|<=D/r<=C/m^2,
 W/w=1+O(1/(mL)), delta<=C r/m^2<=C/m^3, L=1+log m.       (2)

In particular the refined mass box1/(2m)<=p_i<=3/(2m) holds. As before,
the balanced offset argument uses feasibility and gives

 0<=R_U(B)<=J=D/r<=C/m^2.                                (3)

There is no assumption E>=0. The final comparisons will imply it.
Fixed-m first-moment attainment is still a legitimate input; full-noise
attainment is unnecessary.

2. ROW GEOMETRY REMAINS UNIFORMLY CONTROLLED

Normalize Bhat=B/s, s=sqrt(W/w), and set beta=sqrt(w/m). The exact radial
identity gives R_U(Bhat)<=C R_U(B). Individual centroid entropy and (2)
give a fixed normalized row cap, independent of m and r. The one-row
coercivity bound therefore gives

 max_i ||Bhat_i|^2/beta^2-1|
   <=C sqrt(m^2 R_U(Bhat)/L)<=C/sqrt(L).                  (4)

The summed-row theorem gives

 E_rad=sum_i(|Bhat_i|-beta)^2<=C L^2 R_U(B)<=C L^2/m^2.    (5)

Conditional Cauchy for the actual assignment implies B^T B<=p_max I,
so ||Bhat||op^2<=C/m. With u_i=Bhat_i/|Bhat_i|,

 ||m^-1 sum_i u_i u_i^T||op<=C/L.                       (6)

These are the required endpoint-sized geometric bounds. They no longer
carry the sharper r-specific rates from exact competitiveness, and those
old rates must not be asserted here.

3. REPROVE THE GEOMETRIC PROXY UNDER (5), WITHOUT A WRONG LEMMA CITATION

The frozen manuscript's proxy lemma explicitly assumes E_rad<=C rL^2/m.
For very small ACTUAL r, (5) does not imply that premise. We therefore
give the geometric argument directly rather than applying that lemma
with an unmet hypothesis.

On the centroid span let Phi(c)=m^-1 sum_i |Bhat_i-c|. Centering and
the operator bound yield

 |grad Phi(0)|
 <=C ||Bhat||op sqrt(E_rad)/(m beta^2)
 <=C sqrt(m) sqrt(E_rad)/L<=C/sqrt(m).                    (7)

On |c|<=c0 beta, with c0>0 sufficiently small and absolute, all row
distances lie between beta/2 and2beta. Unit directions change by at most
C c0. Equation (6) then makes their average outer-product operator at
most1/2. Each summand in the Hessian is positive semidefinite, so

 Hess Phi(c)=m^-1 sum_i [I-u_i(c)u_i(c)^T]/|Bhat_i-c|
             >=I/(4beta).                               (8)

For large m, (7) and (8) make the outward radial derivative positive on
the boundary of that ball. A minimizer of Phi on the ball is interior;
convexity makes it a global minimizer, with gradient zero and
  |c_*|<=C beta |grad Phi(0)|.
Define Z_i=beta(Bhat_i-c_*)/|Bhat_i-c_*|. Then

 sum_i Z_i=0, |Z_i|=beta,
 ||Z-Bhat||^2<=C[E_rad+m|c_*|^2]<=C E_rad.                 (9)

For the final inequality use
 m|c_*|^2<=C m beta^2[m E_rad/L^2]<=C E_rad/L.
The radial identity also gives ||B-Bhat||^2<=2R_U(B). Thus

 ||Z-B||^2<=C L^2 R_U(B)<=C L^2 J.                       (10)

This construction stays in the actual centroid span and tolerates rank
loss and duplicate proxy rows. It makes no centroid-realizability or
noise-monotonicity claim about Z.

4. THE NOISE COMPARISON USES (10), NOT THE OLD r-SPECIFIC CEILING

If d(B)<=1/m, the local own-centroid theorem assumes only its score ball
and mass box. Both hold. Its conclusion is
  E(f)>=delta/2+rR_U(B).                                 (11)
In its proof d(B)+||p-U||<=C/m and the forcing amplitude is bounded by
C L^4(r/m+r^2); the all-amplitude estimate covers arbitrarily small r.
The theorem does not require delta<=C r^2/m.

If d(B)>1/m, covariance coercivity gives the unchanged exterior floor
J>=R>=c/(m^3 sqrt(L)). With (10), the actual routing loss and proxy mean
defect sum to (w-W+||Z-B||^2)/2<=C L^2 J. The common-threshold Gaussian
comparison and direct tensor test consequently give
  A2(f)-A2(g)>=-C L^3 J.
The absolute Gaussian tail is paid by the same exterior floor. No smaller
upper bound on J is used at this stage.

The entire signed-tail proof uses p_i<=3/(2m), the regular routing loss
R0=(w-W+d(B)^2)/2, Delta<=J, and that exterior floor. Its two cases
J>=d(B)^2 and J<d(B)^2 give exactly the previous bound
  |H_r(f)-H_r(g)|<=C r^3 m^(7/4)L^(7/2)J.
Neither case uses J<=Cr/m. Thus

 E(f)>=D[1-CrL^3-Cr^2m^(7/4)L^(7/2)]>=D/2.              (12)

By delta<=2D and rR<=D, (11)-(12) prove (S').

5. COMPLETE THE GLOBAL AND SMALL-r CONCLUSIONS

The all-distance covariance remainder and exact top-two margin argument
in Sections3-5 of the previous quantitative source use only W<=Cw,
the actual centroid identity, and Gaussian geometry. They still give
  d1(f)^4<=C m^(5/2)L^2 R_U(B).
Combine with (S') when E(f)<=r/m^2. When E(f)>r/m^2, (T') is immediate
after reducing c to at most1. This proves the theorem.

For a balanced f, divide (T') by r and let r decrease to zero. The
Hermite expansion, with total energy at most1, gives
  E(f)/r -> w-||B||^2.
Hence the same proof supplies the consistency corollary

 w-||B||^2 >= c min{1/m^2, d1(f)^4/[m^(5/2)L^2]}.

This is a quantitative consequence, not a priority claim for the already
known balanced first-moment theorem. The full negative-noise candidate
and its foundational analytic inputs remain subject to external review.
