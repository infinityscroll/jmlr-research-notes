GLOBAL QUANTITATIVE STABILITY IN THE EVENTUAL-LARGE-m NEGATIVE VOTING RANGE
1 November 2026. New root theorem, submitted for independent reconstruction.

STATUS AND SCOPE

This is a quantitative strengthening of the preserved full-noise proof
candidate. It uses that proof's analytic inputs, whose external validation
remains outstanding. It neither settles the remaining small alphabets nor
provides a numerical m0. The result concerns one rule, including fractional
rules, with arbitrary masses. Constants do not depend on the finite ambient
dimension or on the correlation within the stated interval.

THEOREM

There are absolute c>0 and m1 such that the following holds. Let m>=m1,
N>=m-1 be finite, 0<r<=1/(m-1), and f:R^N->Delta_m be measurable. Put
  L=1+log m,
  E(f)=S_-r(f)-S_-r(g),
  p=E_gamma f, U=(1/m,...,1/m), delta=||p-U||^2,
  d1(f)=inf_Q integral sum_i |f_i-g_i^Q| dgamma_N,
where g^Q ranges over regular simplex assignments in R^N, including label
permutations. Then

  E(f) >= c min { r^2/m,
                 delta + r d1(f)^4/[m^(5/2) L^2] }.          (T)

More specifically, whenever E(f)<=r^2/m, its actual centroid matrix
B_i=E_gamma[X f_i] satisfies

  E(f)>=(delta+r R_U(B))/6,                                (S1)
  d1(f)^4<=C m^(5/2) L^2 R_U(B),                          (S2)
  delta<=6E(f),
  d1(f)<=C [m^(5/2)L^2 E(f)/r]^(1/4).                     (S3)

Here alpha=mu/(m-1), mu=E max_{i<=m} G_i for independent standard normals,
w=alpha^2(m-1), and R_U(B)=(w+||B||_F^2)/2-F_U(B), where F_U is the
balanced linear-assignment support functional. A closest regular centroid
embedding exists by compactness of the finite-dimensional Stiefel manifold;
no full-noise optimizer or stationary interface is used.

The exponent four is a proved, possibly non-sharp modulus. A numerical
constant or optimal stability exponent is not claimed. The proof below
does not assume E(f)>=0; (T) also recovers the global inequality.

  1/(100m)<=p_i<=2/m

The positive-degree-two tangent comparison251c3c7c similarly becomes

   <=E(f)+r^2 Delta2+r^3+r^4,                             (2)

4. AN EXACT REGULAR TOP-TWO MARGIN BOUND

Let G be the gap between the largest and second largest of m independent
standard normals. Its density at t>=0 is
  h_m(t)=m(m-1) integral phi(x+t)phi(x)Phi(x)^(m-2) dx.
Integration by parts gives h_m(0)=mu. Let T have density proportional to
phi(x)^2 Phi(x)^(m-2). Then
  h_m(t)=mu E exp(-tT-t^2/2).

This one-dimensional law has potential curvature at least2, including
after every linear tilt. Brascamp--Lieb therefore gives variance at most
1/2 under every such tilt. If K(t)=log E exp(-tT), then K''<=1/2 and
K'(0)=-ET<=0; ET>=0 follows from symmetry of phi^2 and monotonicity of
Phi^(m-2). Hence K(t)<=t^2/4 and

  h_m(t)<=mu exp(-t^2/4)<=mu.                             (10)

The gap between the two leading regular centroid scores is alpha G.
Its density is therefore at most mu/alpha=m-1. For any measurable
0<=h<=1 with E h=epsilon, layer cake gives

  E[h alpha G]>=epsilon^2/[2(m-1)].                       (11)

The weight h may depend on every ambient coordinate. Only the scalar
cost distribution is used; independence of h and G is not assumed.

 ||f-g0||_1^4<=64(m-1)^2 w d^2<=C m L d^2.              (13)

6. GLOBAL ASSEMBLY

If E(f)<=r^2/m, combine (S1)-(S2) to obtain
  E(f)>=c[delta+r d1(f)^4/(m^(5/2)L^2)].
If E(f)>r^2/m, inequality (T) is immediate after reducing c to at most1.
This proves (T), and (S3) follows directly from the small-excess case.
