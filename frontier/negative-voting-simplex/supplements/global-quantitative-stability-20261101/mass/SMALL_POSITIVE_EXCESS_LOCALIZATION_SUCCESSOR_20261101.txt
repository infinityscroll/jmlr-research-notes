The prescribed-mass function below includes fractional assignments by the

1. THE COARSE MASS BOX WITH THE EXACT ADDITIVE EXCESS
Use the notation of45e2cc45:
 F_m=m I(1/m)^2, I(t)=phi(Phi^-1(t)),
 B_m=max(2log m+1,6),
 A2g=m(E M_m^2-1)^2/[2(m-1)(m-2)].
Its rearrangement and parity estimates are assignment inequalities, so

 E >= delta-r(W-w)-r^2 A2g-r^3-r^4,
 W<=F_m+B_m delta.

Consequently, once m-1>B_m,

 delta <= [E+r(F_m-w)+r^2 A2g+r^3+r^4]/(1-rB_m).       (S1)

Every coefficient in the old numerator is nonnegative. Denote the old
endpoint certificate from45e equation(2) by mathcalL_m. For E<=r^2/m,

 m^2 delta <= mathcalL_m
      + m r^2/(1-B_m/(m-1))
 <= mathcalL_m + m/[(m-1)^2(1-B_m/(m-1))].             (S2)

The additional term is O(1/m), uniformly in the full interval. This uses
an upper bound on E, so it remains valid when E<0. Since
mathcalL_m->2-2gamma<1, exactly the same fixed positive relative-mass box
c/m<=p_i<=C0/m holds for all sufficiently large m. The constants
c=1/100,C0=2 suffice. No full-noise minimizer was selected.

2. RETAINED DEGREE TWO: SIGNS DO NOT CHANGE
Let ell=mw-1, and set
 F0=ell^2/(2m), Delta2=A2g-F0=O(1/m),
 c_(m,r)=ell[1/r-(ell+2)/2].

The universal positive-part tangent from251c3c7c is

 A2(f)>=F0-(ell/r)D+c_(m,r)delta.                      (S3)

The exact energy definition and the one-sided parity bounds give

 D+r^2[A2(f)-A2g] <= E+r^3+r^4.

Thus the retained tangent comparison becomes exactly

 (1-r ell)D+r^2 c_(m,r)delta
       <=E+r^2 Delta2+r^3+r^4.                       (S4)

No term changes sign when E is positive. No assertion W>P is needed:
the positive-part perspective underlying(S3) makes it a global tangent.

3. THE VALUE BOUND SUPPLIES D>=0, WITHOUT COMPETITIVENESS
The already obtained mass window permits11a6959e with6d19bda9:
 V(p)<=w+A_m delta, A_m=O(log^2 m).
The actual fractional f is feasible for V(p). Hence, for large m,

 D=delta+r(w-V)+r(V-W)
  >=(1-rA_m)delta+r(V-W)
  >=delta/2+r(V-W)>=0.                               (S5)

This is independent of E. Dropping the nonnegative second term on the
left of(S4), and using E<=r^2/m, gives

 0<=D<=2[E+r^2 Delta2+r^3+r^4]<=C r^2/m.              (S6)

The middle expression must be nonnegative for any actual f in the class;
we do not assume that fact independently. Since delta<=2D,
|delta-D|<=D and therefore|W-w|=|delta-D|/r<=D/r.
Also w is of order(1+log m)/m, so W/w=1+O(r/(1+log m)).
Finally,

 max_i|m p_i-1| <=m sqrt(delta)<=C sqrt(m)r<=C/sqrt(m),

giving the sharper displayed[1/(2m),3/(2m)] box. These are exactly the
localization outputs used in the direct proof. Every possible W>w
competitor is retained in this reasoning.

4. WHAT THE SAME INTERIOR/EXTERIOR CERTIFICATES WOULD THEN IMPLY
This paragraph is an assembly consequence, conditional on the validity
of the local and exterior certificates themselves; their full analytic
proofs are outside the present mass branch. Their scopes are sufficient:
1a489344 explicitly assumes any measurable assignment with its actual
centroid in the A/m ball and the mass box, not E<=0.

Let R=R_U(B), d=dist_F(B,regular centroid orbit), J=D/r.
The score-dual calculation uses only feasibility and the bounds proved
above, so R<=J. The direct uniform-row/proxy construction and exterior
estimates likewise use the localization bounds, not the sign of E.

If d<=1/m, the local certificate gives E>=delta/2+rR.
If d>1/m, the existing exterior certificate gives E>=D/2, while(S5)
and R<=D/r give delta+rR<=3D. Therefore throughout E<=r^2/m,

 E >=(delta+rR)/6.                                    (S7)

For E>r^2/m the trivial strict bound gives the unconditional truncated
consequence, with the same qualifications about the certificates:

 E >=(1/6) min{delta+rR_U(B), r^2/m}.                   (S8)

Thus the assumption E<=0 is unnecessary both for localization and for
the proposed near-optimal stability statement. A hypothetical negative-E
assignment lies in the near class and contradicts(S7). The equality
classification still uses the established zero-remainder rigidity;
it is not inferred from mass localization alone.
