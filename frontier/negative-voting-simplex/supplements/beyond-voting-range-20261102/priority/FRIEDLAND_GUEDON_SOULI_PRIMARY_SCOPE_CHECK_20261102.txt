Actual scope (pp3–5): Theorem1.1 compares generalized means of
Z_beta(X)=sum_i exp(beta X_i) over centered Gaussian vectors with
unit-diagonal PSD covariance. The regular simplex maximizes for q<1
and minimizes for q>1. Theorems1.2–1.3 give strong covariance convexity
and centroid/Gram-shape stability for E[Z_beta(X)^q], requiring q>1
and beta!=0. Theorem1.4 treats positive log-concave scalar profiles;
Corollary1.5 adds R−11^T/N>=0. Its identical-profile case includes
common-threshold indicators; Remark1.6 explicitly identifies the
extension of Mulgund’s comparison.

Overlap beyond Gaussian maxima: finite-temperature partition functions,
quantitative covariance stability, and general log-concave-profile
comparison. These deserve acknowledgment where relevant.
