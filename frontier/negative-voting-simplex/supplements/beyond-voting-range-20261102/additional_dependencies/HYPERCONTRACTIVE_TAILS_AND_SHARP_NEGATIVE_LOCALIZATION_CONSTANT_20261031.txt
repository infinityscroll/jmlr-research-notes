1. Classical hypercontractivity and the exact tail estimate
Use the standard Gaussian Ornstein--Uhlenbeck hypercontractivity theorem
in its correlation parametrization:
  ||T_theta h||_q <= ||h||_p
whenever 1<p<=q and 0<=theta<=sqrt((p-1)/(q-1)).
This is the classical Nelson theorem, used as an external standard input;
the new claim here is its application and combination below. Bibliographic
origin: E. Nelson, The free Markoff field, Journal of Functional Analysis
12(2) (1973), 211--227, DOI10.1016/0022-1236(73)90025-6. The precise Gaussian
correlation normalization is also explicitly stated in the introduction
of the primary research paper On Weissler's Conjecture on the Hamming Cube I,
IMRN2022(9),6991, https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2022/9/6991/6049955 .
Only the standard theorem in the first display is imported.

Let a_k(f)>=0 be the summed Hermite weight of an assignment. If
p_i<=C0/m and s=1/log m, then
  S_s(f)=sum_i||T_sqrt(s) f_i||_2^2
        <=sum_i ||f_i||_(1+s)^2
        <=sum_i p_i^(2/(1+s))
        <= C0^2 exp(2)/m.                              (1)
Here 0<=f_i<=1 gives E f_i^(1+s)<=p_i; and
m^(2s/(1+s))<=exp(2), C0>=1. For m sufficiently large, r<=1/(m-1)<=s,
so for every integer k0>=0,

 sum_{k>=k0} r^k a_k(f)
       <= (r/s)^k0 S_s(f)
       <= C0^2 exp(2) r^k0 log^k0(m)/m.                (2)

This is an upper bound for the entire positive tail. It therefore also
bounds the negative odd tail when used in a lower estimate for S_{-r}.
For the balanced reference g, the same calculation has C0=1.

2. The exact limiting reference degree-two defect
Continue the notation of the core source:
  mu=E M_m, v=Var(M_m), u=mu^2-1,
  w=mu^2/(m-1), L=mw-1, F0=L^2/(2m), Delta2=A2g-F0.
The pinned tail proof gives second-moment convergence of
Z_m=b_m(M_m-b_m) to G with distribution exp(-exp(-z)), and mu/b_m->1.
Consequently
  b_m^2 v -> Var(G),       u/b_m^2 ->1.
For completeness Var(G)=pi^2/6 follows directly without a new tail claim.
Write G=-log E with E exponential of mean1, and let E' be an independent
copy. The ratio E/E' has density (1+t)^-2 on t>0. Hence
  Var(G)=1/2 int_0^infty (log t)^2/(1+t)^2 dt
        =int_0^1 (log t)^2/(1+t)^2 dt
        =2 sum_{n>=1} (-1)^(n-1)/n^2=pi^2/6.
The series step can be obtained first with (1+eta t)^-2, eta<1, then by
dominated convergence; the resulting series in eta is absolutely
summable at1. The final alternating Basel sum is elementary.

Multiply the exact identity (3) of the core source by m. Its first,
third and fourth terms tend0, since u=O(log m). The v^2 contribution
tends0, and the uv contribution tends Var(G). Therefore

  m Delta2 -> pi^2/6.                                 (3)

3. Uniform full-voting-range localization with an explicit asymptotic bound
Let f be any competitive assignment S_{-r}(f)<=S_{-r}(g), and put
  delta=|p-U|^2, D=delta-r(W-w),
  c_{m,r}=L[1/r-(L+2)/2].
The already accepted first localization places all p_i in [c/m,C0/m]
with fixed c=1/100,C0=2, uniformly over 0<r<=1/(m-1). Retain degree2
and use (2) with k0=3 for the competitor and k0=4 for the reference.
The convex tangent argument from the core source now yields

 (1-rL)D+r^2 c_{m,r} delta
 <= r^2 Delta2
    +C0^2 exp(2) r^3 log^3(m)/m
    +exp(2) r^4 log^4(m)/m.                           (4)

All terms and factors in (4) are explicit. For sufficiently large m,
c_{m,r}>=0 and 1-rL>0. Divide by r^2/m. Since
r log^3 m=O(log^3 m/m), r^2 log^4 m=O(log^4 m/m^2), and rL=o(1),
(3)-(4) give

  D <= [pi^2/6+o(1)] r^2/m,                           (5)

where the o(1) depends on m alone and is uniform over both r in the entire
stated interval and all competitive assignments in every ambient dimension.
The accepted mass-window inequality, with A_m=O(log^2 m), gives the joint
conclusion

 (1-rA_m)delta+r[V_m(p)-W]
                    <= [pi^2/6+o(1)] r^2/m.           (6)

In particular
  delta <= [pi^2/6+o(1)] r^2/m,
  V_m(p)-W <= [pi^2/6+o(1)] r/m,
  |W-w| <= [pi^2/6+o(1)] r/m.                         (7)
The last conclusion uses delta<=D/(1-rA_m): since rA_m=o(1),
|delta-D|<=D for all sufficiently large m, as already in the core source.
At r=1/(m-1), these imply
  limsup m^3 D <=pi^2/6,
  limsup m^3 |p-U|^2 <=pi^2/6,
  limsup m^2 [V_m(p)-W] <=pi^2/6.
The limsups allow an arbitrary sequence of competitive assignments and
ambient dimensions. The same bounds with m^3 scaling hold uniformly
over smaller r, though the r-dependent forms (5)-(7) are more informative.

4. Scope
This improves the additive localization bound. It neither proves D=0 nor
controls the higher-chaos difference relative to D. In particular (4)
retains a reference degree-two Jensen defect, asymptotic to pi^2/(6m),
even at the reference assignment. No completed Standard Simplex or
Plurality theorem is claimed, and no optimality of the constant for
actual competitive assignments is asserted.
