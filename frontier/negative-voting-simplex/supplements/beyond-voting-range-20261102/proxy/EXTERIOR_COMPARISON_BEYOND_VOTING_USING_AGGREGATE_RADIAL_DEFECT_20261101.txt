EXTERIOR COMPARISON AT r<=m^(-7/8)/(1+log m)^2
1 November 2026. Scoped proof audit and conditional extension.

VERDICT

The exterior comparison extends to the displayed range once its
localization hypotheses are supplied. The old individual row-lower-bound
input can be removed from the proxy step by the accompanying elementary
unit-direction median theorem. Both final error coefficients tend to
zero at the proposed endpoint. No step of the exterior tensor or signed
tail calculation intrinsically requires r<=1/(m-1).

This is NOT a proof that all competitive assignments satisfy the required
localization in that larger range, and NOT an extension of the separate
local full-noise theorem. Those are independent gates. In particular the
old coarse mass-window proof cannot be imported merely because the
exterior estimates close. The theorem here states its actual premises.

CONDITIONAL EXTERIOR THEOREM

Let L=1+log m, r_max=m^(-7/8)/L^2, and 0<r<=r_max. For all sufficiently
large m, finite N>=m-1, let f:R^N->Delta_m be any measurable assignment.
Write p=Ef, B_i=E[X f_i], W=||B||^2, U_i=1/m,
delta=||p-U||^2, Delta=w-W, D=delta+r Delta, J=D/r,
R=R_U(B), and d=dist(B,regular centroid orbit). Assume

  p_i in[1/(2m),3/(2m)],
  0<=D<=C0 r^2/m, delta<=2D, |W-w|<=D/r, d>1/m.          (A)

Retain the previously proved covariance/offset/summed-row inputs and
the external equal-threshold Gaussian maximum comparison used in the
frozen exterior proof. Then, with constants depending only on C0 and
the fixed absolute input constants,

  S_-r(f)-S_-r(g)
    >=D[1-CrL^3-Cr^2m^(7/4)L^(7/2)]>=D/2>0.             (B)

The conclusion concerns actual measurable assignments, including
fractional ones and unequal masses. No rank, interface, purity,
stationarity, pair separation, or minimizer existence premise is used.

1. NORMALIZED ROW CAP AND REMAINDER BUDGET

Assumptions (A) imply W/w=1+O(r/L), uniformly up to r_max. Thus
s=sqrt(W/w) is bounded above and below. The actual centroid entropy
bound yields |B_i|<=C alpha, hence a fixed normalized row cap for
Bhat=B/s. Conditional Cauchy, valid also for fractional f, gives

  B^T B<=p_max I, ||Bhat||op^2<=C/m.                      (1)

The balanced dual offset certificate and feasibility F_p(B)>=W give

  R<=Delta/2+CL sqrt(R delta)
   <=Delta+C L^2 delta<=Delta+delta/r=J.                  (2)

The last step needs CrL^2<=1, which holds uniformly in the proposed
range since r_max L^2=m^(-7/8). There is no individual row-lower-bound
input here. Nonnegativity of R is the covariance tangent theorem.

Normalize only in the summed-row theorem. The exact radial identity
gives R_U(Bhat)<=C R, and the fixed cap yields

  E_rad=sum_i(|Bhat_i|-beta)^2<=C L^2 R<=C rL^2/m,
  E_rad/w<=C rL<=C m^(-7/8)/L=o(1).                     (3)

Also ||Bhat||op^2/w<=C/L=o(1). Apply the accompanying theorem
GLOBAL_EQUAL_ROW_PROXY_WITHOUT_INDIVIDUAL_ROW_LOWER_BOUND_20261101.txt.
It gives, in exactly the same row span,

  sum_i Z_i=0, |Z_i|=beta,
  ||Z-Bhat||^2<=C E_rad.

Adding ||B-Bhat||^2<=2R produces the sole proxy estimate needed below:

  E_Z:=||Z-B||^2<=C L^2 R<=C L^2 J.                      (4)

The old one-row certificate would only give a worst-row error of order
sqrt(mr/L), which need not vanish at r_max. It is not used. Aggregate
radial smallness and the actual-centroid operator bound replace it.

2. EXTERIOR COVARIANCE FLOOR

Set q=d/alpha and e=||BB^T/alpha^2-P||_F. Procrustes and (A) give
e>=q and c/sqrt(L)<=q<=C sqrt(m). The global unnormalized covariance
remainder gives

  R>=c alpha^2 e^2/m min(1,c/(Le))
   >=c q/m^3>=c/(m^3 sqrt(L)),
  J>=R>0.                                                (5)

Neither the rank of B nor a row lower bound enters this argument.
In particular the absolute Gaussian errors below remain payable even
as r tends to zero within the proposed range.

3. SECOND-CHAOS TEST AT AN EQUAL-ROW PROXY

The existing common-threshold maximum comparison applies to the centered
equal-row matrix Z, whether or not it is singular or has duplicates.
For V=max_i Z_i.X and regular V_*, it yields EV<=w and

  (EV_*^2-EV^2)_+
   <=C beta T(w-EV)+C beta^2 m exp(-T^2/2), T>=1.         (6)

This comparison is entirely independent of the noise correlation r.
For c_f=V-sum_i f_i Z_i.X>=0, its two nonnegative mean defects satisfy

  Ec_f+(w-EV)=w-<Z,B>
             =(Delta+E_Z)/2<=C L^2 J.                   (7)

The pointwise quadratic estimate and the unit-marginal Gaussian union
bound give

  EV^2-E sum_i f_i(Z_i.X)^2
   <=2beta T Ec_f+C beta^2 m(T^2+1)exp(-T^2/2).            (8)

At T=sqrt(32L), the score-square loss is at most
C L^3 J/m+C beta^2 mL exp(-16L). The direct tensor test has numerator

  sum_i <E[f_i(XX^T-I)], Z_i Z_i^T-Z^TZ/m>
    =E sum_i f_i(Z_i.X)^2-beta^2,

using only sum_i p_i=1, and denominator at most
D_*=alpha^4(m-1)(m-2)/m. Its regular coefficient is at most Cm.
Cauchy and the positive-part-square inequality therefore give

  A2(f)-A2(g)>=-C L^3 J-C L^2 exp(-16L)
             >=-C L^3 J.                                (9)

The final absorption uses (5). No dependence on the voting endpoint
or on individual row lengths occurs anywhere in (6)-(9).

4. ENTIRE SIGNED HIGHER TAIL

For a closest regular centroid B0 and its rule g0, let
epsilon=||f-g0||_1/2. The actual first moments give exactly

  R0=w-<B0,B>=(Delta+d^2)/2>=0,
  epsilon<=min(1,C sqrt(m R0)).                            (10)

Take tau^2=1/(4L). The mass cap in (A) and rare-label hypercontractivity
give

  ||T_tau f||^2+||T_tau g0||^2<=C/m,
  ||T_tau(f-g0)||^2<=C epsilon/m.

Factoring all signed Hermite multipliers of degree at least three through
T_tau is valid whenever r/tau^2=4rL<=1. Here
4r_max L=4m^(-7/8)/L tends to zero, so this condition is satisfied uniformly.
It yields

  |H_r(f)-H_r(g0)|<=C r^3 L^3 sqrt(epsilon)/m.             (11)

If J>=d^2, then Delta<=J gives R0<=J; (5) yields

  |Hdiff|/J<=C r^3 L^3 m^(-3/4)J^(-3/4)
            <=C r^3m^(3/2)L^(27/8).

If J<d^2, then R0<=d^2. Write d=alpha q and use J>=cq/m^3,
q>=c/sqrt(L). This yields

  |Hdiff|/J<=C r^3m^(7/4)L^(13/4)q^(-1/2)
            <=C r^3m^(7/4)L^(7/2).

The second bound dominates the first. Hence

  |H_r(f)-H_r(g)|<=C r^3m^(7/4)L^(7/2)J.                 (12)

No individual proxy-row lower bound, full-rank score path, or old
upper bound r<=1/(m-1) is used in these two cases.

5. ENDPOINT ARITHMETIC AND EXACT ASSEMBLY

The exact signed decomposition is

  S_-r(f)-S_-r(g)=D+r^2[A2(f)-A2(g)]+H_r(f)-H_r(g).

Using (9), (12), and J=D/r proves the first inequality in (B).
At r=r_max the two relative errors satisfy exactly

  rL^3 <=m^(-7/8)L ->0,
  r^2m^(7/4)L^(7/2) <=L^(-1/2) ->0.                      (13)

Thus the coefficient is at least 1/2 for all sufficiently large m,
uniformly in 0<r<=r_max. The strict sign comes from D=rJ>0 in (5).
The bound is existential in m; no numerical threshold is claimed.

6. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN REMOVED

Removed from the exterior proxy route:
  - uniform individual lower bounds on actual or normalized row norms;
  - the one-row covariance coercivity theorem used to obtain them;
  - the need to apply the original median construction to possibly tiny
    or zero unnormalized rows.

Retained:
  - the fixed mass window and localized D-budget (A);
  - the fixed normalized row cap needed by summed-row coercivity;
  - actual-centroid operator control;
  - global balanced covariance concavity, offset and summed-row estimates;
  - the external equal-threshold Gaussian maximum theorem;
  - Gaussian hypercontractivity and the regular margin estimate.

For a full theorem beyond the voting interval one must separately prove
(A) for every competitive assignment and recheck the local full-noise
case d<=1/m at this same enlarged r-range. This report makes no assertion
that those tasks follow from (13). It establishes an actual proof of
the new geometry and of the conditional exterior extension.
