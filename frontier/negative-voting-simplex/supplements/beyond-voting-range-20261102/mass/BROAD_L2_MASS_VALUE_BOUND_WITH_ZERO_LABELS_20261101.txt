BROAD L2 MASS VALUE BOUND, INCLUDING ZERO AND LARGE LABELS
1 November 2026. New proof from the fixed-relative-window value theorem.
No original or frozen source is edited. This is a mathematical candidate
with an explicit proof, not external validation or publication.

THEOREM
There are absolute epsilon0>0,C<infinity,m0 such that for every m>=m0,
every finite N>=m-1, and every measurable simplex-valued assignment f
with masses p_i and delta=sum_i(p_i-1/m)^2<=epsilon0/m,

 W(f):=sum_i|E[X f_i]|^2 <= w_m+C(1+log m)^2 delta,
 w_m=(E max_(i<=m)G_i)^2/(m-1).                          (B1)

There is NO coordinate lower or upper bound on p. In particular p_i=0
is allowed. The same bound holds for the prescribed-mass supremum V_m(p)
over fractional assignments. One may take epsilon0=1/32; the absolute
C and eventual threshold depend on the fixed-window input below.

1. REMOVE ONLY LABELS OUTSIDE A FIXED RELATIVE WINDOW
Let A={i:p_i<1/(2m) or p_i>2/m}, h=|A|, G its complement, k=m-h,
M=sum_(i in A)p_i. Each bad label has |p_i-1/m|>=1/(2m), hence

 h<=4m^2 delta<=m/8, k>=7m/8.                            (B3)

For t=mp_i outside[1/2,2], the elementary inequality
 t<=2(t-1)^2
holds. Thus p_i<=2m(p_i-1/m)^2 on A and

 M<=2m delta<=1/16.                                    (B4)

The weaker M<=1/8 will suffice. If A is empty, the fixed-window theorem
already gives(B1). Hence assume A is nonempty. Then

 delta>=1/(4m^2).                                      (B5)

For j in G put q_j=p_j/(1-M). These sum to1. Equations(B3)-(B4) imply

 1/(4k)<=q_j<=3/k.

Indeed q_j>=1/(2m)>=1/(4k), while q_j<=16/(7m)<=3/k.
Let delta_q=sum_G(q_j-1/k)^2. Since subtracting the mean minimizes a
sum of squares,

 delta_q=(1-M)^(-2)sum_G[p_j-(1-M)/k]^2
         <=(1-M)^(-2)sum_G(p_j-1/m)^2<=2delta.           (B6)

2. REASSIGN THE BAD MASS FRACTIONALLY, WITH ITS FIRST MOMENT RETAINED
Put f_A=sum_A f_i, b=E[X f_A], b_i=E[X f_i], and for j in G set

 g_j=f_j+q_j f_A.

The functions g_j are nonnegative and sum to1 pointwise, so they are a
k-label simplex assignment. Its j-th mass is p_j+q_jM=q_j, and its
centroid is c_j=b_j+q_jb. Since sum_G b_j=-b, define

 v=sum_G(q_j-1/k)b_j,
 W_G=sum_G|b_j|^2, W_A=sum_A|b_i|^2.

Expansion gives the EXACT identity

 W_G=W(g)-2b.v+|b|^2/k-delta_q|b|^2.                    (B7)

The last term is nonpositive and may safely be discarded in an upper
bound. The sign of b.v is not assumed.

3. ALL REASSIGNMENT ERRORS COST AT MOST C L delta
Write L=1+log m. Gaussian exponential Jensen gives for every bounded
weight0<=a<=1 of mass u the dimension-free entropy estimate

 |E[X a]|<=u sqrt(2log(1/u)),                           (B8)

with the value0 at u=0. This follows by projecting in the first-moment
direction, applying Jensen to the probability density a/u, and minimizing
the exponential parameter. It applies to f_A as well as to each f_i.

For a bad low mass put t=mp_i<=1/2. Then
p_i^2 log(1/p_i)/(p_i-1/m)^2
=t^2[log m+log(1/t)]/(1-t)^2<=log m+log2<=L.
The term involving log(1/t) is bounded by log2 because
 t^2 log(1/t)<=log2/4 and (1-t)^2>=1/4.
For a bad high mass p_i>=2/m, p_i/(p_i-1/m)<=2 and
log(1/p_i)<=log m. Therefore(B8) gives

 W_A<=8L delta.                                        (B9)

If M=0 then b=0. Otherwise x^2 log(1/x) is increasing on0<x<=1/8.
Use the weaker bound M<=4m delta<=1/8 and(B5), which imply
1/m<=4m delta<=1/8. Then(B8) gives

 |b|^2<=2(4m delta)^2 log[1/(4m delta)]
       <=32m^2 delta^2 L.                              (B10)

This handles arbitrarily small M without incorrectly replacing log(1/M)
by log m before multiplying by M^2.

For every unit direction u, conditional Cauchy and p_j<=2/m on G give

 sum_G(b_j.u)^2<=sum_G p_j E[f_j(u.X)^2]
               <=(2/m)E[(sum_G f_j)(u.X)^2]<=2/m.

Consequently the retained centroid matrix has operator norm at most
sqrt(2/m), and

 |v|<=sqrt(2delta_q/m)<=2sqrt(delta/m).                  (B11)

Combining(B10)-(B11), m delta<=epsilon0, and k>=7m/8,

 2|b.v|<=C sqrt(m delta)sqrt(L)delta<=C L delta,
 |b|^2/k<=C m delta^2 L<=C L delta.                     (B12)

All constants are independent of dimension and of the individual masses.

4. PAY FOR THE CHANGE IN THE NUMBER OF LABELS
The means mu_j=E max_(i<=j)G_i are nonnegative and increasing for j>=2,
and mu_j^2<=2log j. Hence

 w_j-w_(j+1)
 =mu_j^2/(j-1)-mu_(j+1)^2/j
 <=mu_j^2/[j(j-1)].

No monotonicity assertion about w_j itself is needed. Telescoping from
k to m and using k>=7m/8 gives

 w_k-w_m<=C hL/m^2<=C L delta.                         (B13)

Apply(B2) to g, use(B6), and combine(B7),(B9),(B12),(B13):

 W(f)=W_G+W_A
 <=W(g)+2|b.v|+|b|^2/k+W_A
 <=w_k+C L^2 delta_q+C L delta
 <=w_m+C L^2 delta.

This proves(B1). Since it holds for every feasible f, it also proves the
same bound for V_m(p) by taking the supremum. No new attainment argument
or regularity of the original f is involved.

5. EDGE-CASE STRESS CHECKS AND LIMITS
If h labels have mass0 and the remaining k labels have mass1/k, then
M=0,b=0,delta=h/(mk),q is uniform, and the construction leaves the
remaining assignment unchanged. Thus the proof covers genuine deleted
labels; its sole cost there is w_k-w_m, paid by(B13).

If a bad mass is positive but arbitrarily tiny, its entropy logarithm
can diverge. Equation(B10) controls the product M^2 log(1/M), which is
monotone on the required interval. No false lower bound on M is used.
The lower bound(B5) is on delta, not on M.

The result is global on the displayed L2 mass ball, not on the entire
probability simplex. It supplies no individual centroid-row lower bound
and no full-noise inequality by itself. Its only substantial imported
analytic ingredient is the fixed-window first-moment bound(B2).
