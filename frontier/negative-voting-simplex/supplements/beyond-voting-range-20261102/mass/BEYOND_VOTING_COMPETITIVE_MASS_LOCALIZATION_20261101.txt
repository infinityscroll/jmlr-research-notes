THEOREM
Put L=1+log m and r_max=m^(-7/8)/L^2. There are absolute C,m0 such that,
for every m>=m0, finite N>=m-1,0<r<=r_max, and every measurable
simplex-valued f with S_-r(f)<=S_-r(g),

 p_i in[1/(2m),3/(2m)],
 0<=D<=C r^2/m, delta<=2D,
 0<=V_m(p)-W<=D/r, |W-w_m|<=D/r,
 W/w_m=1+O(r/L),                                     (L1)

where p_i=E f_i, B_i=E[X f_i], W=sum_i|B_i|^2,
delta=sum_i(p_i-1/m)^2 and D=delta+r(w_m-W).
All constants and the threshold are uniform in r and finite N.

1. COARSE LOCALIZATION INTO THE BROAD L2 MASS BALL
For any0<r<1, let a_j(f)>=0 be the summed degree-j Hermite weights.
Then a_0=P=sum p_i^2, a_1=W, and sum_j a_j(f)=sum_i E f_i^2<=1.
Parity therefore gives

 S_-r(f)>=P-rW-r^3,
 S_-r(g)<=1/m-rw_m+r^2 A2g+r^4.

Competitiveness implies

 delta-r(W-w_m)<=r^2 A2g+r^3+r^4.                      (L2)

The scalar Bregman/rearrangement calculation of45e2cc45 is independent
of r and gives W<=F_m+B_m delta, where
F_m=m I(1/m)^2 and B_m=max(2log m+1,6). Its Gaussian maximum asymptotics
also give F_m-w_m=O(1/m), A2g=O(L^2/m). Thus, whenever rB_m<=1/2,

 delta<=C[r/m+r^2 L^2/m+r^3].                         (L3)

For r<=r_max the denominator condition holds for large m and

 m delta<=C[r+r^2 L^2+mr^3]=o(1)

uniformly over the interval. Hence delta<=epsilon0/m eventually, so the
new broad mass-value bound applies. It yields

 D>= (1-CrL^2)delta+r[V_m(p)-W]
   >=delta/2+r[V_m(p)-W]>=0.                          (L4)

Here rL^2<=m^(-7/8)->0. This step is established without first assuming
that all masses are positive or comparable with1/m.

2. RETAIN DEGREE TWO TO REACH A FIXED RELATIVE MASS BOX
The universal conditional-Jensen inequality is

 A2(f)>=(W-P)_+^2/(2P).

It applies in every dimension to every fractional assignment. The positive
part makes its convex-perspective tangent valid even for W<=P. Put
ell=mw_m-1, F0=ell^2/(2m), Delta2=A2g-F0. The reference maximum variance
calculation of251c3c7c gives0<=Delta2<=C/m independently of r.
For every r>0 the exact tangent identity is

 A2(f)>=F0-(ell/r)D+c_(m,r)delta,
 c_(m,r)=ell[1/r-(ell+2)/2].                           (L5)

For0<r<=r_max and large m, ell>0,c_(m,r)>=0 and1-r ell>=1/2.
Retaining degree two in the same parity estimates yields

 (1-r ell)D+r^2 c_(m,r)delta
       <=r^2 Delta2+r^3+r^4.

Together with(L4), this gives the preliminary estimate

 D<=C(r^2/m+r^3), delta<=2D.                           (L6)

At the new upper endpoint,

 m^2(r^2/m+r^3)
 <=m^(-3/4)L^(-4)+m^(-5/8)L^(-6)=o(1).

Consequently m sqrt(delta)=o(1), and every mass satisfies
p_i in[1/(2m),3/(2m)] for all sufficiently large m. This preliminary
bootstrap is sufficient to unlock rare-label smoothing. It does not yet
claim the stronger D=O(r^2/m), since r^3 may dominate r^2/m here.

3. RARE-LABEL SMOOTHING PAYS THE ENTIRE ODD AND EVEN TAILS
Use classical dimension-free Gaussian hypercontractivity in correlation
coordinates: ||T_theta h||_q<=||h||_p for1<p<=q and
0<=theta<=sqrt((p-1)/(q-1)). The exact normalization and the primary
source read are recorded below.

Let t^2=1/(4L), and put q0=1+t^2. For any simplex assignment with
p_i<=3/(2m), boundedness0<=f_i<=1 gives

 sum_i||T_t f_i||_2^2
 <=sum_i||f_i||_(q0)^2
 <=sum_i p_i^(2/q0)
 <=m(3/(2m))^(2/q0)<=C/m.                              (L7)

The last constant is absolute because m^(2t^2/(1+t^2))<=exp(1/2).
The same bound holds for g. For r<=t^2, nonnegative Hermite weights imply,
for each integer K>=0,

 sum_(j>=K) r^j a_j(f)
 <=(r/t^2)^K sum_j t^(2j)a_j(f)
 <=C(4Lr)^K/m.                                        (L8)

The new range satisfies r<=t^2 for large m. In particular

 sum_(odd j>=3) r^j a_j(f)<=C r^3 L^3/m,
 sum_(even j>=4) r^j a_j(g)<=C r^4 L^4/m.              (L9)

These are bounds on the entire tails, not finite-chaos truncations.
Substitute them for the earlier r^3,r^4 allowances while retaining
the same tangent(L5). The result is

 (1-r ell)D+r^2 c_(m,r)delta
 <=r^2 Delta2+C r^3 L^3/m+C r^4 L^4/m.

Since rL^3<=m^(-7/8)L->0 and r^2L^4<=m^(-7/4)->0,
(L4) now gives

 0<=D<=C r^2/m.                                       (L10)

Therefore delta<=2D, V_m(p)-W<=D/r, and
|W-w_m|=|delta-D|/r<=D/r. Since w_m is comparable with L/m,
W/w_m=1+O(r/L). This proves every assertion of(L1).

4. EXACT OUTPUT FOR THE NEXT GEOMETRIC STEP, AND A REAL OBSTRUCTION
The actual entropy row cap and bounded scale let the rank-free balanced

These are legitimate downstream outputs. They do NOT imply the old

one-row estimate gives only

At r=r_max its upper bound equals C m^(1/16)/L^(3/2), which eventually

The broad mass theorem and this localization theorem do not by themselves
prove a larger-range full-noise result. A separate replacement proxy
construction must handle small or zero rows. At this source's writing,
that replacement is being developed independently; it is not assumed
proved here and no unverified source is cited as an input.
