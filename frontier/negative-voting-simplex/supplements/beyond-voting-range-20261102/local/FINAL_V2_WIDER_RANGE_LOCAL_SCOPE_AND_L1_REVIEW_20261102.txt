1. CORRECTION TO MY PREDECESSOR REVIEW: SIGNED ENERGY

The prior draft wrote D<=C[E+positive terms] while E had not been shown
nonnegative. My earlier review accepted the recoverable localization
conclusion without flagging that compression. A mass reviewer correctly
identified the need to retain E's coefficient. The earlier blanket
assessment of this particular display is superseded here; its remaining
independent calculations remain unchanged.

Let a=1-r ell, where 1/2<=a<=1, and write P for the sum of the nonnegative
rare-label allowances. The refined tangent estimate has the form

 aD+r^2 c_{m,r}delta<=E+P, with D>=0 and c_{m,r}delta>=0.

Dropping the nonnegative term gives exactly

It is not legitimate in general to replace this by C(E+P') merely by

The second line now bounds a sum of positive upper allowances and uses
a>=1/2. This is valid for positive, zero, or negative E. The needed
rL^3=o(1) and r^2L^4=o(1) hold uniformly at the proposed endpoint.
The initial coarse localization also retains the exact E coefficient,
