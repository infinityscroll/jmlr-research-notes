Checked correspondences:

   Its quadratic form divided by 2(alpha+beta+gamma) is precisely

   form of sum M_ik (partial_l A_ij)(partial_j A_kl).

   triple contributes -2(beta*a*b+gamma*b*c+alpha*c*a), with a=partial_k A_ij,
   b=partial_i A_jk, c=partial_j A_ki. The signs match skew symmetry.
5. The sampled third-derivative tensor is fully symmetric, and dm is the
   correct cyclic derivative-of-M contribution to d(MA+AM). Its cancellation
   therefore tests the intended Codazzi identity, not an unrelated index sum.
6. Count: 7 symbolic assertions; 6*sum_{d=2}^8 binomial(d,3)=756 sampled
   triples with two assertions each; 42 fixtures with two additional

Scope remains exactly algebraic. The 42 rational fixtures are deterministic
finite checks, not an exhaustive proof over all derivative tensors or
functional-analytic data. The symbolic positive-semidefinite identity is
universal algebra, but neither it nor the samples verify existence,
regularity, cutoff limits, variational transfer, or the imported transport
regularity theorem. The checker's existing scope statement is accurate.

Minor operational safeguard: all checks use Python assert. Running with

replace assertions by unconditional checked exceptions. Ordinary python3
