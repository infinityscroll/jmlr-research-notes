INDEPENDENT HODGE ROUTE TO GAUSSIAN COVARIANCE CONCAVITY: ROOT REVIEW
Date: 2026-11-02

RESULT AND VERIFICATION BOUNDARY

The new argument supplies an independent route to the foundational statement
that F_nu(C)=MCov(N(0,C),nu) is concave in ordinary positive-semidefinite C,
for every finite-dimensional probability law nu with finite second moment.
It does not use the original finite-target chain-complex proof, the proposed
global nuclear-variation inequality, or numerical evidence of concavity.

The analytic chain is internally audited. Its all-target extension imports
the stated regularity theorem in Maja Gwozdz, arXiv:2609.04052v1 (2026), as
an external theorem. We checked its statement and applicability, not its
full proof. This is a recent preprint dependency, and is explicitly retained
as such. The new route is therefore conditional on that external statement
being correct. This review is not external mathematical validation or a
novelty determination. The previously published main theorem is unchanged.

1. THE HESSIAN-WEIGHTED HODGE ESTIMATE

Let gamma=N(0,I_d), M=D^2 phi smooth, and mI<=M<=LI with 0<m<=L<infinity.
For a skew field A set
  (delta_gamma A)_i=sum_j (x_j-partial_j)A_ij,
  J(A)=E tr(A^T M A).
Use the exterior-form norm sum_{i<j} A_ij^2; S_p(M) denotes the additive
action of M on p-forms, with eigenvalues equal to p-fold sums of those of M.

The root independently reconstructed the identity
  E[(delta_gamma A)^T M(delta_gamma A)] = J(A)+R(A),
  R(A)=E sum_{i,k,j,l} M_ik (partial_l A_ij)(partial_j A_kl).
Both derivative-of-M terms disappear by the full symmetry of D^3 phi and
skew symmetry of A. Positivity of a matrix field alone would not suffice.

In a fixed orthonormal frame diagonalizing M at one point, repeated-index
terms in R are positive squares. A triple of distinct indices contributes
  q=-2(beta*a*b+gamma*b*c+alpha*c*a),
while the corresponding component of d(S_2(M)A) is
  k=(alpha+beta)a+(beta+gamma)b+(gamma+alpha)c.
The Hessian symmetry also cancels every derivative-of-M term in this last
formula. No spatial derivative of a moving eigenvector basis is taken.

For alpha,beta,gamma>0,
  q+k^2/[2(alpha+beta+gamma)]>=0.
The associated symmetric matrix has null vector (1,1,1), positive diagonal,
and principal 2-by-2 minors alpha*beta*gamma/(alpha+beta+gamma)>0.
This proves the augmented estimate
  ||delta_gamma A||_M^2
  +(1/2)||d(S_2(M)A)||_{S_3(M)^(-1)}^2 >= J(A).

For an arbitrary L2 Gaussian-solenoidal v, minimize J(A) subject to
delta_gamma A=v. The constraint is nonempty: with L_OU=Delta-x.grad,
u=(1-L_OU)^(-1)v and A_ij=partial_j u_i-partial_i u_j give a feasible L2
field by Gaussian Hermite expansion. In particular, the resolvent is
(1-L_OU)^(-1), not (-L_OU)^(-1). The affine constraint is closed in L2,
and J is an equivalent Hilbert norm. Thus a unique minimizer exists.

Variations by Gaussian codifferentials of compact smooth 3-forms give
d(S_2(M)A)=0 distributionally. To apply the augmented estimate, cut off A
and use local Friedrichs graph approximation for
  A -> (delta_gamma A,d(S_2(M)A)).
The cutoff commutators are controlled by |grad chi_R||A| and
|grad chi_R||M||A|, so they vanish in L2. On each compact set all
coefficients and the Gaussian weight are smooth. Graph approximation
therefore gives delta_gamma A_n->v and d(S_2(M)A_n)->0. The correction
term is at most (6m)^(-1)||d(S_2(M)A_n)||_2^2. Passing to the limit yields
  J(A)<=E v^T Mv.                                      (H)
This closes the weak-L2 step, without assuming a smooth minimizer or a
complete flow for an arbitrary polynomial vector field.

2. THE DISCRIMINANT AND LOCAL COVARIANCE CURVATURE

For a symmetric constant H and T=grad phi, the same weak integration by
parts and (H) imply
  |E(HT).v|^2 <= c_* E v^T Mv,
  c_*=E sum_{i<j} [(lambda_i-lambda_j)^2/(lambda_i+lambda_j)] H_ij^2.
Here H is expressed in the pointwise eigenbasis only for algebra. Put
a=E tr(H^2 M). Then c_*<=a and
  a-c_* = E[sum_i lambda_i H_ii^2
             +sum_{i<j} 4lambda_i lambda_j H_ij^2/(lambda_i+lambda_j)]
         >=m||H||_F^2.

The root independently derived the exact coupling identity, with Q_t=sqrt(I+tH),
  F_nu(I+tH)=E T(G).Q_t G
    +sup_(G,S both gamma){E[(Q_t-I)T(G)].(S-G)-E D_phi(S,G)},
where D_phi(S,G)=phi(S)-phi(G)-T(G).(S-G). This is the usual Bregman
divergence, convex in S. Uniform convexity makes an optimizing displacement
O(t) in L2. Conditional velocities E[(S-G)/t|G] have weak L2 subsequential
limits v with div_gamma v=0, because the two marginals are equal.

The second-order energy limit is justified by Fenchel duality, not by a
uniform Taylor expansion in random displacements. For any q in L2, the
dual Bregman remainder at T(G)+tq is dominated by t^2|q|^2/(2m) and tends
to t^2 q^T M^-1 q/2. Optimizing the dual bound gives liminf energy at least
E v^T Mv/2. Combining this with the discriminant proves
  limsup_(t->0) [F_nu(I+tH)-F_nu(I)-(t/2)tr(H E M)]/t^2
     <=-(a-c_*)/8.
The coefficient refers to the t^2 quotient; a classical second derivative,
when it exists, is bounded above by -(a-c_*)/4. No differentiation of a
Brenier map with respect to covariance is used.

3. GLOBALIZATION: THE EXPLICIT IMPORTED THEOREM

The primary source checked is:
  Maja Gwozdz, Caffarelli Estimates under Lipschitz Perturbations,
  arXiv:2609.04052v1, 3 September 2026,
  https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04052v1
  Theorem 3.2, Corollary 3.6, Proposition 3.8.
The stated forward and reverse Brenier estimates give uniform positive
lower and finite upper Hessian bounds between Gaussian laws and smooth
compact log-density perturbations of elliptical Gaussian laws. Smooth
positive densities supply the required local smoothness. This is the only
new external regularity input; its proof was not audited in this work.

The target density class, rather than a class of transport potentials, is
closed under invertible linear changes of variables. Consequently the
local inequality applies at every positive-definite covariance by
  F_nu(C0^(1/2) D C0^(1/2))=F_((C0^(1/2))#nu)(D).
There is no claim that compositions of Brenier maps are Brenier, or that
quadratic-outside-compact potentials are closed under target transforms.

At every interior point of a covariance segment the upper Peano second
quotient is nonpositive. This implies concavity: if a chord is violated,
subtract it, then subtract a sufficiently small positive quadratic. An
interior minimum gives a strictly positive lower second quotient, a
contradiction. The local expansion itself supplies the first derivative.

For an arbitrary P2 target, choose compact smooth probability densities
f_n converging in W2 and set rho_n=(1-epsilon_n)f_n+epsilon_n gamma.
This is exactly a smooth compact log perturbation of gamma, since
  q_n=-log[1+((1-epsilon_n)/epsilon_n)f_n/gamma_density]
is smooth and compactly supported. The second moments stay bounded and
rho_n->nu in W2. The bound
  |F_nu(C)-F_eta(C)|<=sqrt(tr C) W2(nu,eta)
passes concavity to all targets. Gaussian-source W2 continuity extends
the result to singular positive-semidefinite covariances. Strictness is
not asserted to survive these limits.

4. CHECKS AND WHAT THEY DO NOT ESTABLISH

The core Hodge argument, the planar special case, and the Bregman transfer
were derived independently by separate branches and reconstructed here.
The separate audit gives an elliptic-regularity/cutoff proof of the same
Hilbert-space closure; the graph-norm proof above is sufficient on its own.

verify_hodge_algebra.py uses only the Python standard library. It checks
seven polynomial identities with rational coefficients, plus rational
tensor fixtures in dimensions 2 through 8: 1,603 exact checks in total.
Those checks cover the three-index matrix minors and determinant, Codazzi
cancellation, and the remainder decomposition. They do not formally verify
the analytic proof or the external regularity theorem.

No global weighted partition inequality, global nuclear-rearrangement
theorem, new noise-correlation range, or new conjecture-resolution claim is
made by this supplement. The original negative-voting candidate remains
subject to mathematical validation and defensible priority assessment.
The broad weighted-perimeter route is no longer needed for this independent
covariance proof; its finite searches must not become another completion gate.
