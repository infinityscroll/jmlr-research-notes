A COMPLETE RECURSIVE SUBCLASS, AND THE PRECISE MISSING GENERALIZATION
30 October 2026. Independent derivation and calibration, no novelty claim.
The unrestricted mixed-graph identification problem remains unresolved.

1. General exact block-factorization lemma
========================================
Suppose vertices have an ordered partition C1,...,Ct such that directed
edges only stay inside a block or go to a later block, and every
bidirected edge stays inside one block. Thus the Gaussian errors in
different blocks are independent, while errors within a block need not
be. Let A_j be the union of the blocks preceding C_j. From the observed
covariance define the conditional regression and covariance
  R_j=Sigma_(A_j,A_j)^(-1) Sigma_(A_j,C_j),
  Q_j=Sigma_(C_j,C_j)-Sigma_(C_j,A_j)R_j.
For j=1, omit R_j and use Q_j=Sigma_(C_j,C_j).
All Q_j are positive definite.

Write T_j=I-Lambda_(C_j,C_j), and B_j=Lambda_(A_j,C_j).
The structural equations give exactly
  R_j=B_j T_j^(-1),  Q_j=T_j^(-T)Omega_j T_j^(-1).
Therefore the entire real fiber is the Cartesian product, over j, of
the following block solution sets:
  T_j has its required directed sparsity, diagonal1, and is invertible;
  B_j=R_j T_j satisfies every required zero of the incoming arrows;
  Omega_j=T_j^T Q_j T_j satisfies every required error-covariance zero.
Positivity of Omega_j is automatic. Once these choices are made, the
conditional Gaussian laws and hence the joint covariance are fixed.
This proves both necessity and sufficiency, including global branches.
It also explains why downstream independent-error blocks cannot resolve
ambiguity in an earlier block: their solution sets do not use the
earlier block's parameter values.

Ordinary independent-error sinks are singleton blocks. Their incoming
coefficients are uniquely their observed regression coefficients; their
removal preserves the whole earlier fiber. Removing a source or an
error-correlated sink and discarding its cross-covariance is NOT covered.

2. Recursively defined graph class
==================================
Build a graph by repeatedly adjoining a block, with arbitrary forward
arrows from existing vertices and no error correlation across blocks.
Each new block is one of these types:
  E. No internal arrows; arbitrary allowed bidirected edges.
  C. A directed simple cycle of length at least2; no internal bidirected
     edges. There are exactly the cycle arrows, no directed chords.
  F. Arbitrary internal arrows; EVERY pair inside the block has an
     allowed bidirected edge (fully unrestricted internal error matrix).
The decomposition is a supplied certificate defining the subclass.
Singletons are type E. Graphs can have arbitrarily many vertices,
cycles, and unknown allowed error correlations. A complete
necessary-and-sufficient generic identification test follows below.

3. Type E is always identified
=============================
Here T=I, so B=R and Omega=Q. The allowed zeros merely constrain the
observed distribution; there is no parameter ambiguity.

4. Complete type-C cycle criterion
=================================
Call an earlier vertex a partial parent of a cycle if it has arrows to
a nonempty proper subset of the cycle vertices. Then:
  (i) a cycle with a partial parent is generically rationally identified;
  (ii) a cycle of length at least3 without a partial parent has exactly
       two admissible real branches generically;
  (iii) a 2-cycle without a partial parent has a one-dimensional fiber.

Proof of (i). Number a cycle modulo m, with arrow coefficient a_i from
i to i+1. Its incoming coefficient matrix is B=R T. Hence
  B_(u,j)=R_(u,j)-a_(j-1)R_(u,j-1).
For a partial parent u choose a missing target j. Since u has at least
one incoming arrow into the cycle, R_(u,j-1) is generically nonzero:
the directed cycle reaches every vertex, and setting one nonzero
incoming coefficient gives a nonzero path expression. Consequently
  a_(j-1)=R_(u,j)/R_(u,j-1).
Thus one cycle coefficient is identified by a rational formula.

For m>=3, set d_i=1/(Omega_ii), and K=Q^(-1). Direct multiplication gives
  K_(i,i+1)=-a_i d_(i+1),
  K_ii=d_i+a_i^2 d_(i+1).
Known nonzero a_i gives d_(i+1)=-K_(i,i+1)/a_i. Iterate
  d_(i+2)=K_(i+1,i+2)^2/(K_(i+1,i+1)-d_(i+1)),
  a_(i+1)=-K_(i+1,i+2)/d_(i+2).
At a true nonzero-edges parameter point the denominators are positive
and nonzero. These formulas recover the entire cycle, then B and Omega.
For m=2, knowing a and writing the other coefficient as b, its one
noise-zero equation instead gives
  b=(Q12-a Q11)/(Q22-a Q12).
Positive definiteness of Q ensures the denominator is nonzero at any
admissible solution, so the 2-cycle is also recovered.

5. Exact two-branch calculation for an uninstrumented cycle
=========================================================
For m>=3 assume all a_i are nonzero, p=product a_i is not1, and p^2
is not1. These remove only proper algebraic exceptional sets.
Suppose another cycle has the same Q, with noise precisions d_i'>0.
The off-diagonal precision entries force
  a_i'=a_i d_(i+1)/d_(i+1)'.
Set x_i=d_i'/d_i and r_i=a_i^2 d_(i+1)/d_i, R=product r_i=p^2.
The diagonal precision equations become
  x_i-1=r_i (x_(i+1)-1)/x_(i+1).
If any x_i=1, all are1 and the branch is the original. Otherwise set
  y_i=1/(x_i-1).
The equations become the affine recurrence
  y_(i+1)=r_i y_i-1.
There is exactly one cyclic solution when R!=1:
  y_i=S_i/(R-1),
  S_i=1+r_(i-1)+r_(i-1)r_(i-2)+...+
                 r_(i-1)...r_(i-m+1)>0.
Hence x_i=1+(R-1)/S_i is positive. For R>1 it exceeds1; for 0<R<1,
S_i>=1 makes its numerator S_i+R-1 strictly positive. Multiplying
the original recurrence gives product x_i=R, so
  product a_i'=1/p.
The second T is invertible since p!=1, and its noise variances are
positive. Thus there are exactly TWO admissible real branches.
This independently recovers the known directed-cycle obstruction.

If no partial external parent exists, every incoming row B_u is either
fully unrestricted or zero on the cycle. The corresponding condition
B_u=R_u T accepts BOTH internal branches: unrestricted rows have no
zeros to violate; zero rows have R_u=0. Thus both branches survive in
the full block. Conversely part4 shows any partial row generically
removes the second branch, proving the claimed complete criterion.

For m=2, every real a except a possible single pole gives b by the
displayed formula. When Q12!=0 the pole is a=Q22/Q12; its numerator
does not vanish there because det Q>0. Moreover
  1-ab=(Q22-2aQ12+a^2Q11)/(Q22-aQ12),
whose numerator is positive. Every other real a is admissible and
has positive-definite diagonal noise by congruence. If Q12=0 there
is no pole. This proves the one-dimensional obstruction.

6. Complete type-F criterion, including arbitrary cyclic arrows
=============================================================
For target vertex j in a fully confounded block let P_j be its internal
parents, H_j its allowed external parents, and U_j=A\H_j. Its internal
coefficient vector beta_j satisfies exactly the LINEAR equations
  R_(U_j,P_j) beta_j = R_(U_j,j).                       (1)
There are no internal error zeros. Therefore the block is generically
identified if and only if EVERY matrix R_(U_j,P_j) has full column rank.
If any rank is deficient, the product of these affine solution sets has
positive dimension. A small real movement from the true solution retains
invertibility of T and positivity of T^TQT, so this is actual
nonidentifiability, not merely a deficient sufficient test.

This rank has a structural characterization. Form a directed network
whose source nodes are U_j, whose other nodes are the current block,
and whose edges are the allowed incoming arrows and internal arrows.
The generic rank of R_(U_j,P_j) equals the maximum number of mutually
vertex-disjoint directed paths from distinct sources in U_j to distinct
targets in P_j. Consequently the condition is the existence of |P_j|
such paths for every j. Empty P_j passes vacuously.

Proof of the generic-rank statement. A vertex separator of size q
factors the walk-transfer matrix through q coordinates, by decomposing
every walk at its first hit of the separator; this is an identity of
formal power series and hence rational functions. Rank is at most q.
Conversely, on q vertex-disjoint paths set their edge coefficients to1
and every other allowed coefficient to0. The selected transfer minor
is a permutation matrix, since the surviving graph is a disjoint union
of directed paths. This supplies a nonzero determinant specialization.
Directed vertex Menger equates the maximum linking and minimum cut.
Cycles do not disturb either argument, and the specialization has
invertible T. This is the classical structural transfer-rank mechanism,
not a newly discovered general identification theorem; compare
https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/6465/6465D.pdf .

The generic fiber dimension of a type-F block is precisely
  sum_j (|P_j|-maximum_linking_size(U_j,P_j)).
It is singleton if this is zero, and infinite otherwise; there is no
finite degree greater than1, because the fiber is affine with the
det(T)=0 hypersurface removed.

7. Complete theorem for the recursive class
==========================================
A graph with the certified decomposition of part2 is generically
globally identifiable if and only if:
  every type-C block has a partial external parent; and
  every type-F block passes the linking condition in part6.
Necessity follows from the exact product-fiber lemma and the explicit
alternative branches/affine directions. Sufficiency gives rational
recovery block by block. If all type-F tests pass, no uninstrumented
2-cycle occurs, and u uninstrumented cycles of lengths>=3 occur,
the full graph has exactly 2^u admissible real branches generically.
Failed type-F tests and uninstrumented 2-cycles give positive-dimensional
fibers, whose dimensions add across blocks.

8. Checks and indispensable generalization
==========================================
cycle_block_audit.py verifies the reciprocal branch formulas for signed
rational cycles of every length3,...,8, every single-edge recovery
position, and every nonempty proper incoming support through length6.
It checks the 2-cycle continuum and a ten-vertex exact fixture with a
correlated root block, one identified cycle, one ambiguous cycle and
an independent-error sink. Two distinct full parameterizations have
exactly the same covariance. All checks pass; the proof is analytic,
not inferred from finitely many instances.

What cannot be discarded:
* Cross-block error correlations destroy the independent conditional
  factorization. The one-error-probe example from FIBER_COMPOSITION.txt
  already rescues an otherwise ambiguous ancestral block.
* Partial internal error sparsity combined with general internal arrows
  lies between the fully confounded linear criterion and the diagonal
  cycle recurrence. Its quadratic constraints must be used jointly.
* Multiple overlapping cycles inside one block are outside the cycle
  primitive; pure cycle degree2 is not a theorem about arbitrary SCCs.
* Jacobian rank only settles local dimension, not competing real branches.

A concrete contrast: one partial parent identifies a diagonal-noise
cycle completely, but if the same cycle's error matrix becomes fully
unrestricted, every cycle coefficient at a vertex targeted by ALL active
external parents remains free. At the other vertices, the incoming zero
constraints identify coefficients linearly. This exposes exactly how
error-covariance zeros carry information missing from regressions.

9. Current-method comparison and prior-art boundary
==================================================
The April2026 IIC paper (arXiv:2604.09309v1) propagates known coefficients
through sufficient rules. The May2026 latent-factor paper
(arXiv:2605.28105v1) allows unidentified parents in sufficient criteria.
Their settings and guarantees do not imply the complete real-fiber
necessity claims above for arbitrary graphs, nor does this subclass
supersede those methods. Explicit latent factors can restrict Omega
beyond a bidirected zero pattern.

The nearest warning against a novelty claim is Gupta--Blaeser,
AAAI2024: the directed-TREE case with arbitrary confounding already has
a complete randomized polynomial-time algorithm, returning identifiable,
2-identifiable or infinite possibilities for each coefficient.
https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/30023/31799
Thus a tree-only extension or fractional-linear propagation itself is
not a new full solution. The cycle and block derivations here are an
independent exact testbed and a boundary for the missing generalization.

No complete characterization of unrestricted mixed graphs is proved.
