Bounded primary-source review, 30 October 2026

Ian M. Wanless, Lieb's permanental dominance conjecture, arXiv2202.01867v1,
3 February2022, Section2 Conjecture3:
https://arxiv.org/html/2202.01867
States Marcus's arbitrary-m block-permanent conjecture; records Lieb's
m2 result and a real-matrix result of Pate in a different parameter range.

Fuzhen Zhang, An update on a few permanent conjectures, Special Matrices4
(2016),305-316, Section7:
https://doi.org/10.1515/spma-2016-0030
Records the Marcus per-in-per conjecture as dating to1965 and discusses
the two-block case and earlier weaker/special-case results.

Yair Lavi, Minc's list, updated29 October2026:
https://yair-lavi.com/mincs-list/
Its Conjecture9 entry still marks the general problem open. It records the
two-block theorem and a three-2-by-2-block rank-at-most-two result, with
the remaining small case reduced to ranks3-4.

Targeted searches for Marcus three-block and block-permanent proofs did
not locate an existing all-size, all-rank complex three-block theorem.
This is bounded retrieval, not exhaustive novelty clearance. The current
research-page entry is a status report, not a substitute for reviewing
all original papers. No external mathematician has reviewed this draft.
