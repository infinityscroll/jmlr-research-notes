Root independent audit: three blocks of arbitrary size
30 October 2026. Verdict: the stated three-block theorem passes internal audit.

Scope checked
-------------
For every k>=1 and every complex Hermitian PSD matrix of order3k, partitioned
into k-by-k blocks, per(A)>=per([per(Aij)]). No rank restriction. No claim for
four or more groups and no equality characterization. The polynomial theorem
covers arbitrary degree-k triples in d<=8k^2/9 for k>=3, and d<=4 for k2.

Independent derivation
----------------------
Before receiving either completed proof, root derived the pure-entry limit,
the ordinary-cell indicator kernel for l<k, the 1,3/4,0 final coefficients,
and the rank-at-most-two final isotypic defect. The two other agents then
independently found the compensation by the preceding boundary contraction.
Root checked the compensation directly as follows.

For fixed l,p,q, the distinguished slot choices and cross bijections give
binom(k,l)^2(l!)^2(k-l)!=(k!)^2/(k-l)!. The ordinary binomials are in E_p,E_q.
In the exceptional ordinary-cell term, the remaining uncontracted k-subsets
must match. Precisely k!(k-l)! permutations implement the reordered identity
between the separately symmetric outputs. Consequently the coefficient of
||B_l||^2 is (k!)^3 binom(k,l)^2/2, with no coset-normalization divisor.

At l=k, the complete remaining symmetrizer contributes k!. With I the weighted
interior sum and B the two boundary traces, the exact expression is
(k!)^3[||I+3B/4||^2-9||B||^2/16]. The two pure (k-1)-fold contractions have
the common codomain bar(V) tensor V tensor Sym^k(V), and tracing its first
two factors gives B exactly. This trace has squared norm d. The preceding
boundary term therefore compensates the negative square when d<=8k^2/9.
For k>=3 this includes d<=2k. No ambient highest-weight decomposition is
needed for this argument, and it works also in dimensions1 and2.

At k2, the same layer formulas give Q1=112||Cs||^2+16||Ch||^2. For the traces
U=tau Cs and Z=tau Ch, U=(B+I)/3 and Z=(2B-I)/3. The restricted squared trace
norms in d4 are both2. Substitution gives the nonnegative lower bound
87||U||^2+4||Z-(5/2)U||^2. This is an analytic endpoint, independent of a finite
PSD certificate. At k1 the original matrix statement is identity.

Transfer and normalization
--------------------------
For a polynomial P with its unscaled symmetric coefficient tensor a,
||P||_Fock^2=k!||a||^2. The expectation of W on a tensor b tensor c is
exactly ||PQR||_Fock^2-per Gram_Fock(P,Q,R). The main TeX source uses this
convention; the independent notes also describe the equivalent isometric
polynomial convention, where the expectation acquires the factor(k!)^3.

The sum of the three coefficient functions is3 off the wreath subgroup and0
on it. Thus the three partial-transpose tests average to the actual permanent
defect. Partial transpose is used only against a product of whole symmetric
blocks. Root rechecked the normal-ordering identity and all-rank projection:
the first two blocks span at most2k dimensions; the discarded component of
the third block contributes a nonnegative multiple of the two-block defect.
The projected polynomial remains a product of projected linear factors.

Limits
------

the pure-entry coefficients equal the old quadratic-power coefficients for
