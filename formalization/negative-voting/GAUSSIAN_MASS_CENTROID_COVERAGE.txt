ACTUAL MEASURE AND INPUT CLASS
E is any finite-dimensional real inner product space with its Borel measurable
structure. The measure is mathlib's actual stdGaussian E. A fractional cell f
is AEMeasurable and satisfies 0 <= f <= 1 almost everywhere; no pointwise Borel
representative, assumed centroid integrability, or prescribed Gaussian score law
is required by the concrete centroid endpoints. Gaussian centroid means the
Bochner integral c(f) = integral f(x) * x d(stdGaussian E), with scalar multiplication.

CHECKED ENDPOINTS
1. gaussianCentroid_norm_le_threshold:
   mass(f) = 1 - Phi(t) implies ||c(f)|| <= phi(t).

2. gaussianCentroid_norm_le_quantile:
   mass(f) = p, 0 < p < 1 implies ||c(f)|| <= phi(Phi^{-1}(p)).
   normalQuantile is defined by Classical.choose only after proving that the
   actual Gaussian CDF attains every p in (0,1); normalQuantile_unique proves
   this is the unique inverse value. Existence, strict monotonicity, continuity,
   and Phi(-t) = 1-Phi(t) are proved from the checked CDF derivatives and limits.

3. gaussianCentroid_norm_le_profile:
   ||c(f)|| <= gaussianMassProfile(mass(f)) without a positive-mass hypothesis.
   The profile equals phi(Phi^{-1}(p)) in (0,1), and zero at both endpoints.
   A zero-mass fractional cell is zero almost everywhere; a full-mass cell is
   one almost everywhere. Their actual Gaussian centroids are zero.

4. unit_direction_remainder_bound:
   For ||w||=1 and every real t,
   <w,c(f)> <= phi(t) + t*(mass(f) - (1-Phi(t))).

5. gaussianCentroid_norm_le_mass_threshold:
   For t >= 0, ||c(f)|| <= t*mass(f) + phi(t).

6. gaussianCentroid_norm_le_logarithmic_mass_bound:
   For real m >= 1 and mass(f) <= C/m,
   ||c(f)|| <= (C*sqrt(2*log(m)) + 1/sqrt(2*pi))/m.
   The density substitution at sqrt(2*log(m)) is exact; this theorem contains
   no unspecified asymptotic constant.

PROOF CONTENT
The fractional bathtub comparison is proved pointwise, retaining the exact
mass remainder, and then integrated after establishing every integrability
claim. The upper Gaussian half-line has mass 1-Phi(t) and first moment phi(t).
The actual law of <w,X>, for unit w and X distributed as stdGaussian E, is proved
in the already-checked GaussianCapTailAndStrip module and applied here. Choosing
w in the centroid direction converts the scalar bound into the Euclidean norm
bound. No Gaussian isoperimetric inequality is postulated or imported as an axiom.

ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE
This is a formalization of a classical sharp Gaussian bound used by the research
proof, not a new mathematical priority claim. It reuses WeakSimplex.normalCDF,
normalPDF, their checked derivatives/limits, and truncated_first_moment from the
attributed WeakSimplexConjectureLean dependency. It also reuses the checked actual
projection law NegativeVotingCapTail.gaussian_inner_map. Equality rigidity for
nontrivial extremizing cells is not asserted by this module. This module alone
does not prove the negative-voting theorem.
