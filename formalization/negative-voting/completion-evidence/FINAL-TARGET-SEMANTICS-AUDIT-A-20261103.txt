QUANTIFIERS AND NONVACUITY
1. MainGoal:25-43 chooses a natural threshold M >= 3 before m, dimension,
   correlation or competitor. The bound is consequently uniform in all of them.
   For each m >= M and every n with m-1 <= n+1 it then constructs a regular
   reference before introducing rho and f. A reference depending on rho or f
   cannot satisfy the literal quantifier order used here.
2. N=n+1 covers every allowed finite dimension: m >= 3 and N >= m-1 imply
   N >= 2. No relevant zero-dimensional case is silently lost. The natural
   subtraction m-1 agrees with the intended integer subtraction on this range.
3. The regular-reference requirement is existential, not an implication from
   an impossible Gram premise. RegularSimplexExistence constructs the centered
   simplex in dimension m-1 and pads it in every larger dimension. The final
   assembly actually calls exists_regularSimplexGram_main_dimensions.
4. The correlation interval is exactly -1/(m-1) <= rho < 0. Its left endpoint
   is included, and there is no positive lower bound on r=-rho. Since m >= 3,
   this interval is contained in [-1/2,0), so the Gaussian coupling is valid.
   Replacing m by k+2 in the final assembly gives 0 < r <= 1/(k+1) exactly.
5. No equal-mass, purity, optimality, stationary-point, rank, positive-label-mass,
   or near-regular hypothesis is imposed on the target's competitor. Routing:18
   defines SimplexWeights as coordinate nonnegativity and sum one; each weight
   is therefore at most one. Constant, fractional and nonuniform rules are
   included. Componentwise AE measurability and AE simplex membership suffice.
