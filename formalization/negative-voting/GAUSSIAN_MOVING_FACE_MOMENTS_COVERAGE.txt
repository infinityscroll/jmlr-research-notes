Actual objects
FSC.sliceMass w b j is the existing Gaussian mass of all remaining halfspace
constraints under FSC.sliceLaw(w_j,b_j). The new sliceFirstMoment is the
unnormalized vector integral over the same actual constrained slice.
No covariance/moment function is substituted by an assumed proxy.

Closed analytic steps
1. At unit, NoCoincident base normals/supports, every remaining inequality
   on the explicit affine slice has locally stable truth value for almost
   every sample of one fixed ambient standard Gaussian. The exceptional
   boundary sets are null by FSC.ae_slice_boundary_ne.
2. The actual slice mass is jointly continuous in every normal and support,
   using the finite Gaussian measure and proved AE indicator convergence.
3. The actual first moment is exactly the integral of the affine slicePoint
   times the actual moving constraint mask under that fixed ambient Gaussian.
4. If ||w'_j||<=2 and |b'_j|<=A, the slice realization obeys
     ||slicePoint(w',b',j,X)|| <= 2 A + 5 ||X||.
   The neighborhood A=|b_j|+1 is derived from ordinary parameter continuity.
5. This explicit Gaussian-integrable dominator and the derived pointwise
   convergence prove joint continuity of the actual vector first moment.

The base hypotheses are unit normals and FSC.NoCoincident. Positivity of
face mass, assumed continuity, assumed domination, and assumed boundary
variation identities are not premises. Nearby parameters are permitted to
have nonunit normals; the explicit affine pushforward defines the slice law
there too, and the joint continuity conclusion is on the full parameter space.
The physical fan application composes with its unit-normal parameter map.
