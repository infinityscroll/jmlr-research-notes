GaussianAffinePositiveMass: derived pathwise positivity

A positive-mass finite Gaussian cap with unit normals has a point satisfying
every defining inequality strictly: the finite union of hyperplanes is null.
Every invertible affine pullback therefore contains a nonempty open subset,
which has positive standard Gaussian measure. Consequently the entire actual
near-identity path I+tA, tz has positive mass for |t|<=1 and ||A||op<=1/2.

Only the original cap's mass is assumed positive. No positivity-along-path,
interior-feasibility, or full-support hypothesis is supplied; those facts are
proved or discharged by the actual standard Gaussian library theorem.
This includes empty constraint sets and dimension0 whenever the hypotheses
can be satisfied.
