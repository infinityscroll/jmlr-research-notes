GaussianAffineNormControl: actual dimension-independent operator control

This module identifies the coordinate Hilbert--Schmidt norm with the sum of
squared images of the standard orthonormal basis, bounds the operator norm by
it, and proves ||M A||HS <= ||M||op ||A||HS. For ||A||op <= 1/2 and |t| <= 1,
it proves the lower bound ||(I+tA)x|| >= ||x||/2, constructs the actual
continuous linear equivalence I+tA by finite-dimensional surjectivity, and
derives the inverse operator norm bound <=2. The results include dimension0.

positive-mass and path modules apply them to actual Gaussian probabilities.
