ACTUAL MATHEMATICAL ENDPOINT

Let gamma_n be the finite product of n standard real Gaussians. For an arbitrary
completed-Gaussian-measurable f in L2(gamma_n x gamma_1), let

  U_a(x) = integral f(tau*x + sqrt(1-tau^2)*z,
                      tau*a + sqrt(1-tau^2)*y) d(gamma_n x gamma_1)(z,y).

If 0 < tau and tau^2 <= 1/31, the kernel-checked theorem
actual_product_normalTrace_l32_bound_sharp proves

  ||U_a||_32 <= sqrt(C(tau,a)) ||f||_2,

  C(tau,a) = 1/[sqrt(1-tau^2)*sqrt(1+tau^2)]
             * exp(tau^2*a^2/(1+tau^2)).

The coefficient has no dependence on n. No boundedness, continuity, Borel-only
representative, or a pre-assumed trace estimate is required. The input is exactly
MemLp f 2 under the actual product measure. The theorem also covers n=0.

PROVED STRUCTURE

1. The actual one-dimensional Gaussian density ratio
   K(tau,a,y)=density(N(tau*a,1-tau^2))/density(N(0,1)).
2. Its exact exponential formula, positivity, measurability, normalization,
   membership in L2, and the change-of-Gaussian-measure identity.
3. Its exact squared row norm: integral K^2 dgamma_1 = C(tau,a).
   The proof completes the square and identifies a Gaussian of mean
   2*tau*a/(1+tau^2) and variance (1-tau^2)/(1+tau^2).
4. Genuine L2 Cauchy-Schwarz and a Gaussian normal-section bound.
5. Actual Gaussian tangential averaging, combined with the separately compiled
   GaussianHypercontractivityBridge, to obtain the L32 bound.
6. The normal affine change of variables, full changed-measure integrability,
   almost-everywhere integrability of the product kernel, and Fubini. Thus the
   final statement concerns the single actual product integral displayed above,
   rather than an unconnected auxiliary operator.

STATUS AND PRECISE BOUNDARY

The module supplies the fixed-coordinate analytic trace input needed for the
local comparison argument. It does not prove normal derivative bounds, a normal
supremum estimate, arbitrary-normal orthogonal transport, a two-normal estimate,
polyhedral face counting, or the full negative-voting main theorem. These are
separate mathematical steps; none is encoded as a custom axiom in this module.
