ACTUAL GAUSSIAN MAXIMUM SECOND MOMENTS

GaussianMaximumSecondMoment.lean proves a weighted layer-cake identity for
arbitrary measurable real functions under finite measures, then applies it
to Z=b(M-b), where M is the actual maximum of n+2 independent standard
Gaussian coordinates and b is the actual upper 1/(n+2) quantile.

All tail regions are included. For n>0 the finite bound is

 E Z^2 <= 5 + 4 b^4 exp(-exp((99/100)b^2/50)) + 8 b^2 2^(-(n+2)).

The two remainders are proved to tend to zero, giving eventually E Z^2<=6.
The variance is the literal ProbabilityTheory.variance of this finite
Gaussian maximum, and its equality to the centered second-moment integral
is exported. The actual conclusions include eventually

 Var(M) <= 6/b^2,      Var(M) -> 0,      E M^2 <= 5 log(n+2).

There is no assumed moment limit or Gumbel approximation. The module does
not identify the regular simplex chaos coefficients, prove localization
for arbitrary fractional competitors, or complete the negative-voting
main theorem. GaussianMaximumLocalization separately strengthens the
quantile and variance bounds and assembles the finite coefficient.
