GaussianFanCycleForms defines the literal Gaussian data:
w_ab = mu_{a,b}/|b_a-b_b| for distinct a,b, and 1 on repeated edges;
D_abc = triangleContent/mu_{a,b,c} on distinct triples, and 1 otherwise;
c_abcd = mu_{a,b,c,d}/tetrahedronContent;
mean_abc(v) is the actual normalized Gaussian mean of <v,x> on that stratum.
The contents are the already-proved sqrt Gram determinants, namely 2A and6V.
Masses include the proved perpendicular Gaussian density factor.

The forms are
A(lambda,eta) = (1/2) sum_ab boundary(lambda)_ab boundary(eta)_ab / w_ab;
B(lambda,eta) = (1/2) sum_abkl lambda_kab eta_lab
                 (1/w_ab-D_kab mean_lab(v_k)).
The exact intended factorization on alternating triangles is

right side and proves the exact entrywise A-B expansion that starts the proof.
