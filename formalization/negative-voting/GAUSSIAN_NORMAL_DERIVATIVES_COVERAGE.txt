ACTUAL MATHEMATICAL ENDPOINTS

Let gamma_n be the finite product of n standard real Gaussians and let f belong
to L2(gamma_n x gamma_1), with no boundedness or Borel-representative assumption.
Set sigma=sqrt(1-tau^2), and use the actual joint Gaussian integral

  U_a(x) = integral f(tau*x + sigma*z, tau*a + sigma*y)
                    d(gamma_n x gamma_1)(z,y).

1. Exact actual normal derivatives.
   The first and second derivatives of the density ratio K(tau,a,y) are proved
   pointwise from its exponential formula. For the first derivative,

     integral (partial_a K)^2 dgamma_1 = D(tau,a),
     D(tau,a) = C(tau,a) *
       [tau^2/(1-tau^4) + tau^4*a^2/(1+tau^2)^2],
     C(tau,a) = 1/[sqrt(1-tau^2)*sqrt(1+tau^2)]
                 * exp(tau^2*a^2/(1+tau^2)).

   All row identities use tau^2<1. Differentiation under the normal integral is
   proved for arbitrary Gaussian L2 input with an explicit local polynomial
   dominating function. Differentiation through the tangential integral is also
   proved. The endpoint ae_hasDerivAt_actual_product_trace states that one
   Gaussian-full-measure set of x supports the actual derivative of U_a(x) at
   every real normal parameter a.

2. Literal uncountable normal supremum.
   If 0<tau and tau^2<=1/31 and A>=0, define

     S(x) = sup over all real a in [-A,A] of |U_a(x)|.

   actual_product_normal_sup_memLp_and_bound proves both

     S belongs to L32(gamma_n),
     ||S||_32 <= sqrt(C(tau,A)) * ||f||_2.

   This is an actual supremum theorem. Its AE measurability is derived from the
   proved normal continuity by reduction to a countable dense set. It is not
   merely a list of separate fixed-a estimates.

3. Uniform envelope for actual first derivatives.
   exists_actual_product_trace_derivative_l32_uniform_envelope proves the
   existence of a nonnegative measurable L32 envelope G with

     ||G||_32 <= sqrt(D(tau,A)) * ||f||_2,
     for gamma_n-almost every x, for EVERY a in [-A,A],
       |d U_a(x)/da| <= G(x).

   Here the derivative is Lean's actual deriv of the displayed Gaussian product
   integral. This gives a measurable envelope for the full derivative family;
   the file does not separately define a raw derivative-supremum random variable.

All constants above are independent of the tangential dimension n. A radius-one
interval centered at b is included by choosing A=|b|+1.

HOW THE COMMON AE SET IS PROVED

Fibre L2 membership first gives a parameter-independent full-measure set of
normal fibres. The actual Gaussian tangent mixture preserves Gaussian measure.
Its product pullback followed by Fubini produces one x-exceptional set before
quantification over the real parameter a. The common pointwise dominator is
sqrt(C(tau,A)) times the tangential Gaussian average of the fibre L2 norm.
That fibre norm has exactly the original L2 norm, and actual Gaussian
hypercontractivity gives its L32 bound.

Completed-measurable f is replaced by its strongly measurable representative.
A proved all-parameter congruence transfers both traces and derivative traces
back to f on one common AE set. No uncountable intersection of separate
fixed-parameter AE conclusions is used. The actual joint-integral identification
is likewise simultaneous in a, with integrability and Fubini proved.
