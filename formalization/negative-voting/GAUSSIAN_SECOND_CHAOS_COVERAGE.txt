The measure is the actual finite product of standard normal measures,
OAI.MinUncut.GaussianHermite.γpi. The coefficients and chaosEnergy are the
concrete normalized tensor-Hermite coefficients from GaussianSpectralBridge.
No orthonormal-basis premise or second-chaos representation is assumed.

For a finite label family f, set
  Q_i(j,k) = integral f_i(x) (x_j x_k - delta_jk) dγ(x).
The main theorem chaosEnergy_two proves exactly
  chaosEnergy f 2 = (1/2) sum_i sum_j sum_k Q_i(j,k)^2.
The coordinate type is finite and linearly ordered; in particular every
Fin N is allowed. No positivity, equal-mass, dimension lower bound, or
correlation-sign hypothesis is used in this tensor identity.

The proof explicitly computes H_2(x)=(x^2-1)/sqrt(2), the degree-two
diagonal basis function, and the distinct-coordinate product H_1(x_j)H_1(x_k).
Sym2 identifies unordered pairs with multiindices of total degree two.
Reindexing by sorted pairs proves the exact off-diagonal multiplicity:
diagonal terms contribute Q_i(j,j)^2/2 and each unordered off-diagonal pair
contributes Q_i(j,k)^2. Thus the complete ordered tensor sum has factor 1/2.

Integrability is proved, not included as an unexplained moment assumption.
quadraticCoordinate_memLp derives L2 membership of x_j x_k-delta_jk from
the concrete Gaussian polynomial integrability theorem.
quadratic_moment_integrable then derives integrability of its product with
each L2 coordinate f_i by Holder/Cauchy--Schwarz. The finite coefficient
identity is also algebraically valid using Lean's total Bochner integral;
the L2 and AE-simplex results establish genuine integrability for the
intended applications.

For any real finite test tensor T define the actual Gaussian integral
  P(f,T) = integral sum_i,j,k T_i(j,k) f_i(x)
                         (x_j x_k-delta_jk) dγ(x).
quadratic_test_pairing_eq identifies it with sum_i,j,k T_i(j,k)Q_i(j,k).
quadratic_test_pairing_bound proves
  P(f,T)^2 <= 2 chaosEnergy f 2 sum_i,j,k T_i(j,k)^2.
For a nonzero tensor, quadratic_test_tensor_certificate proves the lower
certificate
  P(f,T)^2 / (2 sum_i,j,k T_i(j,k)^2) <= chaosEnergy f 2.
No symmetry assumption on T is needed. Antisymmetric components simply do
not improve this certificate.

ae_simplex_quadratic_test_tensor_certificate requires only that each f_i
is AE-measurable, all f_i are nonnegative almost everywhere, and their sum
is one almost everywhere. It derives L2 membership from the resulting
0<=f_i<=1 bound, then applies the actual Gaussian certificate. It does not
require an everywhere-defined simplex representative or a separate L2
premise.
