Actual moving Gaussian caps with quartic-growth tests

For every continuous real test F with |F(x)| <= C(1+norm(x)^4), the module proves the actual joint Frechet derivative of its Gaussian polyhedral integral and the exact intrinsic-facet formula. The moving-slab domination is derived from Gaussian eighth moments and the explicit normal estimate s^4 phi(s) <= 8 phi(0). The fixed normal-fibre envelope is 73 C phi(0)(1+norm(y)^4), which is L2; the actual linear boundary velocity is also L2. No derivative, integrability, or domination result is assumed. This extends the already checked quadratic moving-cap machinery to the quartic bulk tests required by a second quadratic-test derivative.
