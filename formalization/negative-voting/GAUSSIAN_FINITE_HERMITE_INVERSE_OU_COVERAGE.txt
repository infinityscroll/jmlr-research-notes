Actual finite normalized Hermite sums: exact coefficients and squared Gaussian integral, pointwise inverse OU identity for every input point, inverse L2 cost tau^(-k), and actual fixed-normal trace at q=1+tau^(-2) with dimension-independent Gaussian row constant. Finite coefficient degree bound is explicit in this module; its derivation for affine products is in the separately built but not yet audited GaussianPolynomialHermiteDegree. No ambient almost-everywhere identity is restricted to a measure-zero plane. This is an analytic prerequisite, not the full negative-voting theorem.
