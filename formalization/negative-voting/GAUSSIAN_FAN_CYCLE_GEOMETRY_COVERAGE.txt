Actual barycentric and reciprocal-height geometry

GaussianFanCycleGeometry.lean imports only Mathlib. An actual affine basis in a finite-dimensional real inner-product space supplies its coordinate affine maps. Their negative Riesz derivatives are the concrete outward vectors; no vector-field, coordinate-gradient, height, or normal identity is assumed.

Proved: sum of these vectors is zero; their pairing with every target edge is the correct Kronecker difference; each vector annihilates every edge of the opposite face. For the actual mathlib simplex, the vector is minus its vertex-to-altitudeFoot vector divided by height squared, and its norm is exactly1/height. Mathlib defines that height using the actual orthogonal projection onto the opposite affine face.

For every subset of target vertices, the direction space is the actual vectorSpan of that face. Every excluded barycentric vector lies in its orthogonal complement. The projected target gap, its strictly positive norm, and its unit normal are constructed by the actual starProjection. Their pairing with any excluded barycentric vector is delta(k,l)/height(S,l). The projected-gap norm equals distance to the actual affine-face projection and is independent of the chosen anchor vertex. Thus reciprocal heights and all other-boundary annihilation terms are derived rather than assumed.
