Equation-level dependency map
1. Fixed affinely independent finite sites, prescribed strictly positive masses: dual offset minimizer and actual winning masses. GaussianFanMassExistence now proves this (indeed distinctness suffices for existence). Affine independence is subsequently needed for complete simplex-face geometry and square tangent bases.
2. Actual offset Jacobian and positive weighted graph Laplacian: separate GaussianFanMassJacobian work. Joint covariance/offset smoothness and the implicit function theorem still need their concrete covariance-parameter bridge.
3. Gaussian first/second boundary moments and covariance domination on each affine stratum. Current cap modules prove concrete Euclidean-polyhedron versions; the coordinate/projector and physical Hausdorff-density transfer to every winner stratum remains a separate geometric construction.
4. Barycentric vectors v_k=-grad l_k satisfy sum v_k=0 and <v_k,y_i-y_j>=delta(k,j)-delta(k,i). On facet e, these identify N_e^{-1} rows and give E_e=N_e T_e^T, B_e=N_e^{-1}E_e N_e^{-T}, and (B_e)_(g,g')=1/w_e-D_g<v_k,m_g'>.
5. Concrete simplex boundaries partial2 and partial3: partial2 partial3=0; every edge cycle has a triangle filling; ker partial2=im partial3. These follow from explicit coning identities, independently of Gaussian geometry or concavity.
6. First moments on triangle strata and target triangle/tetrahedron height identities give the exact assembled factorization A-B=-D partial3 diag(mu_h/(6V_h)) partial3^T D. This is the central still-unformalized geometric matrix equality. It uses the local Gaussian boundary identities, not Hodge or covariance concavity.
7. Finite weighted gauge: keep partial2 lambda=j while arranging partial3^T D lambda=0. The abstract finite-dimensional projection theorem is already kernel-checked in NegativeVoting.WeightedGauge; the actual chain operators and diagonal weights must be instantiated. Step6 then gives exact equality of the B-energy and the cycle norm. No covariance-deficit inverse is needed.
8. Cell-vector differences telescope on each triangle, giving j.c=sum_e z_e.T_e eta_e. With the actual least-squares offset, both this cross term and the quadratic cycle term equal CYC. Expanding r_e=z_e-N_e^{-T}eta_e gives sum r_e^T E_e r_e=sum z_e^T E_e z_e-CYC exactly.
9. The actual square-root covariance/offset Hessian is 4F''=min_b sum_e w_e E[(d_e.HX+db_e)^2]-sum_e w_e|Hd_e|^2. Its factor4 and negative interior term come from sqrt(I+tH)'=H/2 and sqrt(I+tH)''=-H^2/4. This Hessian identity still requires a concrete differentiated optimized Gaussian objective; it must not be inserted as an assumed interface and counted as completed analysis.
10. Steps3,7,8,9 already imply -4F''>=sum_e w_e(d_e^T H d_e)^2/|d_e|^2. Thus ordinary covariance concavity and normal strain for simplex targets are downstream conclusions, not inputs to the cycle calculation.
11. The now-checked actual Ehrhard covariance-mean correction applied to r_e gives the retained offset term. N_e^{-1}P_e m_e=1/w_e yields r_e.P_e m_e=-db_e-z_e.(I-P_e)m_e. With z_i=Hy_i and b=2h', this is the bracket 2h'_e-h_e(d_e^THd_e)/|d_e|^2, up to an irrelevant square sign.
12. Whitening handles positive definite bases; continuity handles singular covariance limits. For the negative-voting proof only finite simplex targets are needed. General-target lifting, P2 approximation, CFJ regularity, and infinite-dimensional Hodge can therefore be omitted from this Lean route. Their broader claims in the manuscript are separate.

Independent sign and normalization checks
Off-diagonal shared-edge term: D_g<v_k,m_g'> = D_g*mu_h*A_g'/(3V_h*mu_g') = D_g D_g' mu_h/(6V_h). The two incidence signs equal minus the corresponding tetrahedron-boundary signs. Diagonal term uses sum_{k in g}v_k=-sum_{k outside g}v_k and gives -D_g^2 sum_{h containing g}mu_h/(6V_h). Neither calculation invokes concavity.
The residual r_e as written may retain a normal component; E_e and P_e m_e annihilate it, so only P_e r_e enters the estimates. Calling r_e itself tangential without projecting is imprecise but does not change either identity.
The Ehrhard probability is the intrinsic facet probability pi_e=mu_e/phi(h_e/|d_e|), not a cell mass. The coefficient and derivative scaling must retain this distinction.
