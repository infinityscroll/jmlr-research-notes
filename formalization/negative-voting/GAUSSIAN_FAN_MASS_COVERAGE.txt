Prescribed Gaussian masses for actual finite affine fans

Endpoint: NegativeVotingFan.exists_affine_fan_with_prescribed_gaussian_masses.
For any finite nonempty label set, finite Euclidean dimension, fixed injective sites B_i, and real p_i>0 with sum p_i=1, there exist real offsets h_i summing to zero such that
  gamma({x : for every j, <B_j,x>+h_j <= <B_i,x>+h_i}) = p_i.
The statement uses actual Gaussian measures of explicit closed winning cells. Full rank is not required. Distinctness supplies null tie sets for each fixed offset vector.

Proof chain:
1. Actual finite Gaussian scores and their maximum are integrable. The expected maximum is 1-Lipschitz in the sup norm of offsets and is at least every individual offset.
2. The dual E max_i(<B_i,X>+h_i) - sum_i p_i h_i is coercive on centered offsets. A compact sublevel gives an actual minimizer.
3. Gaussian ties are null. At a unique winner the finite maximum has its literal directional derivative. A global Lipschitz bound permits differentiation under the Gaussian integral without assuming one common neighborhood in which all samples keep their winner.
4. The dual derivative in direction v is sum_i (mass_i-p_i)v_i. At a minimizer choose v_i=mass_i-p_i; it is centered. Stationarity forces the sum of squared discrepancies to be zero.
5. Tie-nullness identifies the maxWeights integrals with the Gaussian measures of the actual closed winning cells.
