1. A PRECISE SMALL-CAP LEMMA THAT THE ARGUMENT ACTUALLY PROVES

Let l_i(x)=B_i.x+h_i be a full-rank centered Gaussian affine fan. Write
ell_ij=|B_i-B_j|, n_ij=(B_i-B_j)/ell_ij, and let the ij hyperplane be
{x:n_ij.x=a_ij}. Assume
  c alpha <= ell_ij <= C alpha,   |a_ij|<=C sqrt(L),
  alpha^-1<=m^C,
and every distinct triple has tangent comparison slope at least c alpha.
These conditions follow from the stated singular-value interval and the
positive mass box. Let F_ij be the actual ij facet, lambda_ij its Gaussian
surface measure, and v_i>=0 be a structured forcing. The cap is
  C_theta = E max(0, max_{j!=i(X)} [theta(v_i+v_j)-(l_i-l_j)]).
The following sufficient bounds are what the proof uses:

(a) Each pair-plane normal supremum
  W_ij(y)=sup_{|z|<=1} (v_i(y+zn)+v_j(y+zn)
                         +Lip_n(v_i+v_j) near y+zn)
has L32 norm at most m^C under the ENTIRE plane's intrinsic standard
Gaussian law. A bound on a.e. derivatives of the smooth factors suffices
for this Lipschitz bound; v_i itself can contain absolute values.

(b) Each v_i has full-space L32 norm at most m^C.

(c) The weighted facet energy obeys
  sum_{i<j} ell_ij^-1 int_{F_ij}(v_i^2+v_j^2) d lambda_ij <= C.
The constant in (c), unlike the polynomial constants allowed in (a,b),
must be independent of m.

Then for fixed B0,K and all 0<theta<=m^-B0,
  C_theta <= C theta^2
       + C m^K [theta^(21/8)+theta^(39/8)+theta^32].          (A)
Consequently a fixed B>max(B0,8K/5), with a larger alphabet threshold,
gives C_theta<=C theta^2 whenever 0<theta<=m^-B. None of these exponents
or constants depend on theta, r, or ambient dimension.

Derivation of (A): upper-bound the maximum by ordered-pair positive parts.
For one ordered pair, disintegrate x=y+zn, y in its comparison plane,
z>=0 on the old winning side, and use density phi(a+z) dgamma_plane(y) dz.
Take M=theta^-1/8. At a good y with W_ij(y)<=M, positive reward for z<=1
forces 0<=z<=eta=theta M/ell_ij. For theta<=m^-B0 this is at most
1/[2(1+|a|)]. Put u(z)=v_i(y+zn)+v_j(y+zn). The positive-part map is
1-Lipschitz, hence
  |(theta u(z)-ell z)_+-(theta u(0)-ell z)_+|<=theta M z.
Its integral on [0,eta] is at most theta M eta^2/2. The normal density
ratio obeys
  |phi(a+z)/phi(a)-1|<=C(1+|a|)eta
on that interval. Since the fiber reward is at most theta M and the
fiber length is eta, the density error is bounded by
C phi(a)(1+|a|)theta M eta^2. Both errors are bounded by
C m^C theta^3 M^3. The exact constant-density full fiber integral is
  phi(a) theta^2 u(0)^2/(2ell).

If y is on the actual facet, discarding the old-cell fiber restriction
only enlarges the upper bound. If y is outside the facet but y+zn belongs
to old cell i for some z<=eta, then for some k!=i,j
  -C alpha eta <= l_i(y)-l_k(y) < 0.
The tangent part of B_i-B_k has length >=c alpha, so this set is an
intrinsic Gaussian strip of physical width <=C eta. Its probability is
at most C eta, independent of its center. At most m-2 strips occur.
Multiplying by the good-fiber bound C phi(a) theta M eta gives another
C m^C theta^3 M^3 term. This is an UNCONDITIONAL entire-plane strip
probability. Dividing by the probability of the actual facet here would
be unnecessary and potentially destroy the estimate.

For bad y, discard the switching cost and use
  E[W_ij 1_{W_ij>M}] <= M^-31 E W_ij^32.
The unit normal interval costs at most a polynomial factor in m since
|a|<=C sqrt(L). Thus the bad part is <=C m^C theta M^-31.
For z>1 a positive reward requires theta(v_i+v_j)>=c alpha. Full-space
L32 then bounds this portion by C m^C theta^32. Sum the ordered pairs;
their m(m-1) count is absorbed only in the remainder polynomial.

On the actual facets, the two ordered sides together have main term
  theta^2 sum_{i<j} ell_ij^-1 int (v_i+v_j)^2 d lambda_ij
  <=2 theta^2 sum_{i<j} ell_ij^-1 int(v_i^2+v_j^2) d lambda_ij.
This uses (c); no extraneous factor m is introduced in the main term.
Because M^3=theta^-3/8 and M^-31=theta^31/8, the displayed exponents
in (A) follow. Since 39/8 and 32 exceed 21/8 and theta<=1, they can be
absorbed into the larger theta^(21/8) remainder used in the manuscript.

2. THE THIRD-LABEL ALTITUDE IS QUANTITATIVELY VALID

For e=e_i-e_j and f=e_i-e_k in H=1-perp, the tangent slope equals
  inf_t |B^T(f-t e)| >= sigma_min(B|H) inf_t|f-t e|.
For three distinct labels, |e|^2=|f|^2=2 and e.f=1, so the last infimum
is sqrt(3/2). Hence it is >=c alpha uniformly in m and N. Also
|(B_i-B_k).n_ij|<=|B_i-B_k|<=C alpha. These are exactly the lower
slope and upper normal-drift bounds used above; no simplex angle is
asserted for an arbitrary rank-deficient fan. The full-rank hypothesis is
essential and follows here from d(B)<=A/m and alpha asymptotic to sqrt(L)/m.

The mass-calibrated affine fan itself is well-defined in this regime.
For fixed full-rank B, G(h)=E max_i(B_i.X+h_i)-p.h on H is coercive:
max h-p.h>=p_min(range h)>=p_min|h|/sqrt(m), up to the fixed finite
Gaussian score expectation. It therefore has a minimum. The nondegenerate
Gaussian score law on H makes every pair facet have positive surface mass,
so the Hessian is the strictly positive weighted graph Laplacian on H.
Gaussian convolution makes G smooth, and its critical-point equation is
exactly the prescribed-mass equation. Thus its minimizer is unique modulo
common offsets, and the implicit-function theorem gives the smooth offset
map on the full-rank/interior-mass domain. Uniform derivative bounds still
require the quantitative graph-gap estimates used elsewhere.

3. WHY THE STRUCTURED FORCING HAS THE NECESSARY NORMAL-SUP BOUNDS

Use the manuscript's notation
  D=d+|p-U|,   u=T_sqrt(r) Pi_(odd>=3)(f-f0),
  qB-q0=T_t vD,     |vD|_2<=C r^2 L^(5/2)D,
  T_sqrt(r)u=T_t vu, |vu|_2<=C r^(3/2)L^(3/2)|u|_2,
  t=c/sqrt(L),
  theta=C2 L^4(rD+sqrt(r)|u|_2),
  psi_i=kappa^-1 [e_r |delta l_i|+|qB_i-q0_i-T_sqrt(r)u_i|/r].
Take v_i=psi_i/theta. The dangerous normalization factors cancel:
  |vD|_2/(r theta) <= C L^-3/2,
  |vu|_2/(r theta) <= C L^-5/2,
  rD/theta <= C L^-4.                                    (B)
These are valid by dropping one nonnegative term in theta's denominator.
When the dropped amplitude is zero its numerator is zero. No lower bound
on r, D, |u|, or a single label's amplitude is required. If theta=0,
all forcing terms vanish and the cap is zero directly.

For the smoothed terms, split the OU kernel into one normal coordinate
and the tangential Gaussian operator. At |a|<=C sqrt(L), |z|<=1,
the one-dimensional kernel row and its first two normal derivatives have
L2 norm <=m^C. Tangential hypercontractivity gives the same polynomial
L32 bound on the entire plane from the normalized L2 inputs in (B).
The fundamental theorem of calculus bounds the normal supremum of a
function and its first derivative by its values at one point plus the
integrals of first and second derivatives. Minkowski then gives its L32
bound from the pointwise-in-normal-coordinate bounds. One never evaluates
all N Gaussian coordinates at a point.

For the elementary factor, write e_r=r(e_r/r), use |h|<=C L D,
|delta B|<=D, and (B). Its normalized affine coefficients are bounded
by a fixed power of L. The smooth range W has bounded first two normal
derivatives, and its normal-plane moments are polynomial in m. Absolute
values do not supply classical second derivatives at zero, but this is
not needed: |F| is locally Lipschitz with |d|F|/dz|<=|F'| a.e.; the
normal Lipschitz supremum is bounded using the smooth F',F'' instead.

The radial compensation depends only on the rank-(m-1) projection P0.
For a standard Gaussian restricted to a convex facet, Brascamp-Lieb
under linear tilts gives
  E exp(t.Y)<=exp(t.EY+|t|^2/2).
Integrating this inequality against a finite-dimensional auxiliary
Gaussian proves
  E exp(b|P0Y|^2) <= (1-2b)^(-rank(P0)/2)
       exp(b|P0EY|^2/(1-2b)).
This includes entire planes and their bounded normal shifts directly.
The needed |EY|^2<=C L follows from the facet's polynomial probability
and offset bounds. With eta=O(r^2 L), q=C1 L, b=q eta, one has
b rank(P0)=O(L^2/m). The radial factor and its first two derivatives
therefore have the required fixed moments; no N-dependent determinant
is present. Fixed Holder multiples needed for L32 products are permitted
by enlarging the alphabet threshold once.

The constant weighted facet energy in (c) is stronger than those entire-
plane polynomial estimates. For the polynomial term it follows from
conditional linear moments, |h|<=C L D and bounded weighted degrees:
  sum_e w_e E[psi_i^2+psi_j^2] <=
    C [r^2 L^5 D^2+r L^3 |u|^2].
For smoothed terms, the conditional OU restriction maps each label's L2
input to a bounded conditional L8 norm; sum the label norms with the
bounded graph degrees. Dividing by theta^2 bounds this energy uniformly.
It would be incorrect to replace that graph-weighted sum by m unweighted
label bounds. The source uses the weighted sum correctly.

4. THE MODERATE-AMPLITUDE JOIN AND A NECESSARY WARNING

A source-exit/target-entry decomposition uses one-label maximum margins,
not a sum of pair margins. A one-label offset change a is implemented by
the translation B^+(aPe_i). For |a|<=c alpha/sqrt(L), its length is
O(1/sqrt(L)); Cameron-Martin with exponent 1+log m preserves the O(1/m)
upper cell-mass bound. M=Laplacian*B and the pseudoinverse bound imply
bounded weighted degrees along the shifted interval. Therefore each
one-label margin has sublevel measure <=C a up to a~1/m.

The bathtub calculation then gives, with q=C1 L fixed,
  Ccap <= C[m^(1/(q+1))theta^(2q/(q+1))
                +(c/m)(C theta m/c)^q].
For m^-B<=theta<=m^-5/4 the first term divided by theta^2 is bounded
by exp((1+2B)/C1), a constant; the second tends to zero uniformly for
large m. B was chosen once from the polynomial remainder in (A).
This constant can be large, but the final theorem only needs one finite
constant and a sufficiently large m threshold. There is no need to let
q grow with log(1/theta), which would invalidate the radial estimates.

A plain Lq hypothesis genuinely does NOT imply a uniform quadratic cap
at fixed m. Near a smooth facet, take a reward of height theta delta^-1/q
on a normal strip of width delta, with delta proportional to
(theta/ell)^(q/(q+1)). Its normalized Lq norm is constant, whereas its
cap scales as theta^(2q/(q+1)), whose ratio to theta^2 diverges. This can
be placed on a positive-probability patch away from triple junctions.
Such a forcing fails the uniform structured normal-sup assumptions.
This is a counterexample to an overly broad replacement lemma, not to
the source's structured theorem. It explains why both regimes are needed.

5. EXACT ACTUAL-TO-AFFINE IDENTITY: NO PURITY OR STATIONARITY

Let f have actual centroid B, let f0 be the affine winner with score B
and f's masses p, and let M be f0's centroid. Then v=f-f0 has zero mass
and centroid B-M. Since the old affine allocation maximizes the score
among assignments with masses p,
  Lroute=F_p(B)-|B|^2=<B,M-B>>=0.
This also equals E sum_j f_j(l_i-l_j) on old winning cell i; the offset
term vanishes after integration because the masses agree.

Write qB=T_-r Pi_(>=2)f0 and u=T_sqrt(r)Pi_(odd>=3)v. Orthogonality gives
  S(f)-S(f0)=r[2Lroute+|M-B|^2]+2<v,qB>
              +|T_sqrt(r)Pi_(even>=2)v|^2-|u|^2.
Self-adjointness and the semigroup identity give
  <v,T_sqrt(r)u>=|u|^2.
Thus, putting a=qB-q0-T_sqrt(r)u and dropping only the nonnegative even
square, the actual inequality is
  S(f)-S(f0)>=r|M-B|^2+|u|^2+2[rLroute+<v,q0+a>].           (C)
The centroid square is positive; losing it would break the final
cancellation. Its sign and coefficient in the manuscript are correct.

For any pair, including where neither label is the regular winner,
[R]'s two-label swap gives
  (T_-r g)_j-(T_-r g)_i=r k_ij c_ref,  k_ij>0.
Swap labels for the other sign and take the continuous quotient at a tie.
Its lower bound is k_ij>=kappa=exp(-1-eta|P0x|^2)<=1.
Because q0 removes the constant and first chaos,
  r c_ij+q0_j-q0_i=r k_ij c_ij+r(1-k_ij)(delta l_i-delta l_j).
The stated forcing controls the error by r kappa(psi_i+psi_j).
For j=i the exact bracket is zero. For j!=i its negative part is bounded
by r times the cap. Taking any convex mixture f_j therefore gives
  rLroute+<v,q0+a>>=-r Ccap.
This step uses exactly nonnegativity and sum_j f_j=1. It does not require
indicator-valued f, smooth f, a new winning selector, or an Euler equation.

Substitute the cap bound into (C) and use
  r theta^2 <= C L^8[r^3 D^2+r^2|u|^2].
The remaining odd-square coefficient is >=1-Cr^2L^8. This is nonnegative
uniformly on r<=1/(m-1) once m is large. It follows that
  S(f)-S(f0)>=r|M-B|^2-Cr^3L^8(d^2+|p-U|^2),               (D)
with no hidden r-dependent constant or amplitude floor.

6. CHECK OF THE SIGNED AFFINE HIGHER-TAIL POWERS

The source's OU restriction lemma is dimension-free for a substantive
reason: the current facet normal and the regular quotient normal span
at most two directions. Separate only that plane; smooth the remaining
Gaussian directions by hypercontractivity. The row factor costs
exp(Ct^2 a^2), which is bounded for t=c/sqrt(L). Restriction to a facet
of probability >=m^-C at exponent Q=C1 L costs only m^(C/Q).
For derivatives, the exact identity
  grad^j T_t=(2t)^j T_(2t) grad^j T_(1/2)
and the finite supremum of (k)_j 4^-k yield dimension-free L2 tensor bounds.

For the regular tail q_i^0=T_-r Pi_(>=3)g_i, factor
  q_i^0=T_t[T_(-r/t^2) Pi_(>=3) T_t g_i].
Hypercontractivity gives |T_t g_i|_2<=m^(-1/(1+t^2))<=C/m, so the
bracket norm is <=C r^3 L^3/m. Its pair difference is odd in the regular
pair normal. Integrating that derivative proves a quotient P_e with
  ||P_e||_{Lq(nu_e)}<=C r^3 L^(5/2)/m,
  ||grad P_e||_{Lq(nu_e)}<=C r^3 L^2/m.
These retain the important 1/m rare-label factor.

The actual potential change is T_t v with ||v||<=C r^3 L^(7/2)d,
from the dual affine trace and velocity energy <=C L||Z||^2. The adjoint
Laplacian equation then gives ||c||<=C r^3 L^4 d. Its inverse uses the
gap c/L through energy Cauchy, costing sqrt(L), not an extra full L.
For the coherent field V=-B^+(ZX+k), put K=||DV||_F+|V(0)|;
K<=C L||Z||/alpha. Conditional estimates are
  ||V||_{Lq}<=C sqrt(L)K,   ||div_gamma V||_{Lq}<=C L K.
The Gaussian trace tr(DV) cancels the mean of X.DV X, so these bounds
depend on the Frobenius norm rather than ambient dimension.

The largest current-minus-reference transport remainder is therefore
  C L^(3/2) K (r^3 L^4 d)||Z||
    <=C r^3 L^(13/2)(d/alpha)||Z||^2.
The regular quotient's leading term is <=C r^3 L^3||Z||^2; its other
terms are <=C r^3 L^(11/2)(d/alpha)||Z||^2. Since d/alpha<=C_A/sqrt(L),
all fit inside the source's deliberately loose r^3 L^10 Hessian bound.
The boundary quadratic term is <=C r^3 L^4||Z||^2 by the trace estimate.
This checks the m and r powers in [H]; no lost sqrt(m) is needed.

The coherent second-variation identity is also consistent: differentiating
1/ell cancels the surface-divergence correction because
  n.DV.n=-ell'/ell.
The same affine field transports every interface and intersection. One
can equivalently differentiate fixed reference cells under its flow,
which avoids any independent, uncanceled junction boundary term.
The balancing offset acceleration is killed by the adjoint condition,
not estimated or discarded. For the signed tail, the finite-chaos-cutoff
bounds are uniform and the discarded geometric multipliers are <1;
this is the required convergence mechanism for the derivative passage.

7. WHAT REMAINS A REAL PROOF OBLIGATION

The derivations above identify concrete formalization tasks, rather than
adding an assumed local-comparison conclusion to an interface:
- the genuine Gaussian OU trace/restriction operators and their derivative
  bounds, including Hilbert-valued hypercontractivity;
- affine fan differentiation and the full-rank prescribed-mass offset map;
- the weighted graph-degree and gap bounds, polynomial facet-probability
  construction, and conditional moments;
- the elementary normal-fiber cap lemma (A), including Gaussian disintegration,
  normal suprema, third-label strips, and the fixed-exponent regime join;
- the exact signed spectral/centroid identity (C) and actual divided regular
  reference potential comparison;
- the independent covariance gap, unequal-row second-chaos comparison and
  deficit-relative offset certificate needed to turn (D) into the final
  local theorem.
The last covariance/second-chaos group is not established by this cap audit.
The signed-tail power check is not a replacement for those estimates.

A source wording issue has already been repaired in the current integrated
reconstruction: to get a polynomial facet-probability witness, make only
two SCORE coordinates large and choose the remaining score coordinates equal
in the centered hyperplane. Prescribing all other UTILITIES equal can charge
the full offset-vector norm unnecessarily. The current explicit two-coordinate
construction avoids that problem. It is the construction that should be
formalized, not the ambiguous older shorthand.

No blanket theorem PASS is issued. Within the checked all-amplitude cap and
actual-to-affine chain, I found no missing small-r, tiny-amplitude, fractional-
assignment, or ambient-dimension factor. The remaining work is to encode these
actual analytic arguments and the separately named covariance inputs in Lean.
