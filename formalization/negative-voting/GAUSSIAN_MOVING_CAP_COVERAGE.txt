ACTUAL JOINT GAUSSIAN POLYHEDRON DIFFERENTIATION

The source modules are GaussianFanScoreHessian.lean, GaussianMovingSlab.lean,
and GaussianMovingCap.lean. They build on the attributed FSC Gaussian slicing
and support library, the checked Gaussian facet transport, and the finite
cap telescoping identity in GaussianCapTelescoping.lean.

Checked mathematical endpoints:

1. NegativeVotingFanScore.hasFDerivAt_expectedMax_joint proves the full Frechet
   derivative of E max_i(<B_i,X>+h_i), jointly in every score row and offset,
   whenever the base rows are distinct. The derivative is an actual Gaussian
   integral. jointDerivative_apply evaluates it as
      sum_i (<D_i, centroid_i> + mass_i*v_i).
   Gaussian tie nullness and the integrable Lipschitz dominator ||X||+1 are
   derived. Centroids are the actual unnormalized Gaussian integrals.

2. NegativeVotingMovingSlab.hasFDerivAt_integral_moving_slab is a proved
   differentiation-under-the-integral lemma for genuinely moving normal
   intervals. Local freezing of the other inequalities, endpoint Frechet
   derivatives, and the integrable displacement bound are its explicit
   hypotheses. These hypotheses are all discharged in the concrete Gaussian
   polyhedron result below; they are not assumed boundary-derivative formulas.

3. NegativeVotingMovingCap.hasFDerivAt_capTest proves the full Frechet
   derivative of
      (W,b) |-> integral_{<W_j,x> <= b_j for all j} F(x) d gamma_d(x)
   in every normal-vector and support parameter. Base normals have norm one,
   and FSC.NoCoincident excludes coincident boundary hyperplanes. The test F
   is continuous with |F(x)| <= C*(1+||x||), C>=0. This includes the constant
   mass test, every centroid coordinate, and every affine score test. The
   finite constraint type is arbitrary and may be empty. There is no positive
   cell-mass assumption. Updated normals are not required to remain unit.

4. NegativeVotingMovingCap.capJointDerivative_apply evaluates that derived
   derivative on an increment (deltaW,deltaB) as
      sum_j phi(b_j) * integral_{actual tangent face j}
        F(y+b_j W_j) * (deltaB_j-<deltaW_j,y+b_j W_j>) d gamma_tangent(y).
   The face, density, tangent Gaussian, and test integral are concrete.
   Derivative-integrand integrability is proved with Gaussian second moments.

Proof chain for (3)--(4): exact finite indicator telescoping; actual Gaussian
normal/tangent disintegration; rational normal threshold; its Frechet
quotient derivative; a neighborhood uniform over all tangent fibers; null
intersections with the other boundaries; local freezing on shrinking normal
intervals; FTC; and dominated Frechet differentiation. The two Gaussian bounds
phi(s)<=phi(0) and |s|*phi(s)<=phi(0) are proved directly from the density.
The polynomial dominator requires only the Gaussian second moment.

Scope:
These modules establish actual joint first derivatives of expected maxima
and of weighted polyhedral Gaussian integrals. The latter is the moving-face
input for the second derivative of a Gaussian fan expected maximum. The
paired square-facet Hessian, covariance-coordinate transformation, and the
full negative-voting inequality are not asserted by these modules.
